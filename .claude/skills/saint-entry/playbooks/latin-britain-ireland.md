For saints of England, Wales, Scotland, Ireland, and Cornwall. Checked 2026-09-29. Many saints before 1054 are shared: the Orthodox honor them too. Read "Orthodox sources" for those.

## Catholic sources, by era

### Early Church and the Celtic saints (before 750)

- **Butler.** Patrick: vol. 3, PDF p. 182 onward (17 March). Cuthbert: vol. 3, PDF p. 226 onward (20 March). Brigid ("St. Bridget, Virgin and Abbess"): vol. 2, PDF p. 24 (1 Feb). Checked.
- **Baring-Gould, *Lives of the Saints*.** Patrick: vol. 3, PDF p. 333. Cuthbert: vol. 3, PDF p. 407. Brigid: vol. 2, PDF p. 36. Checked by heading search. Baring-Gould gives many Celtic saints, with folklore and sources.
- **Baring-Gould and Fisher, *The Lives of the British Saints* (4 vols., 1907–13)** — Welsh, Cornish, and Breton saints, and Irish saints in Britain. archive.org ids: `livesofbritishsa02bariuoft`, `livesofbritishsa03bariuoft`, `livesbritishsai04fishgoog`, `livesbritishsai05fishgoog`. Volume 2 full text works (212,000 words; St. David appears). Checked.
- **O'Hanlon, *Lives of the Irish Saints* (1875–)** — nine volumes reach September only. Ids: `livesofirishsain01ohan` to `livesofirishsain09ohan` (some as `…uoft`). Vol. 3 is March and holds Patrick's Life (17 March). Full text works (755,000 words; checked). Brigid is in vol. 2 (February): not opened.
- **CELT Irish Lives.** Whole-text URL: `https://celt.ucc.ie/document/<ID>/`. Chapter text: `https://celt.ucc.ie/published/<ID>/text001.html`. Checked IDs:
  - `T201002` *Bethu Brigte* (Life of Brigit), works (7,700 words).
  - `T201010` Stokes, Life of Brigit; `T201009` Stokes, Life of Patrick (text001 works, 9,100 words).
  - `T201045` Muirchú's Life of Patrick; `T201040` Adomnán, Life of Columba (Reeves).
  - `T201000G` *Bethada Náem nÉrenn* (Plummer, Lives of Irish Saints); `T201054` and others for Columbanus.
  - The list of all texts: `https://celt.ucc.ie/publishd.html`. The Confession of Patrick was not found (T201044 is 404). Unchecked: other host for it.
- **Welsh Lives.** Wade-Evans, *Vitae Sanctorum Britanniae et Genealogiae*, is on archive.org (`bwb_T5-CQR-253`, lending copy, not opened). Rhygyfarch's Life of David and the other Welsh Lives are in Baring-Gould and Fisher. The Dictionary of Welsh Biography (`https://biography.wales/`) loads (checked home page).
- **Bede.** *Ecclesiastical History* (1903 translation): `ecclesiasticalhix00bede`, full text works (119,000 words). It has Cuthbert (27 mentions) and Hilda, Aidan, Chad. Bede's prose *Life of Cuthbert*: Colgrave's *Two Lives of Saint Cuthbert* (`twolivesofsaintc0000colg`) returned an error. A free text was not found. Unchecked.

### Medieval (750–1500)

- Butler and Baring-Gould for the calendar Lives.
- Baring-Gould and Fisher for late Welsh Lives.
- **Challoner, *Britannia Sancta* (1745)**: two volumes on archive.org, for example `bim_eighteenth-century_britannia-sancta-or-th_challoner-richard_1745_1`. Not opened.
- Dictionary of Irish Biography (`https://www.dib.ie/`) returned a JavaScript check page, so it needs a browser. Not usable by `curl`.
- Oxford DNB is paywalled. Unchecked.

### Early modern (1500–1900): the English Martyrs and Thomas More

- **Thomas More is not in the local Butler or Baring-Gould.** They were written before his canonization (1935). Do not spend time there.
- **Roper, *Life of Sir Thomas More*.** archive.org `lifeofsirthomas00rope` (1822 edition), full text works (75,000 words; checked). Other early Lives: Harpsfield and Stapleton (unchecked).
- **Bede Camm, *Lives of the English Martyrs* (1904, 1914).** archive.org `livesofenglishma00camm`, full text works (382,000 words). The contents list "B. Thomas More, Lord Chancellor". Checked.
- **Challoner, *Memoirs of Missionary Priests*.** `memoirsofmission00chal` (1924 edition), full text works (306,000 words; checked).
- **Catholic Record Society.** `https://www.crs.org.uk/` loads (checked). Its *Records Series* volumes are on archive.org (search "Catholic Record Society records"), but the copy I tried (`catholicrecordso0009unse`) returned 401 (lending). Use CRS for recusant records, and check for open volumes.
- **Yale *Complete Works of St. Thomas More*, and More's letters:** under copyright. Unchecked.

### Modern: Newman and after 1900

- **Newman Reader (newmanreader.org).** Works, letters, and biography pages: `https://www.newmanreader.org/works/apologia/` (Apologia), `https://www.newmanreader.org/biography/` (chronology and biographical works). Both checked. It is the best source for Newman's own words.
- **Wilfrid Ward, *Life of John Henry Cardinal Newman* (1912).** archive.org `lifeofjohnhnewm01warduoft`, full text works (283,000 words). Checked.
- **Canonization (2019):** Dicastery pages (`https://www.causesanti.va/it/celebrazioni.html`, loads) and Vatican homilies. Search on vatican.va with a browser header.
- **Newman-era Anglican and Catholic sources** (Tracts for the Times, Oratory records): Newman Reader has many. Others unchecked.

## Orthodox sources

The Orthodox honor many saints of the British Isles from before 1054, such as Patrick, Brigid, Columba, Aidan, Cuthbert, Bede, and Edmund. Thomas More and Newman are Catholic only unless a source shows otherwise.

- **OCA Lives of the Saints:** `https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/YYYY/MM/DD`. Checked. 17 March gives Patrick, and 20 March gives Cuthbert of Lindisfarne. It is the best short Orthodox source with a fixed URL.
- **OrthodoxWiki:** `https://orthodoxwiki.org/Patrick_of_Ireland` and `.../Cuthbert_of_Lindisfarne`. Both checked (short pages, with lead sources).
- **ROCOR Europe, "An Initial Listing of Saints of the British Isles":** `https://orthodox-europe.org/content/an-initial-listing-of-saints-of-the-british-isles/`. Checked. About 2,300 words. Lists Patrick, Mel, Cuthbert, and many more, with feast dates. It is the best list to see which local saints the Orthodox honor.
- **Orthodox England (St John's Orthodox Church, Colchester; Fr Andrew Phillips):** use `http://www.orthodoxengland.org.uk/oe4/`. Checked. The `https://` form did not connect. The old front page only forwards to `/oe4/`. Pages on the Old English saints and service texts sit under this site. Deeper pages unchecked.
- **Orthodox Christianity and the Old English Church:** `https://orthochristian.com/42688.html` (found by search, not opened).
- Orthodox feast dates use the Julian calendar in some jurisdictions. Record both dates.

## Access

- **archive.org.** Search: `https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=<query>&fl[]=identifier&fl[]=title&fl[]=year&rows=10&output=json`. Text: `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`. Ids that end `0000…` are usually lending copies and return 401 or 500. Old scans (before 1929) work.
- **CELT.** The new site `research.ucc.ie/celt/document/<ID>` forwards to `celt.ucc.ie/document/<ID>/`. Use the whole-text form for search.
- **Local libraries.** Butler months: vol. 1 January to vol. 12 December (vol. 12 also holds an index; it hit the March list). Baring-Gould: vol. 1 to 12 by month, vols. 13 to 16 unchecked. `find.py` gives the PDF page. OCR errors are common: Butler's OCR is poor, so search several spellings and open the page image.
- **New Advent** pages need the exact article number. Guessed URLs give 404.
- **Newman Reader** and **CELT** load with plain `curl`.
- The Irish Dictionary of Biography needs a browser.

## Language

- Irish and Welsh saints have many forms: Patrick (Patricius, Pádraig), Brigid (Brigit, Bridget, Bride, Brighid), Columba (Colum Cille), David (Dewi). Butler prefers "Bridget" and "Brigit". Search all.
- Old English: Cuthbert (Cuthberht), Bede (Beda), Aidan (Áedán). Latin: Cuthbertus, Beda.
- Irish Lives are in Old and Middle Irish or in Latin. CELT gives English beside most.
- Dates: Butler uses the Roman calendar order. Orthodox pages use the Julian calendar in part.
