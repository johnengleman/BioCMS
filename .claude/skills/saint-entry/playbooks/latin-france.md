## Catholic sources, by era

### Early Church and Merovingian Gaul (before 750)

- **Sulpicius Severus, *Vita Sancti Martini* (Latin).** `https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/sulpiciusseverusmartin.html`. Full text, about 6,900 words. Checked. English (NPNF 2nd series, vol. 11): `https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf211.html`, checked (index page).
- **Gregory of Tours** (*Histories*, *Glory of the Confessors*, *Miracles of St. Martin*): the main source for Gallic saints of the 5th and 6th centuries. Latin in MGH and Migne. Location of a free text not checked.
- **Guérin.** Martin: vol. 13, PDF p. 320 ("SAINT MARTIN, ÉVÊQUE DE TOURS"). Checked.
- **Catholic Encyclopedia (New Advent, Wikisource).** `https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Catholic_Encyclopedia_(1913)/St._Martin_of_Tours` works. New Advent URLs are `cathen/<number><letter>.htm`. Guess-numbers fail with 404, so search the site first.
- **Acta Sanctorum and BHL.** See "Belgium and the Netherlands".

### Medieval (750–1500)

- **Guérin** for the calendar Lives. **Nominis** for the short form.
- **Regional Lives and diocesan legendaries** on Gallica. Find the `ark:` link through the BnF catalogue.
- **Quicherat, *Procès de condamnation et de réhabilitation de Jeanne d'Arc* (1841–49)** — the trial record, in five volumes, public domain. On archive.org: `procsdecondam01joanuoft` to `procsdecondamn05joanuoft`. Full text works: `https://archive.org/download/procsdecondam01joanuoft/procsdecondam01joanuoft_djvu.txt` (140,000 words; checked). ## Access

- **Gallica blocks bots.** `https://gallica.bnf.fr/…` pages, the SRU search, and `….texteBrut` return a "Vérification de sécurité" page. Do not use them for reading. One exception: `https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/<id>/manifest.json` returned real JSON metadata (checked with `bpt6k307386`). It gives no text. If a Gallica book is essential, add it to the batch download list for the user.
- **How to find an `ark:` link (data.bnf.fr SPARQL).** Works with `curl`, no login. Example: find Gallica copies of a title.

  ```bash
  curl -sL -G "https://data.bnf.fr/sparql" \
    --data-urlencode 'query=PREFIX dcterms: <http://purl.org/dc/terms/>
  PREFIX rdarelationships: <http://rdvocab.info/RDARelationshipsWEMI/>
  SELECT ?m ?t ?d ?g WHERE { ?m dcterms:title ?t . ?t bif:contains "\"petits bollandistes\"" .
  ?m rdarelationships:electronicReproduction ?g . OPTIONAL{?m dcterms:date ?d} } LIMIT 20' \
    --data-urlencode "format=application/json"
  ```

  The `?g` value is the Gallica link, for example `https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k307386`. That is a Petits Bollandistes volume of 1876. Change the title words for another book. For a person, use `foaf:name` with `bif:contains` (checked: it finds "Jean-Marie Vianney").
- **BnF catalogue.** `https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?motRecherche=<words>&critereRecherche=0&depart=0&facetteModifiee=ok` returns a page with `ark:/12148/cb…` record numbers. A record page (`https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb37268348p`) and its data.bnf.fr twin list the Gallica ark links. Both checked.
- **archive.org text.** Search: `https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=<query>&fl[]=identifier&fl[]=title&fl[]=year&rows=10&output=json`. Full text: `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`. Works for public-domain books (checked). Items with an id ending `0000…` are often lending copies and return 401.
- **Nominis URL patterns.** Saint page: `https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/saint/<ID>/<Name>.html`. Checked IDs: 13 (Martin de Tours), 1137 (Jeanne d'Arc), 1927 (Vincent de Paul), 1621 (Jean-Marie Vianney), 662 (Bernadette Soubirous). A guessed ID with no name returns 404. Search is a page script, not a URL. Use the day page instead: `https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/fetes/<D>/<M>/<YYYY>/<D>-<Mois>-<YYYY>.html` (for example `11/11/2026/11-Novembre-2026.html`), then read the saint links.
- **Local Guérin layout.** The 17 volumes follow the calendar, about two volumes for each stretch of months. Vol. 15 also holds later additions (blessed and venerable people, including Joan and Vianney). Vol. 16 is a life of Christ and vol. 17 holds the index (*Table hagiographique*). Use `find.py`, not the volume guess. OCR is rough. Read the page image with the PDF page number when the text is unclear.
- **Lisieux Carmel warning.** `archives.carmeldelisieux.fr` is the real archive. The old domain `archives-carmel-lisieux.fr` is now a casino site. It returned a Cloudflare block to `curl`. Never cite it.
- **Domains change owners.** Confirm that a page's content matches the institution, as for `bollandistes.be`.

## Language

- Search the French name and the Latin name: Martinus, Ioanna Darc or Jeanne d'Arc or Jehanne, Vincentius a Paulo, Bernadette (Bernarde) Soubirous, Ioannes Maria Vianney.
- French spelling differs by era. Old texts use *ſ*, *oi* for *ai*, and *Jehan* for *Jean*. Search both forms. Guérin's OCR often misreads "Jeanne" and "Martin".
- Titles: saint, sainte, bienheureux, vénérable, curé d'Ars. A saint honored before canonization was often "le vénérable" in a 19th-century book.
- Dates: French sources use the civil calendar. Orthodox sources give Julian and civil dates. Record both when the saint is shared.

## Rights

- Guérin (1870s), Quicherat (1841–49), Sulpicius Severus, Gregory of Tours, and the *Acta Sanctorum* are public domain. Guérin's OCR PDFs came from library scans. Quote from them freely, but write our own text.
- Nominis, sanctuary sites, OCA, OrthodoxWiki, and Vatican pages are under copyright: facts only, in our own words.
- Modern editions and translations of old texts (for example a recent Sulpicius or Gregory) carry copyright in the translation.
