# Check: Thérèse of Lisieux (lean)

Sources used: Carmel of Lisieux transcripts of Ms A, B, C (archives.carmeldelisieux.fr); source-library copies of Novissima Verba (1927), Soeur Thérèse (Taylor 1912/1924), Histoire d'une âme (1911); Carmel "Genesis of the introduction of the Cause" page.

## Fixes
1. Opening and "The Last Evening": the prioress (Mother Marie de Gonzague), not Mother Agnes, sent the community away, and placed the picture at 3 pm. Novissima Verba says "Mother Prioress". Also the order was wrong: "Mother, is it not the agony?", "I do not wish to suffer less" and the last words came before the bell and the return of the sisters. Fixed in both places. "Cells" removed (not in source).
2. Christmas 1886 (chapter 4): her father's words in Ms A are "Enfin, heureusement que c'est la dernière année!", not "too babyish". Céline said "do not come down". Father "did not guess" was unsupported; Ms A says he laughed. Fixed.
3. Lift quotation: it is in Ms C (1897, to Mother Marie de Gonzague), not Ms B. Label fixed in biography, teachings, and quotes list. Chronology reworded (Ms B, Sept 1896, is now "earlier"). Labeled as 1912 English translation. Scripture/"need not grow" detail now from Ms C.
4. Vocation quote and French line: Ms B, Sept 1896 (confirmed). The French line comes after the vocation sentence, so the order was changed.
5. Ball quotation: confirmed in Ms A (1912 translation); labeled. The prayer was made "for some time" before Rome, so the text now says so.
6. Good Friday 1896 (chapter 9): "Dearest Mother, what hope was mine!" is not in Ms C. Cut. "Handkerchief" is not in Ms C (she checked at the window). "She told no one" contradicted Ms C (she told the prioress of her hope next morning). Chapter retitled "A Sign in the Night". "Last eighteen months" replaced by "about a year and a half". "Within weeks of Easter" replaced by Ms C's "joyful days of the Easter season".
7. Pranzini: Ms A says "mon premier enfant", not "first sinner"; she read La Croix the day after the execution; she hid her tears (did not run out). "She would never say she converted him" replaced by what Ms A says. Share-kit and miracle entry fixed.
8. Statue of the smile: Ms A says the Virgin appeared and smiled, she could not recognize Marie at first, and the healing came gently. "Statue came alive" and "illness was gone" removed. "The Church's own documents give the later date" had no support; cut.
9. Victoire story: Ms A has Victoire laughing and not leading the prayer at the little May altar, not "family prayers". Fixed. "Cry-baby at heart" quotation not in Ms A; cut.
10. Bayeux bishop: Ms A says he received them "very coldly", not "kind". Pope audience: "everyone would agree if you said yes" (not "all would be settled"). "On the surface all was bitterness" replaced by Ms A (peace in the depths, bitterness filled her soul).
11. Carmel entry: "fell into Pauline's arms" not in Ms A; now "embraces of the sisters who had been mothers to her".
12. Laundry: Ms C says she drew back first, then tried to want more. Noise quarrel: Ms C says she wanted to defend herself, lacked the virtue to stay silent, and fled. The claim "hated confrontation" was cut.
13. "Sundays and feast-days" quotation: source says "tries me most", not "the most". Fixed in biography and quotes list. Counsels quotations now labeled "recorded by a novice" (edited 1912 book, not her manuscripts). "Should I guide you wrongly" and "Good God aught but love" are from the 1912 closing chapter by her sister, not the last conversations; text fixed.
14. "Sisters did not take her promise seriously" and "people who lived with her did not notice anything unusual" had no primary support; cut. "A nun who wrote these down could have cut any. Mother Agnes did not" cut (moralizing).
15. "Fr Rodrigo" corrected to "Rodrigue" (Carmel archives). "Gilo" corrected to "Giloteaux, Paulin" (archive.org metadata) in text, sources, and miracles. Apostolic process opened in the sacristy of Bayeux cathedral (Carmel archives), not "the cathedral".
16. Absolute or "first" claims softened or attributed: first Carmelite buried there (Taylor); wireless and applause firsts (Taylor); "first married couple canonized together" cut; "never gone to the missions" reworded; "not all her sisters would live to see" (false: they outlived her by decades) replaced; Lisieux "first feast" now "after the canonization". Relic tour, York and Darien figures hedged (Wikipedia only).
17. One-sided dispute: Langlois on Mother Agnes's 1917 document now says the research did not reach Agnes's text or Gonzague's side.
18. Polish: cut a moralizing last sentence in "Roses in St Peter's". Added a line in "How This Life Was Researched" and Sources about the Carmel transcripts and edited texts. Summary and share-kit facts aligned with fixes.
