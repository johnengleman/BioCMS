On the evening of 30 September 1897, a prioress sent a dying nun's community out of the infirmary, thinking the end was far off, then had the bell rung to call them back. Thérèse Martin was twenty-four. She had promised to spend her Heaven doing good on earth and to let fall a shower of roses. Who was this young Carmelite, who stamped her foot at a maid, whose eyes filled at a Christmas remark, and broke the rule of silence to ask the Pope for her convent? Follow her from a Christmas night to a fog of doubt, and find out what became of her promise.
