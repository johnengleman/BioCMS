On the evening of 30 September 1897, a prioress sent a dying nun's community out of the infirmary, thinking the end was far off, then had the bell rung to call them back. Thérèse Martin was twenty-four. She had promised to spend her Heaven doing good on earth and to let fall a shower of roses. Who was this young Carmelite who asked to be treated like a little ball kicked into a corner, and who broke the rule of silence to ask the Pope for her convent? Follow her from a Christmas night of tears to a fog of doubt, and find out what happened to her promise.
