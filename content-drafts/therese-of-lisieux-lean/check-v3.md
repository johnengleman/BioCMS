## The three uncertain facts
1. Alexander Grant "became a Catholic": CONFIRMED. He wrote on 9 Apr 1911 that he could not stay a Presbyterian and was baptised on 20 Apr 1911 at Edinburgh (E-P7-081, E-P7-102 cards: PA-05 and the letter of 23 Apr 1911 printed by Laveille). The newspaper notice, influenza, and photograph details match E-P7-050 (Grant's own words). Added the year: "In 1911 he became a Catholic."
2. Sister Saint-Vincent de Paul at Thérèse's feet: CONFIRMED with attribution. Céline's 1911 testimony (PO 4, F-EW-1708) names her and says Céline heard the story from the sister herself. Pauline (1915) and Céline name her as the wreaths sister (F-EW-745, F-EW-747); the 1910 deposition leaves her unnamed. Text now says "Céline testified, on the sister's own word". Not claimed: that she was the meat-juice sister (the text does not name that sister).
3. The 12 May 1892 parlour visit: CONFIRMED. Mme Guérin's letter of 15 May 1892 says Louis came home on 10 May, was carried to the carriage, "wept all the time," and went to the Carmel on Thursday 12 May (12 May 1892 was a Thursday). The 1912 Story of a Soul has "raised his eyes, and pointing upwards ... 'In Heaven!'" and calls it a sad interview. Text kept ("Two days later").

## Fixes
1. Ch. 2 (birth): "Must I lose this one too?" was dated to 1 March. It is from the letter of 17 Jan 1873 (CF 86); "no hope at all" is 1 March (CF 87). Reordered.
2. Ch. 3 (Pauline will be Mama): "She told Céline later why" had no source. The reason (Pauline might feel left out) is Thérèse's own account, so now "she explained".
3. Ch. 4 (1883 illness): Dr Notta's "not hysteria" was told to the family by Marie (PA 7); added "as Marie later reported".
4. Ch. 6 (Pranzini): "the chaplain's successor" overstated. Abbé Faure was at the scaffold and told his successor Valadier, who told the court. Reworded as report on the word of the priest at the scaffold.
5. Ch. 6 (Rome): "Rome brought out every bold thing in her" cut to "her boldness". "The letter that evening" changed to "that day": LT 36 is dated 20 Nov, with no hour.
6. Ball block quote: the old fragment began "Like" (not in the source) and stopped before "or pressed to His Heart just as it might please Him". Now quoted from the 1912 text with an opening ellipsis and the full ending.
7. Ch. 7 (Carmel): "rare hours of direction" had no source ("an hour's direction", F-EW-537). Cut "rare". "Céline wrote to a friend": the letter went to Pauline Romet, whose role the cards do not settle; now "Céline wrote".
8. Ch. 8: the 8 Dec 1892 talk was with one novice (Marthe). The text said "another novice" for the same words. Fixed.
9. Ch. 9: "In the meantime she had made the most daring act of her life" was an invented ranking, and the Offering (June 1895) came before the notebook (Jan 1896). Now "Months before". Pauline "knelt as she passed Pauline's stall" corrected to "passed Pauline in the choir" (the card says Thérèse passed on the way to her own stall).
10. Ch. 10 (black door): the "I don't believe in eternal life" words come from TSA's 1911 testimony (F-EW-1131), a separate confidence. The text had joined them to the dream story (1929 circular). Separated, with "testified".
11. Ch. 11: the top-and-ninepins sentence trimmed, no change of fact.
12. Ch. 13: "her critics under oath" has no support (the nun who wrote in 1907 left before the process). Now "others who had known her". "A seminarian ... cried out" now "as Taylor tells it" (Laveille gives different words, F-OM miracles card). Death scene: the candle at her eyes is from Pauline's notebook; other accounts leave the sister unnamed. Added "By Pauline's account".

14. Note on the Sources: Marie's 1915 letter said the sisters would "set right the Roman advocate's portrait". "Not simple enough" was the writer's gloss; cut. Added that two quotations come from Taylor's lives.
15. "How This Life Was Researched": 3,661 fact cards (counted: 2,670 + 270 + 136 + 585), 33 and 26 depositions, and LT 1–266 all confirmed. "All 266 of her surviving letters" changed to "all 266 letters in the Carmel's edition".
