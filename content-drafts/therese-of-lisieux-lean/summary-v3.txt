As a small child she rolled on the floor in fury, and at about three she took a whole basket of doll clothes, saying, "I choose everything!" At twenty-four, dying of tuberculosis in a Normandy convent, she overheard a sister wonder what anyone could write about her, since she had never done anything worth speaking about. Thérèse of Lisieux wanted to be a great saint and found she was a grain of sand. In her last eighteen months even Heaven went dark for her, and she went on loving. How did the nun who had done nothing worth speaking about become one of the most loved saints of modern times, and a Doctor of the Church?
