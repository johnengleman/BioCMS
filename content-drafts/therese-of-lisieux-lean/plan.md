# Plan: Thérèse of Lisieux (lean, tier A)

**Why famous:** She was a Carmelite nun who died at 24, and her autobiography, Story of a Soul, made her one of the most loved saints of the modern Church (canonized 1925, Doctor of the Church 1997).
**What set her apart:** She taught a "little way": trust and small acts of love, open to people who cannot do great things. She lived it through doubt, illness and dryness, and she said so plainly.

**Hook (scene at the edge, then her own words):** On 30 Sept 1897 her sister sent the community away, thinking the end was far off, then rang the bell again. Thérèse had promised to spend her Heaven doing good on earth and to let fall a shower of roses. The story answers in 1925, when five roses fall at the Pope's feet, and in the cures the Church weighed.

**Chapters**
1. A Bell Rung Twice: the deathbed scene, thesis. Quote: none. Ends on the promise.
2. The Youngest of Nine: Alençon, Lisieux, temper, losing a second mother. Ends: Pauline leaves for Carmel.
3. The Statue That Smiled: 1883 illness, date conflict. Ends: a child who now wants Carmel.
4. A Cry-Baby at Christmas: 1886. Ends: a new strength and one sinner to save.
5. Her First Sinner: Pranzini. Ends: age 14, the door to Carmel still closed.
6. Kneeling Before the Pope: Bayeux, Rome. Quote: the ball. Ends: a no that is not a no.
7. Doors Closing at Carmel: entry, father's illness, profession. Ends: a father who dies, a letter she must write.
8. Laundry Water and Sister Peter: novitiate. Quote: the thread. Ends: a request from Pauline.
9. An Elevator to Heaven: little way, Act of Oblation, Ms B. Quotes: lift, vocation. Ends: Holy Thursday night.
10. Blood on the Handkerchief: 1896, fog, Ms C. Ends: hemorrhages return.
11. A Promise of Roses: summer 1897. Quotes: Sundays, Heaven. Ends: last day.
12. The Last Evening: 29-30 Sept. Quote: last words. Ends: burial, a palm branch.
13. A Book Goes Out: circular, 1898 edition, pilgrims. Quote: love repays. Ends: the Church asks questions.
14. Testing the Witnesses: trials, Verde, exhumations. Ends: war, soldiers' petitions.
15. Miracles the Church Weighed: four approved cures. Quote: Charles Anne. Ends: decree of 1923.
16. Roses in St Peter's: beatification, canonization. Ends: the hook answered.
17. After the Roses: patron, Doctor, parents, relics. Quote: her promise.

**Open loops:** (1) the bell and the promise of roses (answered ch. 16); (2) the "fog" she asked to be told to others (ch. 11); (3) Pauline's edits to her words (ch. 13); (4) the Promoter's doubts about her humility (ch. 14, 17).
