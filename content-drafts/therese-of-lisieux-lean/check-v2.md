# Check v2: Thérèse of Lisieux (story rewrite)

Checked against notes-*.md, check.md (v1 fixes), Ms A/B/C in the 1912 English text, Taylor 1924 and 1930, Novissima Verba 1927, and Laveille 1928 (source-library). Originals kept as *.before-check. Prose was not re-chopped.

## Confirmed
- Zélie Martin died 28 August 1877 (Taylor 1924, quoting Ms A: "My dear Mother died on August 28, 1877"; Laveille 1928: half an hour after midnight). The "1877" in the text is correct. The notes tag [M] is now resolved.
- Last evening: Novissima Verba matches the scene order (ice, dismissal shortly after seven, "is it not yet the agony", "I do not wish to suffer less", crucifix, last words, bell, Credo, 7:20). On 29 Sept she asked "Mother Prioress", so "the prioress" is right.
- Pius XI "first I beatified" is his own 1925 words (Taylor 1930), so the "first person he beatified" claim is supported.
- Father's "In Heaven!" and Duboscq as later Promoter of the Faith are in Taylor.
- Block quotes (ball, thread, vocation, lift, roses, Sundays, last words, Anne) match the notes and the 1912 text. The shoes scene matches the voice-examples card.

## Fixes
1. Carmel entry (chapter "Doors Closing"): "he was weeping, and so was she" is wrong. Ms A (1912) says she did not shed a tear; he blessed her through his tears. Fixed.
2. "Exactly one person in the world would take her side" is false. Ms A: Pauline encouraged her, Marie thought her too young. Replaced with a claim the source supports (her father stood beside her). "Everyone told her" softened to "many people".
3. "A Little Way": "she announced no new teaching" contradicts Ms C ("a little way that is wholly new"). Fixed. "The same pages speak of a ball, a top, and ninepins" was wrong (ball is Ms A; top and ninepins are the 1896 Christmas gift and letter). Reworded.
4. Opening: "her only writings were still in a drawer, and hardly anyone outside Lisieux had heard her name" had no source (invented scenery, absolute). Replaced. "In the weeks before" became "In her last months" (the promise dates are 9 June and 17 July).
5. Rome: "a Noble Guard lifted her away" drifted. Ms A says the Noble Guards motioned, came forward, and she was made to rise; the Pope laid his hand on her lips, then raised it to bless her. Fixed.
6. Good Friday 1896: the order was confusing ("next night", then "that morning"). Reordered. "The consolation she had felt in prayer left her" drifted from Ms C, which says the thought of Heaven became a torment. Fixed.
7. Birds at her death: "the sisters confirm the birds" overstated. Novissima Verba has one older sister remark on them. Fixed.
8. "Should I guide you wrongly" is in the closing chapter of the 1912 book, not Counsels and Reminiscences, and it was cut before "and tell you to take another road". Label and quotation fixed; "told the novices" changed to "a novice".
9. Invented comment cut: "It is a little unusual for the papers ... they do not soften it" (de Teil list). Also cut "dry humor that most readers miss".
10. Absolute or motive claims softened: "never left her convent"; "could hide none of it"; "nobody in the family knew how to name"; Mother Agnes "wanted her sister to be a saint" (now "a stake in how Thérèse was remembered"); "scenes that nobody else recorded".
11. "A few minutes later the bell rang" had no timing in the source; now "soon afterward". Duboscq sentence now says he held the office that raises objections (Taylor), not that he was "asked".
12. Summary: "wept at a Christmas remark" was wrong (her eyes filled; she hid the tears). Fixed, plus "a night of tears" to "a Christmas night". entry-v2.json updated (saint.biography and saint.summary). The JSON copy had drifted ("don't come down", "right now"); it now matches biography-v2.html.
