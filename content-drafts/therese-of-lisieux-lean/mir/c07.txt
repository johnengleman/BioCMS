=== 161 | A scent from the Life at the Abbaye aux Bois, Paris, about 1909

=== 162 | The Presezzi baby with double pneumonia, Trezzo d'Adda, 1910
The only daughter of Ange Presezzi, about eighteen months old, was dying of double pneumonia, and her father had already lost another girl of the same age to the same illness. Her uncle came weeping to the church at Concesa, and the cook, Brother Romée, said to pray to Thérèse instead. The lay brothers said seven Our Fathers and seven Hail Marys, and the uncle soon returned joyful. The child relapsed the next day and was cured for good within fifteen days. The doctor of Trezzo called it a miracle, and she was still well in October 1910.
