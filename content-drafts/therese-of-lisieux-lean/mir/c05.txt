=== 091 | M. David's holy death as an answer to prayer, August 1888
Thérèse wrote to her uncle and aunt Guérin that God had given "the great grace" to the dying M. David, whose dispositions to appear before God she praised. She said God had answered all the prayers her aunt made for the soul of her dear relative. He died on 22 August 1888. This is her own report.

=== 092 | Her father's recovery after the Carmel's prayers, autumn 1888
Louis Martin was very ill in autumn 1888. Thérèse wrote to him that the whole Carmel had been in prayer and that God had at last heard its sighs and given back her King. To her old teacher she wrote that Jesus had restored him for the time of her clothing. No diagnosis is given, and this is her own report.

=== 093 | Snow on her clothing day, Carmel, 10 January 1889
Thérèse always wished for snow on her clothing day, but the day was extremely mild and rainy. After the ceremony, as she entered the cloister, she saw the courtyard covered with snow and wrote of a delicacy of Jesus. She also wrote that the whole town was astonished, and the community spoke of the little miracle. The sources differ on whether any weather record supports this, and none was checked.

=== 094 | A dream for Marie Guérin, 1889
Before she received her cousin Marie Guérin's letter, Thérèse sensed her anguish, and that night she dreamed she was trying to console her without success. Marie suffered from scruples and missed Communions. Thérèse hoped the Virgin would cure her on the last day of May and suggested a candle at Notre-Dame des Victoires. This is her own report.

When a parlour visit ended too soon, Thérèse asked Jesus to tell Céline what she had wished to say. === 100 | Transports of love and flights of the spirit, about 1892
Before Carmel Thérèse had transports of love and told God she would even go to hell so that He would be loved there. In her noviciate, for nearly eight days, she felt separated from her body and did her refectory work as if with a borrowed body. Several times in the garden at the evening silence she felt flights of the spirit. Mère Agnès reported this from Thérèse's words of July 1897. It is an inner experience, not an approved miracle.

Thérèse wept and prayed that Céline would not dance at an evening party. Céline long avoided dancing, until a young man led her onto the floor near the end. A few days after her Offering to Merciful Love of 9 June 1895, while beginning the Way of the Cross in choir, Thérèse felt as if plunged entirely into fire. === 113 | The novice who was changed in an instant, about 1895
A religious, before her profession, was worn out and in interior trial and asked Thérèse for comfort before evening prayer. Thérèse said she had no time now and that God willed her to suffer alone for the moment. In chapel the novice doubted her vocation for the first time, then felt an instantaneous and extraordinary change and came out transformed. === 128 | The ecstasy at the moment of death, 30 September 1897
After her last words, "My God, I love you," Thérèse raised her head and fixed a point just above the statue of Mary for several minutes. Her purple, congested face took on a healthy colour and became white, almost transparent, and her eyes shone with peace. Sister Marie de l'Eucharistie held a candle close, and the eyelids did not move. Marie said the look recalled the cure of 1883. Before this she had a rattle for over two hours, a congested face, purple hands and icy feet. Her sisters describe it as an ecstasy of love, not as a miracle.
