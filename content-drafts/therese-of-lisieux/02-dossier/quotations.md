### A. Her own writing

- **MQ-001** [OW-A-1] « Je choisis tout! » — "I choose everything!" — Ms A 10r–10v. Age about 3, taking Léonie's whole basket of doll clothes; later: "My God, I choose everything. I don't want to be a saint by halves."

- **MQ-004** [OW-A-4] « je remplissais le seau comme un petit poulet remplit son oeuf !... » — "I filled the bucket the way a little chick fills its egg!" — Ms A 16r. She fell backwards into a bucket of water.

- **MQ-005** [OW-A-5] « Oh! non, ma petite Mère, j'aime mieux ne pas avoir de sou!... » — "Oh no, my little Mother, I'd rather not have the sou!" — Ms A 8v. Offered a coin to kiss the ground, aged about 3.

- **MQ-006** [OW-A-6] « Eh bien! moi, c'est Pauline qui sera Maman! » — "Well, for me, Pauline will be Mama!" — Ms A 13r. After her mother's funeral, 1877.

- **MQ-008** [OW-A-8] « Papa, ouvre les yeux! » — "Papa, open your eyes!" — Ms A 14r. Showing her father the little altar she built in the garden wall.

- **MQ-009** [OW-A-9] « Au moins si Papa tombe, je ne vais pas avoir la douleur de le voir mourir, puisque je vais mourir avec lui! » — "At least if Papa falls, I won't have the pain of seeing him die, because I'll die with him!" — Ms A 21r. She pressed herself against his ladder.

- **MQ-010** [OW-A-10] « les cinq années que j'y passai furent les plus tristes de ma vie » — "the five years I spent there were the saddest of my life" — Ms A 22r. The abbey school.

- **MQ-011** [OW-A-11] « Je pense au bon Dieu, à la vie... à l'éternité, enfin je pense !... » — "I think about God, about life... about eternity; I just think!" — Ms A 33v. To a teacher who asked what she did alone behind her bed curtain.

- **MQ-012** [OW-A-12] « mon écriture de chat et mon orthographe qui n'est rien moins que naturelle » — "my cat-scratch handwriting and my spelling, which is anything but natural" — Ms A 37v. Why her uncle's family thought her slow.

### Official wording (our translation)

- **MQ-410** [OM-B4] « de puella enim agitur, quae brevem vitam in claustro absconditam egit, paucis cognita, donec vixit » — "for it concerns a girl who lived a short life hidden in the cloister, known to few while she lived." Decree on virtues, AAS 13 p. 449.

- **MQ-411** [OM-B5] « Ancillae Dei decumbentis lectulus religiosae familiae universae sanctitatis cathedra fuit » — "her sickbed was a chair of holiness for the whole community." Brief *Quod Ioannes*, AAS 15 p. 205.

- **MQ-412** [OM-B6] « "Religionis magistra" seu "parvus Doctor" » — the school chaplain's names for her. Bull, AAS 17 p. 338.

- **MQ-413** [OM-B7] « pergatque pluviam rosarum e caelo in homines demittere » — "and she continues to send down a rain of roses from heaven upon men." Pius XI, 14 May 1923, AAS 15 p. 283.

- **MQ-414** [OM-B8] « alia non occurrit Causa, quae tam celeri tamque prospero actorum cursu supremam conscenderit Canonizationis metam » — "no other cause is found that reached canonization by so swift and happy a course." AAS 17 p. 200.

- **MQ-415** [OM-B9] « se nihil Deo aliud, nisi amorem, dedisse » — "that she had given God nothing but love." Canonization homily, AAS 17 p. 213.

- **MQ-416** [OM-B10] « ut etiam super nos … rosarum, videlicet gratiarum quibus indigemus, pluvia descendat » — "that on us too a rain of roses, that is, of the graces we need, may fall." Bull, AAS 17 p. 346.

- **MQ-417** [OM-B11] Benedict XV (Taylor's English, 1921): "There lies the secret of sanctity." Pius XI (Taylor's English, 11 Feb 1923): "our beloved guiding-star … miracle of virtues and prodigy of miracles."
