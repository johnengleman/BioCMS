# Scenes — merged (stage 2b)

This file merges the four `parts/*/scenes.md` files and lists the 25 strongest scenes first. Each ranked scene combines every source scene entry that tells the same event, so duplicates are merged, not repeated. The merged scene lists its cards and quotes each source entry unchanged below it. Source keys: `S-EW-nn` and `S-OM-nn` as in the parts; `LF-n` (life-family, numbered list); `OW-n` (own-writings main list); `OW-L-n` (letter review LT 110–181); `OW-M-n` (letters LT 1–109); `OW-N-n` (letters LT 182–266 and Carnet jaune). Some OW letter scenes cite cards by title, not by number. Those titles stay in the source text.

## Counts

- Source scene entries read: 124 (eyewitness 23 ranked; life-family 28; official-miracles 22; own-writings 51 in four lists). Eyewitness also has 83 unranked candidates, kept in the appendix.
- Ranked scenes below: 25, built from 69 source entries.
- Source entries not used in the top 25: 55. They are listed in the second part, unchanged.

## The 25 strongest scenes

Ranked by how much concrete human detail the cards give, how well the evidence supports the scene, and how much it shows the person.
