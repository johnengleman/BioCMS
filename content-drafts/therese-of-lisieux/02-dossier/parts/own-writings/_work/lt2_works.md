(Letters agent: this section lists letters that are missing, fragmentary, or only notes. It also lists works mentioned in the letters.)

- LT 118 (8–20 Sept 1890, date uncertain): a mock wedding announcement, not a letter. The 1911 edition prints it in edited form inside the life story.
- LT 119 (23 Sept 1890) and LT 128 (5 July 1891): short keepsake inscriptions only.
- LT 156 (21 Jan 1894): "Le rêve de l'Enfant Jésus", text for a painting by Thérèse (the painting is not in the file).
- LT 161 (26 Apr 1894): cover letter for a poem for Céline's 25th birthday (the poem is not in the file; it is PN 3 on St Cecilia, from outside knowledge). It ends with a Carmel bracket note referring to LT 162.
- LT 162 (26 Apr 1894): the text of a tiny picture and its envelope only.
- LT 167 (18 July 1894): mentions "deux petits cantiques que j'ai composés" (two little canticles), sent to Céline. They are not identified in the letter.
- LT 167 bis (19 July 1894): a two-line P.S. on a letter by Sr Marie du Sacré-Cœur.
- LT 174 (end Jan 1895) and LT 179 (after 8 Sept 1895): one- or two-line notes. LT 179's meaning is unclear.
- LT 177 (7 July (?) 1895): short note. Date uncertain.
- Uncertain dates in headers: LT 149 (20 (?) Oct 1893), LT 153 (Dec (?) 1893), LT 157 (Mar or May (?) 1894), LT 158 (Mar (?) 1894), LT 173 (Jan (?) 1895), LT 177 (7 July (?) 1895).
- Referred to but not in this file: the letter Thérèse sent Céline "at La Musse" promising she would soon be in the Carmel (LT 169 refers to it; it may be LT 168); Fr Pichon's letter quoted in LT 120; the letter "from Rome" (papal blessing) in LT 115; the uncle's letter to Sr Marie-Madeleine (LT 172).
