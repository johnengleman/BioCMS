## 3. Poems (PN 1–54, PN 18bis), supplementary poems (PS 1–8), pious recreations (RP 1–8), prayers (Pri 1–21)

All 92 items were fetched from the Carmel site. Only dates and titles are listed here; occasions, recipients and tunes still need to be read from each page (see HANDOFF.md). Facts from her other writings: she composed PN 17 "Vivre d'amour" on 26 Feb 1895 in her head during the day and wrote the 15 stanzas from memory in the evening silence, without a draft (CJ 5.8.7); she played Joan of Arc in RP 1 (21 Jan 1894) and RP 3 (21 Jan 1895); PN 54 "Pourquoi je t'aime, ô Marie !" (May 1897) is her last long poem and, she said, holds "all I would preach" about the Virgin (CJ 21.8.3); she wrote in Ms C 7v that her 1896–1897 poems express what she *wants* to believe, not what she feels. Her poems were sung in community to popular tunes (tune names are on the pages).
