# Scenes — eyewitness and process testimony

The strongest scenes the evidence supports, chosen from the merged cards. Each lists its cards (final numbers) and why it matters. A longer, unranked list of scene proposals from the extraction batches is in `_scene_candidates.md` in this folder.

### S-EW-01 — Winter evening after Matins: Marie pushes Pauline to order the childhood memoir
- Cards: F-EW-890, F-EW-883
- Why it matters: The origin of Manuscript A, told by two of the four sisters present.

### S-EW-02 — "Thérèse has no conscience!" — a school mistress's remark and fifteen days of tears
- Cards: F-EW-226
- Why it matters: Shows the scrupulous, oversensitive child the Benedictines knew.

### S-EW-03 — The cure of 13 May 1883: Marie sees, Léonie sees nothing
- Cards: F-EW-296, F-EW-297
- Why it matters: Two sister-witnesses at one bedside, with an honest limit to what one of them saw.

### S-EW-04 — On the Rome train and at Bologna station
- Cards: F-EW-425, F-EW-423, F-EW-424
- Why it matters: Her wit and her self-knowledge at 14, as Céline remembered them.

### S-EW-05 — Canon Delatroëtte's outburst and his "Te Deum" at the enclosure door
- Cards: F-EW-379, F-EW-624, F-EW-991
- Why it matters: The main clerical opponent of her early entry, and his later change of mind.

### S-EW-06 — The cobweb and the daily weeding: "this child does absolutely nothing"
- Cards: F-EW-443, F-EW-444, F-EW-2029
- Why it matters: Mère Marie de Gonzague's deliberate severity in the first year.

### S-EW-07 — The Sister she disliked, whom Céline took for her best friend
- Cards: F-EW-2042, F-EW-904, F-EW-1091
- Why it matters: Charity against antipathy, with Marie's jealousy as a twist.

### S-EW-08 — A large particle of the Host on the corporal
- Cards: F-EW-2116, F-EW-1083
- Why it matters: The sacristan's reverence and quick action.

### S-EW-09 — Laundry days: steam, dirty water, and "our children will starve"
- Cards: F-EW-464, F-EW-2220, F-EW-2192, F-EW-2067
- Why it matters: Her physical work and how she drove the novices with humour.

### S-EW-10 — The Offering to Merciful Love, 9–11 June 1895
- Cards: F-EW-1065, F-EW-1055, F-EW-1056
- Why it matters: How the Act began, who joined her, and the priest who corrected a phrase.

### S-EW-11 — The mussel shell for a novice's tears
- Cards: F-EW-2077
- Why it matters: Her playful, practical way as novice mistress.

### S-EW-12 — The spinning top and the Christmas letter of 1896
- Cards: F-EW-2076
- Why it matters: Humour and tenderness in her direction of Marie de la Trinité.

### S-EW-13 — "What will our Mother write in her circular?" — the kitchen remark
- Cards: F-EW-1228, F-EW-1218
- Why it matters: The clearest record that some nuns found her unremarkable.

### S-EW-14 — She could imitate voices and manners
- Cards: F-EW-192
- Why it matters: Her gift for mimicry at recreation — a side the pious portrait hides.

### S-EW-15 — Morphine refused: the prioress and the doctor
- Cards: F-EW-1589
- Why it matters: The cost of Gonzague's rule during the last illness.

### S-EW-16 — Five hundred pointes de feu and a blanket folded in four
- Cards: F-EW-1221
- Why it matters: The primitive treatment of her tuberculosis, seen by her infirmarian sister.

### S-EW-17 — The box of lilies, holy water, and laughing at her own funeral
- Cards: F-EW-1519
- Why it matters: Humour close to death.

### S-EW-18 — 30 September 1897: the last afternoon and death
- Cards: F-EW-1651, F-EW-2101
- Why it matters: Her death as recorded by the Carmel and the witnesses.

### S-EW-19 — "A burial where nobody weeps": the funeral of 4 October 1897
- Cards: F-EW-1738, F-EW-1739, F-EW-1735
- Why it matters: Conflicting memories of a small or large funeral.

### S-EW-20 — The sandals burned as "filth"
- Cards: F-EW-1689
- Why it matters: How ordinary she still seemed to some after her death.

### S-EW-21 — Gonzague weeps before a child portrait of Thérèse
- Cards: F-EW-1264
- Why it matters: The prioress's own late verdict on her.

### S-EW-22 — Pranzini's crucifix, according to the prison chaplain
- Cards: F-EW-415, F-EW-697
- Why it matters: The event she prayed for, checked by the man who was there.

### S-EW-23 — Gallipoli: the banknotes in the cash box, 1910–1911
- Cards: M-EW-080
- Why it matters: The most famous posthumous wonder examined at the Ordinary Process.
