# Batch K — community pages (Gonzague biography, Marie de la Trinité, circulars, short biographies)

Base URL for every source below: `https://archives.carmeldelisieux.fr/au-carmel-du-temps-de-therese/la-communaute/` + the path given.

Nature of the sources (read this before using the cards):
- **Circulars** (circulaire) = printed obituary letters sent to other Carmels, written by the prioress (here all by Mère Agnès de Jésus, 1905–1944). Pious in tone, but they often quote the dead nun's own notes and her process deposition.
- **Biographies** (biographie) = modern articles by Carmel of Lisieux writers (Sr Cécile ocd, 1929–2010; P. Gires; Sr Marie de la Rédemption). They quote older sources: NPPO/NPPA (nuns' notes before the Ordinary/Apostolic Process), PO/PA (the depositions), DE (Derniers Entretiens), CG (Correspondance générale), LT/LC/LD (letters), CJ (Carnet jaune), Ms A/B/C. I name the underlying source when the article gives it.
- The Gonzague biography (by Sr Cécile, unfinished) is openly a defence of Mère Marie de Gonzague against Mère Agnès's Apostolic Process testimony. Treat its opinions as a modern author's opinions, its quotations as useful data.
- Mère Marie de Gonzague = "Gonzague". Mère Agnès de Jésus (Pauline Martin) = "Agnès". Sr Geneviève (Céline Martin) = "Céline/Geneviève". Sr Marie du Sacré-Cœur (Marie Martin) = "Marie du S.-C.".

## A. Mère Marie de Gonzague (biography by Sr Cécile)
Source path for all A-cards: `mere-marie-de-gonzague/biographie-mere-marie-de-gonzague/`

### K-F01 — Gonzague: identity and offices
- Claim: Marie-Adèle-Rosalie Davy de Virville ("Marie Davy de Virville"), born Caen 20 Feb 1834, entered Lisieux Carmel 29 Nov 1860, professed 27 June 1862; sub-prioress 1866–1872; prioress 1874–1883, 1886–1893 (term extended one year in 1892), 1896–1902; bursar (1st depositary) and novice mistress 1883–86 and 1893–96; in 1896 prioress and novice mistress "seconded by Thérèse"; cancer of the tongue spring 1904; died 17 Dec 1904. In all, 6 years sub-prioress and 21 years prioress.
- When / where: 1834–1904, Lisieux
- People: Mère Marie de Gonzague
- Witness: Sr Cécile's chronological table (modern)
- Source: base + mere-marie-de-gonzague/biographie-mere-marie-de-gonzague/, "Survol biographique"
- New to English readers: partial
- Confidence: high — archival dates
- Notes: gives her family name only as "Marie Davy de Virville".

### K-F02 — Gonzague's appearance
- Claim: She was tall and slender, distinguished, with a pleasant voice. A family portrait painted about 1871 (age 37) shows strong features, a prominent nose, fine eyes and very high eyebrows. Photos of 1895–96 show her sewing, rinsing linen and haymaking.
- When / where: 1860–1896
- People: Gonzague
- Witness: Sr Cécile, describing the portrait and photos
- Source: Gonzague biography, §1 and §3 "Habile brodeuse"

### K-F03 — Céline on Gonzague's "childlike" side
- Claim: In 1947 Céline wrote that part of Gonzague's character was childlike; the sisters found joy in spoiling and protecting her, often from a real need of the heart, not by pretence.
- When / where: note to P. Piat, Sept 1947
- People: Sr Geneviève (Céline), Gonzague
- Witness: Céline, from long experience
- Source: Gonzague biography, opening
- Original words: « Elle avait un côté de son caractère comme enfantin. On trouvait de la joie à la gâter, à la choyer »
- Translation: "One side of her character was almost childish. We found joy in spoiling her, in pampering her."
- New to English readers: yes
- Confidence: high — dated written note

### K-F05 — The 1867 garden incident
- Claim: At the clothing of Sr Saint-Pierre (16 July 1867), sub-prioress Gonzague sulked for hours in the garden and could not be found. In the prioress's cell she stepped toward the window; nobody knows whether she meant to throw herself out. The story was hushed up for about 50 years and revived in the 1915 PA report (PA 143).
- When / where: 16 July 1867, Lisieux Carmel
- People: Gonzague; Mère Marie-Baptiste (prioress)
- Witness: report in PA 1915 (Agnès), summarised by Sr Cécile
- Source: Gonzague biography, §3 "Soeur Marie de Gonzague, sous-prieure"
- New to English readers: yes

### K-F08 — The PA text on Gonzague leaked and was used against her

- When / where: 1920–1947
- People: Agnès; P. Ubald; Maxence Van der Meersch; M. Dubosq
- Witness: Sr Cécile citing PA introduction (PA XIX, XXI)

### K-F37 — The October 1891 retreat: Gonzague's anger at the long confession
