# Quotations — eyewitness and process testimony

Original French under ~25 words (facts-only source) with our own English. Speaker is Thérèse unless stated. Public-domain English from the 1913–1926 books is marked PD in batch L entries at the end of works/scenes notes.

### Q-EW-001 — Pinching novices
- Speaker: Thérèse (as reported by Mère Agnès)
- Original: « Il y en a que je suis forcée de prendre par la peau, et d'autres par le bout des ailes. »
- English (ours): "Some I have to grab by the skin, others by the tips of their wings."
- Locator: PO 1, f. 154r, Session 7, 16 Aug 1910
- Rights: facts-only (short quotation)
- Context: Her method with the novices.
- Extraction ID: A-Q01
