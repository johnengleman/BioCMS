# Works — as the witnesses describe them

This family's sources are testimony, not editions. So this list gives what the witnesses say about how each kind of work was made, dated, received, or changed. The texts themselves are covered by the own-writings family. Each line points to cards in facts.md. Rights: the works themselves are public-domain; the testimony about them is facts-only.

## Summary (from the cards)
- Manuscript A: written 1895 for Mère Agnès after a winter evening conversation (Marie's prompting); finished for Agnès's feast, 20 Jan 1896.
- Manuscript B: Sept 1896, at Marie's request during Thérèse's retreat.
- Manuscript C: June–July 1897, for Mère Marie de Gonzague, at Mère Agnès's suggestion.
- Histoire d'une âme: edited by Mère Agnès; printed 1898 in place of the obituary circular (review at Mondaye by Fr Godefroy Madelaine; Gonzague signed). Witnesses discuss the edits.
- Poems, plays, paintings (including decorations and small pictures for Sisters), letters, prayers and the Act of Offering of 9–11 June 1895: see the lists below.
