# Carmel archive pages (rights unknown; facts only; our translation)
personnage/zelie-martin: Azélie-Marie Guérin b. 23 Dec 1831 at Gandelain (Orne) [CONFLICT: couple page says parish of Saint-Denis-sur-Sarthon; Laveille p28 says Saint-Denis-sur-Sarthon]; to Alençon 1844; Dames de l'Adoration; wanted religious life, not admitted for health; learned lacemaking, made it for a Paris house, own account from 1863; d. 28 Aug 1877 breast cancer. URL https://archives.carmeldelisieux.fr/personnage/zelie-martin/
personnage/louis-martin: b. 22 Aug 1823 Bordeaux, d. La Musse 29 Jul 1894; childhood Bordeaux, Avignon, Strasbourg; watchmaking with father's cousin at Rennes; Great St Bernard not admitted; Strasbourg and Paris; 1850 bought watchmaking business at Alençon, rue du Pont-Neuf; "artério-sclérose cérébrale" -> Bon Sauveur de Caen 3 years; nursed at Lisieux by Céline, Léonie, Guérins; died at La Musse (Eure), Guérin property.
le-couple-martin (https://archives.carmeldelisieux.fr/le-couple-martin/): Louis 1.76 m, "prestance d'officier", chestnut hair, grey-blue ("pers") eyes (P. Piat, Histoire d'une famille). Brother Pierre 4 yrs older, sister Marie 3; ondoyé at birth; baptized Ste-Eulalie in October; Avignon 1826 sister Anne Fannie born; Strasbourg 1828; retirement Dec 1830 to Alençon. Louis good at French, copied favourite passages in notebooks, good at drawing, learned German at Strasbourg. 1842 Rennes with father's cousin Louis Bohard (loved Breton culture); Sept 1843 left Rennes for Strasbourg, via Swiss Alps, saw Great St Bernard; lived with Mathey family (watchmakers) at Strasbourg; after 2 years returned to St Bernard hoping to enter; no Latin; studied Latin (and Greek) over a year at Alençon; illness; then Paris apprenticeship lodging with maternal grandmother; friend cousin Henri-Charles de Lacauve (École Militaire). 1850 master watchmaker, workshop rue du Pont-Neuf, parish St-Pierre-de-Montsort; later jewellery; never opened Sunday. 1857 bought the Pavillon (hexagonal tower, small garden, Sénatorerie district); kept fishing gear there; statue of the Virgin in its garden. ~8 years bachelor life.
Zélie: slightly below average height, pretty, dark hair, long nose, black eyes, "ombre de mélancolie" (P. Piat after Pauline). Admits she is impatient; critical remarks about society. Born 23 Dec 1831, parish Saint-Denis-sur-Sarthon, baptized next day. Mother austere; CF 15 to Isidore: "Mon enfance, ma jeunesse ont été tristes comme un linceul, car, si ma mère te gâtait, pour moi, tu le sais, elle était trop sévère". Father became carpenter (menuisier) to supplement pension; mother opened a café briefly. Zélie at 13 at school of Perpetual Adoration (Sisters of Picpus). Sister Marie-Louise 2 years older; Isidore 10 years younger ("gâté"). Confraternities: scapular 1845, Sacred Heart 1852. ~1850 refused by Sisters of St Vincent de Paul, perhaps health: severe headaches and breathing problems throughout childhood. 8 Dec 1851 voice "Fais faire du point d'Alençon" ("Fais faire" not "fais": run a business). Learned assembleuse trade; 1853 own "bureau" in parents' house, rue Saint-Blaise; Thursdays for workers; she repaired tulle and did the joining. Marie-Louise and father made first Paris contacts; Marie-Dosithée letter 10 May 1874: began "petitement et dans les larmes", so timid "je ne pouvais aller à nos voisins sans rougir". From 1856 worked for the Paris house Pigache; silver medal 20 Jun 1858 at Alençon exhibition praising "Mademoiselle Zélie Guérin". Own account again 1863 when Louis handled clients.
Met on Pont Saint-Léonard spring 1858 (Zélie 26, Louis 35); voice "C'est celui-là que j'ai préparé pour toi". Married 13 Jul 1858 at midnight [civil then religious; site says "à minuit"]. Céline: "entente parfaite". Abbé Dumaine (PA 120): union remarkable. Initially brotherly union at Louis's proposal. Veillées with roast chestnuts, songs, poems, Louis's imitations (mimicry). Marie at process: "L'éducation dans notre famille était affectueuse, mais nullement molle." CF 195: "la brutalité n'a jamais converti personne, elle fait seulement des esclaves." Workers' early Mass. Zélie visited sick workers. 1865 noticed lump in breast from knock on table corner in youth; no treatment; pain 11 years later. June 1877 Lourdes "elle qui n'aime pas les pèlerinages". Died 28 Aug aged 45.
Louis at Lisieux: lived on his rentes ("vit de ses rentes"); garden, fishing, trips; first Mass daily; Nocturnal Adoration with Isidore; St Vincent de Paul Society. Pauline entered Carmel Oct 1882; Marie 15 Oct 1886 ("sa première", hard blow). 1886 Léonie "sur un coup de tête" entered Poor Clares during Alençon trip; months; Louis fetched her without reproach. Pentecost 1887 Thérèse asked; Louis said she had time, she convinced him. Léonie then Visitation, left for health; third try 1893. Céline chose to stay with father.
ILLNESS: spring 1887 paralysis attack of a leg; a year later memory loss, absentmindedness, sometimes untidy dress; June 1888 left without warning, found at Le Havre; circulatory accidents causing cries, tears, senseless words alternated with remissions; today doctors agree cerebral arteriosclerosis with uraemia. 12 Feb 1889 took his revolver to defend daughters from imaginary aggressors -> committed to Bon Sauveur, Caen, "l'asile de fous" as then said. Registered No. 14 449, reason: "Manifestations délirantes avec détention d'arme". Bon Sauveur: 215 nuns, up to 300 employees, eight quarters; Louis in "tranquilles et semi-tranquilles". 3 years 3 months. Called "vénérable vieillard", "beau patriarche"; refused private room; shared family treats; daily Mass and communion in good periods. 8 Jun 1889 signed renunciation of management of his property. 10 May 1892 back to Lisieux; 12 May last parlour visit with Carmelite daughters. Isidore rented house next to his own for Louis, Céline, Léonie and servants Marie and Désiré LeJuif; Désiré pushed wheelchair, fed him; they sang hymns; liked niece Marie Guérin's piano. Summers 1893 and 1894 at château de La Musse near Évreux (Guérin inheritance). May 1894 worse; last sacraments; early June heart attack; La Musse early July; 11 Jul fell from bed; 28 Jul Extreme Unction; died Sunday morning 29 Jul 1894. Funeral cathedral Saint-Pierre, Lisieux; buried town cemetery; 10 Oct 1894 Isidore reunited remains of Zélie, four children, grandmother Guérin, grandfather Martin [CONFLICT Laveille p95: "M. Guérin and Mme Martin"].
leonie page = conference by Anne-Marie Pelletier, 30 Sep 1999, Lisieux colloquium (facts only). Léonie b. 3 Jun 1863; Marie 1860, Pauline 1861; Hélène died at 5; Mélanie-Thérèse lived two months (1870). Family called her unstable, irritable, "la pauvre Léonie"; sent to Le Mans school several times, sent back; aunt: "terrible petite fille"; Zélie 7 Sep 1875: "une volonté de fer"; "Elle n'est pas du tout dévote". Entries: Poor Clares Alençon Jul 1886–Dec 1886; Visitation Caen Jul 1887–Jan 1888; Visitation Caen Jun 1893, habit Apr 1894, left Jan 1895; final entry 1899. At Les Buissonnets her room a narrow corridor between the two pairs of sisters. At Visitation: slow, clumsily meticulous, only auxiliary tasks. On her First Communion: "Il n'était pas ce jour, le plus beau de ma vie, parce que mon enfance et ma première jeunesse se sont passées dans la souffrance". 1937 Mgr Picaud's homily at blessing of the basilica forgot Léonie; she heard it on a borrowed radio. Pauline called her "tombée du nid". Léonie's letter to Carmel 19 Apr 1941: "Je suis trop petite pour me damner". Sr Marie-Dosithée believed Léonie would become a saint (Zélie 24 Jul 1874). At 10 wanted to be a Visitandine (29 Nov 1873).
46 editions page (Claude Langlois; facts only): HA composed mid-Oct 1898; first recipients 21 Oct 1898; financed by Isidore Guérin; 1,000 copies at Imprimerie Saint-Paul, Bar-le-Duc, sent to Carmels in place of the usual notice; second 1,000 soon; 475 pp + XXVIII intro. 1899 ed. 4,000 (6,000 total), "Paillettes d'or" appendix of novices' testimonies. Une rose effeuillée cheaper ed. 1907 ed. adds "Pluies de roses"; 1910 60,000th; Feb 1911 printing 30,000 (90,000th); 1912 125,000th, 675 pp the fullest. From 1914 the public learned of the three manuscripts (addressees identified). Last HA edition 1955; facsimile 1956; Manuscrits autobiographiques 1957.
