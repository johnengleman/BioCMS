- The date of Pauline's clothing (spring 1883; F-LF-140).
- The date of Louis's second attack (between mid-1888 and Jan 1889; LAV p.214).
- The Bon-Sauveur register wording "Manifestations délirantes avec détention d'arme" comes from the earlier agent's notes; I could not re-check the page.
- Whether the Martin sisters' "family life" rule (LAV p.261–262) was written or oral.
- Whether Thérèse ever went to La Musse: all sources say never; no letter says why.

## 5. Warnings for the writer

- Do not tell Zélie's tantrum and stubbornness stories from Laveille: use CF 147 and CF 159.
- Do not say Louis was in "a Home". The Carmel names the Bon-Sauveur at Caen, 12 Feb 1889 – 10 May 1892 (F-LF-198, F-LF-210).
- Do not state that the town blamed the daughters' vocations for the illness (F-LF-265).
- Every "Thérèse said" quotation from the 1898 text is the edited text (F-LF-241).
- Laveille wrote in 1925 with the Carmel's approval; his own comments (for example that the demon caused the 1883 illness) are comments, not evidence.
