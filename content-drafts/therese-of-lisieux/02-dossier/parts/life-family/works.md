# Works — life, family and historical context (life-family part)

What this part found about her works, in Laveille (1928), the Foundation book (1913) and the Carmel pages. The texts themselves, and the comparison of the manuscripts with the edition of 1898, belong to the "own writings" part of the dossier. Rights: Thérèse's own texts are public-domain in substance; the Carmel's transcriptions, notes and dating are "unknown" (facts and short quotations only). Locators: LAV p. = Laveille PDF page; F-LF = card in facts.md.

## A. The autobiographical manuscripts

### W-LF-01 — Manuscript A (childhood), written 1895–20 January 1896
- Date and occasion: ordered by Mère Agnès (prioress) after a recreation in December 1894 (F-LF-218); begun at the start of 1895 and written in rare free moments; handed to Mère Agnès on 20 Jan 1896 at evening prayer.
- Audience: Mère Agnès, for her feast day (the Carmel later made the addressee the prioress Marie de Gonzague: F-LF-241).
- Description: "written without erasure on poor paper" (LAV p.368); the first eight chapters of the printed *Histoire d'une âme* (LAV p.368). Mère Agnès did not read it for months.
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux (Carmel archives; the autographs were shown in the facsimile of 1956 and the critical editions of 1992: web-context [T3]).
- Reception: the community and the world's later readers (F-LF-242).
- Rights: public-domain (text); facsimile and edition apparatus: unknown.
- Source: LAV p.367–368; Carmel "fabrication" page https://archives.carmeldelisieux.fr/naissance-dune-sainte/lhistoire-du-ame/la-fabrication-de-lhistoire-dune-ame/

### W-LF-02 — Manuscript B (retreat of September 1896, "little doctrine", to Marie)
- Date and occasion: the pages written during her retreat of 1896 on her "little doctrine", addressed to Sr Marie du Sacré-Cœur, became chapter XI of the 1898 edition (LAV p.368).
- Audience: Marie (her sister and godmother).
- Description: contains the vocation passage ("My vocation is love", LAV p.353–354) and the image of the lift (LAV p.340).
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Source: LAV p.368, p.352–354; F-LF-241 (one addressee)

### W-LF-03 — Manuscript C (June–July 1897), to Marie de Gonzague
- Date and occasion: the Carmel says that on 30 May 1897 Thérèse told Mère Agnès of her first haemoptysis (April 1896); in the night of 2 Jun 1897 Mère Agnès asked Marie de Gonzague to have Thérèse write more, and from 3 or 4 Jun she wrote in a black notebook. Laveille: the pages on her life in Carmel (chapters IX and X of 1898) were written when Mère Agnès persuaded Marie de Gonzague, then prioress, to order them (LAV p.368).
- Audience: Mère Marie de Gonzague.
- Description: in summer 1897 she wrote often in the garden in the sun; novices kept interrupting and she said, "I am writing on fraternal charity. Now is the time to practise it" (LAV p.273).
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Source: Carmel "fabrication" page; LAV p.273, p.368

### W-LF-04 — *Histoire d'une âme* (the edition of 1898)
- Date and occasion: prepared Jun 1897 – spring 1898; imprimatur 8 Mar 1898 (Laveille); published 21 Oct 1898 (Carmel); two runs of 1,000 by the Œuvre de Saint-Paul, Bar-le-Duc; paid for by Isidore Guérin.
- Audience: first the Carmels of France and abroad (as a "circular"); then the public.
- Description: 12 chapters, one addressee, about fifty poems, four prayers, passages of plays, novices' memories, last words, an account of her death (F-LF-241).
- Reception: 46 editions to 1955; 395,200 copies in France to 1926 (Guise); 35–38 translations by 1925 (F-LF-242).
- Where now: printed volumes everywhere; manuscripts at Lisieux.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Source: F-LF-241, F-LF-242

## C. Poems, plays and prayers

### W-LF-09 — Poems named in my sources
- "Vivre d'amour": composed whole at once during her hour of adoration (Summarium 1919 p.599 §1732; LAV p.243 fn.). Quoted stanza at LAV p.263.
- "Jésus, rappelle-toi": a summary of the mysteries of Christ's life (LAV p.244).
- "Pourquoi je t'aime, ô Marie" ("Why I love thee, Mary"): composed when very ill, at her wish to say in a hymn all she thought of Mary (LAV p.270–271).
- "Ce que j'ai aimé" ("What I have loved"): written in Carmel on the Chateaubriand model; recalls Les Buissonnets (LAV p.148).
- "My Arms" and "My Song of To-day" (LAV p.264, p.328).
- The couplet "To die of love" for the first Mass of 16 Jul 1897, sung by Marie Guérin (LAV p.382).
- Method: composed mentally while polishing floors or arranging chasubles, written down in the evening on loose slips or old envelopes, and given away without a copy (LAV p.243–244, p.311). The first poems were written at superiors' request for professions, clothings and feasts, set to known airs; Pauline corrected only "a few mistakes in prosody".
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux; published in the *Poésies*. About fifty poems are in HA 1898.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Source: LAV as cited.

### W-LF-10 — The plays (récréations pieuses)
- "Jeanne d'Arc" (RP 3), performed 21 Jan 1895; Céline's photographs of Thérèse in costume, 21 Jan–25 Mar 1895 (F-LF-220).
- "Le Triomphe de l'humilité" (RP 7), dated 21 Jun 1896, for the prioress's feast; played 20–21 Jun 1896; tunes named: "Les menaces maternelles", "Arrête ! Le Cœur de Jésus est là", "Quand l'étranger", "La patrie des hirondelles" (F-LF-225).
- St Agnes: Sr Agnès wrote on 17 Jun 1888 that Thérèse would play St Agnes on 21 Jun 1888, the prioress's feast, and asked for a large dish of cream (Carmel letter, family-letters notes).
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux; photos in the Carmel photo archive.
- Rights: public-domain (texts); photos: unknown.
- Source: F-LF-220, F-LF-225

### W-LF-11 — Act of Oblation to Merciful Love, 9 June 1895
- Date: 9 Jun 1895 (F-LF-221). Text printed at LAV p.356–358. Lemonnier changed "Infinite" to "Immense". It was found after her death in a Gospel book she carried near her heart. She signed it "Marie-Françoise-Thérèse de l'Enfant Jésus et de la Sainte Face, Rel. Carm. Ind."
- Rights: public-domain.
- Source: LAV p.355–358

### W-LF-12 — The mock wedding announcement, October 1890
- Description: the invitation (text at LAV p.233 fn.), written after Jeanne Guérin's wedding of 1 Oct 1890 (F-LF-205).
- Rights: public-domain.

### W-LF-13 — Prayers and notes
- Prayer for the eyes lowered in refectory, "Thy two little spouses" (LAV p.289–290); the profession prayer on her heart (LAV p.226); "O little Infant Jesus, my only treasure…" (LAV p.231); the three resolutions of First Communion (LAV p.137); Pauline's First Communion notebook: 818 sacrifices and 2,774 acts of love in three months (LAV p.134); the copied passages of Théophane Vénard's last letters, Sep 1897 (LAV p.390); notes to novices (LAV p.376).
- Rights: public-domain.

## D. Paintings and objects made by her

### W-LF-14 — The fresco round the tabernacle of the oratory, 1892
- Date: during two months without an office from the end of June 1892 (her account to Mère Agnès, 13 Jul 1897); "cherubim" (LAV p.243).
- Also: minute paintings on church ornaments and vellum leaflets; paintings on silk, miniatures and pious emblems for the sacristy and sisters (LAV p.243, p.376); the last painting, for a difficult sister, a few months before death (LAV p.280).
- Where now: the oratory of the Lisieux Carmel (the fresco, "still admired" in 1928).
- Rights: unknown (photographs).
- Source: LAV p.239 fn., p.243, p.280, p.376

## E. Works of the family (context)

### W-LF-15 — Zélie's letters
- About 218 letters survive from Zélie (CF 1–217, from 1863 to 16 Aug 1877; the last is CF 217). Laveille printed extracts of about 130 (LAV p.18 and passim, per sources.md). The Carmel prints them in full with editor's notes.
- Where now: Carmel of Lisieux archive; transcriptions at https://archives.carmeldelisieux.fr/correspondance/ .
- Rights: public-domain (letters); transcriptions unknown.
- Source: sources.md; cf-url-map.md

### W-LF-16 — Point d'Alençon lace
- Zélie's business, begun 1853 (some sources 1863), (F-LF-016 to F-LF-018). At her clothing on 10 Jan 1889, Thérèse wore a dress trimmed with point d'Alençon lace (F-LF-270).

### W-LF-17 — Louis's writings
- Letters to his family and friends (Nogrix 1883, 1888; 1885 travel letters; 1877 letters), and the fragment on death and Providence, with a prayer borrowed from Mère Barat (F-LF-127).

### W-LF-18 — Céline's, Marie's, Pauline's and Léonie's works (as far as found)
- Céline: autobiography of 1909 (Carmel page); photographs of Thérèse (F-LF-220); paintings (the Holy Face after the Shroud of Turin, LAV p.232 fn.; a painting of the Virgin and Mary Magdalene of 1893, F-LF-211).
- Marie: notes prepared for her deposition (Carmel page, F-LF-128).
- Pauline (Mère Agnès): miniatures and verse (LAV p.242); notes taken in the last illness (Novissima Verba, F-LF-231); letters and notebooks for the Process.
- Léonie: recollections on the Carmel site (not read).
- Rights: unknown.

### W-LF-19 — Isidore Guérin's articles in *Le Normand*, 1891–1896
- See F-LF-245. Not read.
- Rights: unknown.

### W-LF-20 — *Pluie de roses* and Laveille's *Life*
- *Pluie de roses* ("Shower of Roses"): seven volumes in fifteen years, about 3,000 pages, first facts of 1902 (F-LF-246). Laveille, *St Thérèse de l'Enfant Jésus 1873–1897* (French 1925; English 1928), the local source of this part.
- Rights: public-domain (Laveille, US).
