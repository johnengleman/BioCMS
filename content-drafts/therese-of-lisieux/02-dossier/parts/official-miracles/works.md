# Works — from the official / after-death family (prefix W-OM)

Scope: this file lists her writings and other works only as they appear in the official documents, the cause, and the after-death sources. Full descriptions of the manuscripts, letters and poems belong to the "own writings" family. Locators use the short names in `sources.md`. Where a source does not say where an object is kept now, the entry says so. Rights labels follow the stage prompt.

## A. Her book

### W-OM-001 — *Histoire d'une âme* (Story of a Soul): the three manuscripts and the 1898 book
- Date and occasion: written in Carmel on the order of the prioress (see F-OM-009 and F-OM-018); printed in Oct 1898 by the Carmel, and sent to all Carmels as the usual death notice. Mère Agnès urged the printing; Père Godefroy Madelaine (Premonstratensian prior of Mondaye) revised the text and took it to Bishop Hugonin for the imprimatur. Taylor adds that "a first attempt failed" before it was obtained. The Carmel says 1,000 copies were printed on 21 Oct 1898, paid by Isidore Guérin.
- Audience and reception: postulants came because of it (F-OM-019). Pius XI's brief (AAS 15 p. 205) says Pius X held that in the book "the virtues shine as an example and it almost breathes the soul" of the Lisieux virgin (F-OM-011). The 1921 decree said her fame far exceeded her humble person (F-OM-043).
- Numbers: 410,000 complete copies and 2 million abridged copies, 35 translations, 1898–1925 (Laveille PDF 415; F-OM-086). *Divini amoris scientia* §3: about 50 languages by 1997.
- Where the text is now: the documents record that the writings were examined in the 1910 process on her writings and approved by the Congregation on 10 Dec 1912 (F-OM-026, F-OM-030). The present location of the manuscripts is not given in this family.
- Source: Laveille PDF 402; Taylor 1926/30 PDF 39–42, 443 note; Carmel "Historique"; *Divini amoris scientia* §3.
- Rights: public-domain (Laveille, Taylor); facts-only (1997 letter).
- Cards: F-OM-011, 018, 019, 026, 030, 043, 086.

### W-OM-002 — English editions of the book, 1901–1927
- What: Dziewicki's English version (1901); Taylor's abridgement *As Little Children* (Aug 1911; 175th thousand by March 1927); Taylor's complete *Sœur Thérèse of Lisieux* (Christmas 1912; first printing 9,000; 45,000 by 1924); Taylor's revised translation of 1926–27 with the story of the canonization.
- Also: nearly two million leaflets printed at the Orphans' Press, Rochdale.
- Source: Taylor 1926/30 PDF 39–40; Taylor 1924 PDF 10.
- Rights: public-domain (1924 printing); the 1926/30 scan carries a 1936 imprimatur, so its rights are **unknown** until the 1930 (or 1926–27) first publication is confirmed. Facts here rest on the 1924 printing where possible.
- Card: F-OM-086.

### W-OM-003 — The count of her writings (1997)
- What: *Divini amoris scientia* §3 counts three manuscripts, 266 letters, 54 poems, 8 plays and 21 prayers.
- Source: JP II, *Divini amoris scientia* §3, https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/apost_letters/1997/documents/hf_jp-ii_apl_19101997_divini-amoris.html
- Rights: facts-only.
- Card: F-OM-098.

## B. Prayers and short texts

### W-OM-004 — The Act of Oblation to Merciful Love (9 June 1895)
- Date: 9 June 1895. The bull says she offered herself as a victim to Merciful Love two years before death (F-OM-013). Taylor's list of "days of grace" gives "My Oblation — June 9, 1895" (W-OM-007).
- What: a prayer of offering, "Offering of Myself as a Victim to God's Merciful Love". It asks God to be her holiness. It says she will stand before God "with empty hands". It asks for no throne and no crown but God. It ends with the offering of herself as a holocaust.
- Where it was found: Taylor says it was found after her death "in the copy of the Gospels which she carried night and day close to her heart".
- Text: Taylor 1926/30 PDF 479–480 (book pp. 447–448), full English text. The English is Taylor's, so we must make our own translation from the French before quoting.
- Rights: public-domain (translation), original French public-domain.
- Card: F-OM-013.
- Notes: The bull says "as it is reported" she was wounded by a flame of heavenly fire (« uti fertur »). The Office says "as she herself relates". The Oblation text itself does not contain this claim.

### W-OM-005 — Prayer to the Holy Face, indulgenced by Pius X
- What: a prayer to the Holy Face composed by her. Taylor says Pius X granted an indulgence of 300 days, *toties quoties*, for reciting it. The prayer is printed at Taylor 1924 book p. 417.
- Related object: Céline (Sister Geneviève) spent six months copying the photograph of the Holy Shroud of Turin into a picture of the Holy Face. Pius X attached indulgences to it and wished it to have a place "in every Catholic household" (Taylor's wording).
- Source: Taylor 1924 PDF 308 (book p. 270) and note.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Notes: The dates of the indulgence and of the papal wish are not given in the note. Taylor calls the picture "the most exact likeness of Our Lord" only if the Shroud is authentic; we should not repeat that claim.
- Cards: none direct; see F-OM-114 (the 1917 ribbon) for a related object.

### W-OM-006 — The poem "To live by Love" (« Vivre d'Amour »)
- What: one of her poems. A Polish Carmelite, Fr Raphael of St Joseph (Kalinowski), the Vicar Provincial, wrote to the Carmel that in a night of interior trouble he opened her Life and found this poem, and "the storm ceased" (miracle entry M-OM-051). He had earlier said that Polish could not carry the style of the book.
- Source: Taylor 1924 PDF 393–394 (book pp. 351–352). Taylor's note says Kalinowski "died in the odour of sanctity in 1907" and that his cause had been submitted.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Notes: The poem's full text is not in this family. The letter's own year is 1902 by our check of the page image (see M-OM-051).

### W-OM-007 — Her list "Days of grace granted by the Lord to His little spouse"
- What: a list of anniversaries found in the manuscript of the autobiography: birth 2 Jan 1873; baptism 4 Jan 1873; the Smile of Our Lady 10 May 1883; First Communion 8 May 1884; Confirmation 14 Jun 1884; Conversion 25 Dec 1886; audience with Leo XIII 20 Nov 1887; entry into Carmel "April 9?" 1888; clothing 10 Jan 1889; "Our Great Treasure" 12 Feb 1889 (Taylor's note: her father's illness); profession 8 Sep 1890; taking of the veil 24 Sep 1890; her Oblation 9 Jun 1895.
- Taylor then adds his own table of after-death dates: heaven 30 Sep 1897; cause begun (writings) 10 Feb 1910; interrogation of "Pauline" 12 Aug 1910; first exhumation 6 Sep 1910; cause introduced 10 Jun 1914; second exhumation 9 Aug 1917; Venerable 14 Aug 1921; translation of relics 26 Mar 1923; beatified 29 Apr 1923; canonized 17 May 1925.
- Source: Taylor 1926/30 PDF 318 (book p. 288). Scan text checked on the PDF page.
- Rights: public-domain (list) — see rights caveat on this scan under W-OM-002.
- Notes: Taylor's entry date has a query mark. The Carmel and other sources give 9 Apr 1888. The list is her own choice of days, so it shows what she counted as her "graces".

## C. Objects she made or owned

### W-OM-008 — The pall painted for Fr Roulland
- What: a pall painted by her for her missionary "brother" Fr Adolphe Roulland. On it a dove, which stands for her, sits on the last rock of a headland into the ocean; a white-sailed boat in the distance carries him to China.
- Where it is: in 1927 Taylor says Roulland "possesses" it (Paris, Procurator of the Society, rue du Bac seminary). Present location not stated.
- Source: Taylor 1926/30 PDF 452, editor's note (book p. 420).
- Rights: public-domain.
- Notes: Other dossier parts should hold the Roulland correspondence.

### W-OM-009 — Relic objects kept in the Carmel sacristy (1920s)
- What: her long tresses of hair; her habit; her First Communion dress; her instruments of penance; the palm found in her coffin in 1910; other things. Kept behind a wrought-iron screen in the sacristy.
- Source: Taylor 1926/30 PDF 463, from Cardinal Dougherty's account.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Card: F-OM-103.

### W-OM-010 — The words on the wooden cross over her grave (1897)
- What: « Je veux passer mon ciel à faire du bien sur la terre », painted on the wooden cross a few days after 4 Oct 1897. Later this saying was carried on the scroll of the picture in the beatification "Gloria" (F-OM-058).
- Source: Laveille PDF 400.
- Rights: public-domain.
- Card: F-OM-016.

## E. The great collection of favours

### W-OM-026 — *Pluie de roses* (Shower of Roses), 1907–1926
- What: ten volumes of favours and healings attributed to her: seven chronological, two thematic, one anthology; over 3,200 accounts. First printing (early 1910), 8,000 copies. Rome stopped publication in 1914 when the cause was introduced; it resumed in 1920.
- Source: Carmel "Pluie de roses"; Laveille PDF 414; Taylor 1926/30 PDF 273.
- Rights: unknown (facts only).
- Cards: F-OM-020, F-OM-021. Miracle list: `miracles.md`.
- Not read directly: see `sources.md`.

### W-OM-027 — Taylor's own "Shower of Roses" chapters (1912, 1924, 1926/27)
- What: translated and selected letters and certificates, in four "bouquets", and later "Conquest of America". These are the main English source for the accounts in `miracles.md`.
- Source: Taylor 1924 PDF 369–450; Taylor 1926/30 PDF 415–476.
- Rights: public-domain (1924); unknown for the 1926/30 scan.

### W-OM-029 — The Carmel chapel and the reliquary figure
- The relics were brought to the Carmel chapel on 26 Mar 1923; twin altars and "rose" windows by 1925; a golden rose placed in the hand of the recumbent figure on 30 Sep 1925.
- Source: Carmel "Retour de Thérèse au Carmel"; Taylor 1926/30 PDF 26–27; Laveille PDF 444–446. Cards: F-OM-055, 056, 081, 082.
- Rights: public-domain (Taylor, Laveille); unknown (Carmel).

## H. Added objects (second pass)

### W-OM-030 — The ribbon with her saying (1910–1917)
- What: a white silk ribbon with gold letters, « Je veux passer mon ciel à faire du bien sur la terre. Après ma mort, je ferai tomber une pluie de roses », found in the coffin on 10 Aug 1917. Source: Carmel exhumation page. Rights: unknown. Cards: F-OM-114, M-OM-103. It is not her writing; it is a later inscription of her words.

### W-OM-031 — The 1915 charcoal drawings of Thérèse at the front
- What: works by Charles Jouvenot and Pierre Léon Adolphe Annould, commissioned by the Carmel. Jouvenot also drew soldiers' stories, kept as colour glass plates. Where: Carmel archive. Rights: unknown. Card: F-OM-122.

### W-OM-032 — The reliquary casket of 1917
- What: carved oak casket (1.2 × 0.4 × 0.3 m), white satin lining, Holy Face on the lid; lead coffin; rosewood sarcophagus from the firm Borniol. Card: F-OM-114.

### W-OM-033 — Documents about her put to the apostolic process
- "Voie d'enfance spirituelle" by Sr Isabelle du Sacré-Cœur; "Dans quel milieu sœur Thérèse de l'Enfant-Jésus s'est sanctifiée". Card: F-OM-112.
