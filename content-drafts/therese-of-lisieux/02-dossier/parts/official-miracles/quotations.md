# Quotations — from this source family (her words as reported; plus official wording)

## Her own words (as reported)
1. « Je veux passer mon ciel à faire du bien sur la terre » — "I want to spend my heaven doing good on earth." Painted on the wooden cross over her grave a few days after 4 Oct 1897. Laveille PDF 400. public-domain.
2. "How touching is the zeal of this postulator! How glad one would be to have miracles to make known to him!" — after de Teil's talk on the Compiègne martyrs in the Lisieux parlour (her lifetime). Laveille PDF 413 note (Eng. tr. 1928). public-domain. Shows her humour.
3. "Oh! … I love Him! … My God, I … love … Thee!" — last words, 30 Sep 1897, 7.15 pm. Carmel account in Taylor 1926/30 PDF 266; Laveille PDF 398. Latin in the bull: « Deus meus, Te diligo » (AAS 17 p. 339). public-domain.
4. "Very well … be it so … Ah! I do not wish to suffer less." — told the agony might last hours. Taylor 1926/30 PDF 266. public-domain.
5. "I would never have believed that it was possible to suffer so much, never, never! I cannot explain it except by the ardent desire I have had of saving souls." — 30 Sep 1897. Laveille PDF 397. public-domain.
6. "Yes, I have never sought anything but the truth. Yes, I have understood humility of heart." / "I do not repent of having surrendered myself to love." — 30 Sep 1897, afternoon. Laveille PDF 397; Taylor PDF 265. public-domain.
7. "Oh, I have prayed to her so fervently; but it is pure agony without any measure of consolation." — looking at the statue of the Virgin, morning of 30 Sep 1897. Laveille PDF 396. public-domain.
8. "My good … that, without doubt, is to suffer." — to Mère Agnès, 24 Sep 1897, when a Mass brought no relief. Laveille PDF 395. public-domain.
9. « Ah ! Je le sais bien ; tout le monde m'aimera ! » — "Ah! I know well that everyone will love me!" — on her deathbed. Taylor 1926/30 PDF 273 and note. public-domain.
10. "I feel that my mission is about to begin, my mission to make the good God loved as I love Him." Laveille PDF 401. public-domain.
11. "Little souls must be able to do everything I do." Taylor 1926/30 PDF 271. public-domain.
12. "The death of love I desire is that of Jesus upon the Cross." Taylor 1926/30 PDF 266. public-domain.
13. To Maurice Bellière, Feb 1897: "if Jesus realises my presentiments I promise to remain your little sister in Heaven … There will be no enclosure then, no grating, and my soul will be able to fly to you in the mission-field afar." Taylor 1926/30 PDF 451. public-domain.
14. Prayer ending her book, as Pius XI made it his own (Latin): « Supplices tibi sumus, o bone Iesu, ut magnum parvarum animarum numerum respicias, itemque parvarum legionem victimarum in terris deligas, quae tua dignae sint caritate. » AAS 17 p. 214. public-domain.

## Words attributed to her after death (label as such)
- « ma voie est sûre, et je ne me suis pas trompée en la suivant » — "My way is sure, and I am not mistaken in following it." Gallipoli, 16 Jan 1910. Carmel; Taylor 1924 PDF 377.
- "You must make me known everywhere. I desire to do so much good." 20 Jul 1910. Taylor 1924 PDF 382.
- "Be generous; you will soon be cured; I promise it." To Sœur Louise, 10 Sep 1916. Laveille PDF 426.

## Official wording (our translation)
- « de puella enim agitur, quae brevem vitam in claustro absconditam egit, paucis cognita, donec vixit » — "for it concerns a girl who lived a short life hidden in the cloister, known to few while she lived." Decree on virtues, AAS 13 p. 449.
- « Ancillae Dei decumbentis lectulus religiosae familiae universae sanctitatis cathedra fuit » — "her sickbed was a chair of holiness for the whole community." Brief *Quod Ioannes*, AAS 15 p. 205.
- « "Religionis magistra" seu "parvus Doctor" » — the school chaplain's names for her. Bull, AAS 17 p. 338.
- « pergatque pluviam rosarum e caelo in homines demittere » — "and she continues to send down a rain of roses from heaven upon men." Pius XI, 14 May 1923, AAS 15 p. 283.
- « alia non occurrit Causa, quae tam celeri tamque prospero actorum cursu supremam conscenderit Canonizationis metam » — "no other cause is found that reached canonization by so swift and happy a course." AAS 17 p. 200.
- « se nihil Deo aliud, nisi amorem, dedisse » — "that she had given God nothing but love." Canonization homily, AAS 17 p. 213.
- « ut etiam super nos … rosarum, videlicet gratiarum quibus indigemus, pluvia descendat » — "that on us too a rain of roses, that is, of the graces we need, may fall." Bull, AAS 17 p. 346.
- Benedict XV (Taylor's English, 1921): "There lies the secret of sanctity." Pius XI (Taylor's English, 11 Feb 1923): "our beloved guiding-star … miracle of virtues and prodigy of miracles."
