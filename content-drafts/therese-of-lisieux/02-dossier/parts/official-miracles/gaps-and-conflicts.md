# Gaps and conflicts — official documents, miracles, after death

## Conflicts (card numbers in facts.md / miracles.md)
1. Introduction of the cause: 9 Jun 1914 (bull AAS 17 p. 340; Taylor 1924) v. 10 Jun 1914 (Laveille, Taylor 1926/30, Carmel). F-OM-033/034. Unresolved.
2. General congregation on virtues: 2 Aug 1921 (AAS 13 pp. 451, 453; bull) v. 10 Aug (Taylor). Official date clear: 2 Aug. F-OM-040.
3. General congregation on canonization miracles: 17 Mar 1925 (decree AAS 17 p. 149; Diarium p. 162; Taylor) v. 7 Mar (bull p. 344, image checked). Evidence favours 17 Mar. F-OM-066.
4. Resumption of the cause: Diarium "Tuesday 23 July 1923" (23 Jul was a Monday) v. bull 27 Jul 1923. F-OM-064.
5. Feast: canonization formula "30 September" (AAS 17 p. 211) v. bull "3 October" (p. 346). Resolved by Martyrology entries (AAS 20 p. 152): death-day 30 Sep, feast 3 Oct. F-OM-071/079/088.
6. Translation of relics: 26 Mar 1923 (Laveille, Carmel, Taylor table) v. "March 25, Feast of the Annunciation" (Taylor narrative). 26 Mar is right (Monday of Holy Week). F-OM-055.
7. Second exhumation: 9 Aug (Taylor table) v. 10 Aug (Taylor narrative) v. 9–10 Aug (Laveille, Carmel). F-OM-039.
8. Sœur Louise's cure: woke cured 22 Sep 1916 (Laveille, Carmel) v. 25 Sep (Taylor). M-OM-002.
9. Mrs Dorans: 27 Aug 1909 (Taylor 1924 with certificates) v. 27 Aug 1910 (Taylor 1926/30). 1909 right. M-OM-020.
10. Augouard's cure: c. 1910 (letter of 1912, "two years ago") v. 1909 (Taylor 1926/30). M-OM-023.
11. Canonization numbers: cardinals 33 / 34 / 23; bishops ~200 / >200 / 250; faithful 50,000 inside (Laveille, Carmel) v. "60,000 faithful, 4,000 priests" (Laveille preface). F-OM-073.
12. Priests at the translation: >200 (Laveille, Taylor) v. nearly 300 (Carmel).
13. Lisieux feasts 1923: Laveille 28–30 May and 6–8 Aug; Taylor "September 1923". F-OM-061.
14. Time of death: 7.15 pm (Carmel via Taylor) v. about 7.20 pm (Laveille). F-OM-014.
15. *Pluie de roses*: 10 volumes, 3,200+ accounts (Carmel) v. 7 volumes, 3,000 pages (Laveille 1925; Taylor 1927) — explained by date of writing. F-OM-021.
16. Apostolic process: Carmel "Historique" 17 Mar 1915 – 30 Oct 1917 (Taylor: 91st session 30 Oct 1917) v. plan §9 "58 sessions, closed 25 Aug 1916". Settled in part by the Carmel's process pages: 58 sessions to 25 Aug 1916, then sessions 80–82 (9–11 Aug 1917) and 91 (30 Oct 1917), so two phases. The overlap with the 1 Apr 1916 "continuative" date remains. F-OM-036, F-OM-109.
17. Religious name: the 1923 brief links "a Puero Iesu" to her profession (1890); other sources give it earlier. F-OM-009.
18. Pellemans cure date: "March 1923" (bull) v. "March 24" (Taylor). Compatible.
19. Taylor's "first daughter of St Teresa canonized" ignores St Mary Magdalene de' Pazzi (1669, Carmelite of the older observance). F-OM-075.
20. Coffin in 1923: "mahogany with silver handles" (Carmel page, as summarized by the fetch tool) v. oak chest in rosewood coffin lined with lead (Taylor, 1917). Recheck the Carmel page wording.
