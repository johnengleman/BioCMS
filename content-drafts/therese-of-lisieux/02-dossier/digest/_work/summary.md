# P6 digest — 1897, her last year (Thérèse of Lisieux)

Source: `_by-period/P6.md` (807 cards). Every card ID appears in at least one event below. Events run in date order; events with the same date are grouped by time of day. Undated 1897 material is placed at the start or end of its month ("July, undated") and marked in the heading.

## Period summary

### Counts
- 807 cards read, merged into 418 events (E-P6-001 to E-P6-418).
- Many events merge cards from several witnesses: Mère Agnès (Process 1910, 1915, and her Carnet jaune), Céline, Marie du Sacré-Cœur, Marie de la Trinité (MT), Marie de l'Eucharistie (MdA), Thérèse de Saint-Augustin (TSA), Marie-Madeleine, Marthe, Aimée, Léonie, Jeanne Guérin, Godefroy, Maupas and Faucon, plus her own letters and poems and Laveille and Piat.

### Turning points
1. Hidden decline, Jan to May 1897. Letters state she expects a short life (E-P6-002, E-P6-011, E-P6-012, E-P6-021). Fever note 3 Apr (E-P6-016), cautery and blisters (E-P6-014), the Yellow Notebook begins 6 Apr (E-P6-017), Easter (E-P6-019), relieved of all offices (E-P6-031).
2. 2 to 4 June: Pauline's midnight visit and the order to write Manuscript C (E-P6-052, E-P6-055); Ms C opens (E-P6-059); it breaks off in pencil about 8 to 11 July (E-P6-155).
3. 4 to 9 June: farewell to her sisters, the white hen, the "shower of roses" (E-P6-066, E-P6-069), her "farewell" letter to Bellière (E-P6-071).
4. 8 July: carried to the infirmary (E-P6-138). Daily blood-spitting 6 July to 5 Aug.
5. 30 July: Extreme Unction and Viaticum (E-P6-226). "Heaven doing good on earth" is dated 17 July (E-P6-180).
6. 19 August: last Communion (E-P6-314). Month without a doctor and the refusal of morphine (E-P6-308, E-P6-356).
7. 29 to 30 September: the death (E-P6-383, E-P6-386 to E-P6-392).
8. 1 to 4 October: the body at the grille, the burial (E-P6-398, E-P6-401). The sandals burned (E-P6-394), the critic cured (E-P6-395).
9. After death: the community changes (E-P6-408), the manuscript is edited and printed (E-P6-406, E-P6-416).

### The five best scenes
1. The death sequence, 30 Sept: agony, "I would not want to suffer less", "I love Him", the gaze "for the space of a Credo" and the candle held to her eyes (E-P6-389, E-P6-390, E-P6-391).
2. The chestnut walk: a feverish Thérèse leaves her carriage to comfort a weeping novice on a tree stump (E-P6-124).
3. The kitchen remark and the meat juice: "she has never done anything worth speaking about"; "not even a good nun", and her joy (E-P6-085, E-P6-234).
4. The comic-sad infirmary scenes: buried under blankets by the deaf infirmarian (E-P6-088), "I don't know the trade" when she looks too cheerful to be anointed (E-P6-148).
5. The alpargates (patched rope sandals) thrown on the fire after her death (E-P6-394), with the lay Sister who scolded her cured at her feet (E-P6-395).
Runners-up: the stairs climbed in half an hour (E-P6-060); the broken pane (E-P6-007); the robin and turtle-dove (E-P6-381); the last photograph (30 Aug); "I tell the whole truth".

### The five best "new to English readers" facts
1. Her own words "if there is a Heaven, it is for me" (LT 216), a phrase absent from the 1911 edition, and later scratched out in the original (E-P6-002).
2. "If I had not had faith, I would have killed myself" (22 Sept, Carnet jaune), absent from Novissima Verba (E-P6-370). Sensitive: quote with its context.
3. The 1911 edition splits and redates her farewell letters to Bellière, so that "I am not dying, I am entering life" (written 9 June) appears as a saying of 14 Aug (E-P6-165); and Mère Agnès scratched out a saying of 16 July (E-P6-175).
4. The medical record: a month without a doctor because the prioress refused Dr La Néele; morphine syrup given by stratagem and injections refused (E-P6-308, E-P6-356); conflicting witnesses on who was responsible.
5. The Sister who was to be cured at her feet had said she was "not even a good religious" and had brought her the meat juice; Thérèse was glad of it; the lobster letter to Roulland (E-P6-011) and the TSA "black door" dream with her darkest words (E-P6-001) are also new.

### People who matter in this period
- Mère Agnès (Pauline): nurse, recorder of the Yellow Notebook, first editor; Marie du Sacré-Cœur, Céline (Sr Geneviève, assistant infirmarian); Léonie.
- Mère Marie de Gonzague (prioress): daily communion, refusal of a doctor and of morphine, the printed circular, changed after 1897.
- Novices: Marie de la Trinité, Marthe, Marie de l'Eucharistie (Marie Guérin), TSA; the Sisters Saint-Vincent de Paul, Saint-Jean-Baptiste, Saint-Stanislas (old first infirmarian), Hermance.
- Doctors: de Cornière (community doctor), La Néele (cousin by marriage). Priests: Maupas (superior), Youf (chaplain, died 15 days later), Faucon, Godefroy Madelaine (Mondaye).
- Missionary brothers: Roulland, Bellière. The Guérin family (Isidore, Céline, Jeanne, Marie). Théophane Vénard (picture, relic, poem).

### Open conflicts
- Time of the agony (3 pm / 4:30 pm / about 5 pm) and of death (7:15 / about 7 / 7:20 / 7:30); whether she spoke after 5 pm (Marie) or not; who held the candle at the gaze; whether anyone watched the last night.
- Who withheld relief: the prioress (Marie, Céline, TSA, Marie du Sacré-Cœur) or the doctor (Marie des Anges, MdA); the number of times La Néele was allowed in.
- Date and wording of many sayings: the arena and the "little saint" (July or August), the flies (30 July or 26 Sept), the devil scene (16 Aug, late Sept or 28 Sept), the petals (six wordings), the sacraments (who gave Extreme Unction), the rose saying (7 June or 9 June), the painting request (25 Apr or 28 May), Easter (18 or 25 Apr), the hymn place (oratory or garden hermitage).
- Source criticism: the Yellow Notebook was recopied in 1921 to 1923 from a destroyed 1904 to 1905 notebook (E-P6-017); Pauline's "strategic" sayings (permission to edit, the manuscript, the "mission") are the most open to reworking; MT's testimony recites her own 1897 notes.
- Publication: date (30 Sept, 21 Oct or "October 1898"), imprimatur 7 or 8 March, and the claim that Gonzague wanted to destroy the autographs (E-P6-416).
