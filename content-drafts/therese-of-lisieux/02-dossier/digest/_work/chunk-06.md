## publication-history | 1898-03-07 | x | x
- What happened: More detail from the Carmel archive and Laveille. A dead Carmelite normally produced a short circular (about three pages) for other Carmels. Agnès saw the illness was far advanced when Thérèse told her on 30 May 1897 of the first haemoptysis of April 1896. Marie de Gonzague wrote a preface near the end of 1897. On 29 October 1897 Gonzague wrote to Godefroy that Thérèse left "delightful pages" and asked him to correct them (see gonzague-letter-godefroy).
