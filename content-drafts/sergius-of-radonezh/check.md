# Check: Sergius of Radonezh (tier B)

Checked against the raw azbyka.ru Russian text of the Life (fetched with fetch_text.py, chapters 2-33 read), the raw ru.wikipedia text, and the raw archive.org OCR of Rogovich 1922. Originals are kept as `*.before-check` (biography.html, summary.txt, entry.json). Fixed in place: biography.html, entry.json (biography, miracles, teachings, relic_description, share_kit, quote source, sources, gaps). summary.txt needed no change.

## High-risk items, confirmed

- Blessing of Dmitri. Confirmed from ru.wikipedia: the earliest text has Sergius say "if God helps you, you will win and return unharmed"; the Pachomius text (azbyka ch.22) says "with God's help you will win and return unharmed with great honor"; Dimitry of Rostov makes it a firm prophecy. The Life has no Kulikovo, no year, and no Peresvet or Oslyabya (grep of the whole azbyka text: zero hits; "Kulikovo" appears only in a modern editor's note). The Tale of the Mamai Rout and Kuchkin's 1378 Vozha view are attributed. Kept as written, with the fixes below.
- Birth year 1314 or 1322. Confirmed against the ru.wikipedia table (Borisov 1314; Kuchkin, Kloss, Averyanov 1322; Pachomius line 78 years and tonsure at 23; Epiphanius line about 70 and tonsure at 20). birth_year stays null.
- Feast dates. 25 Sept (8 Oct civil) repose and 5 July (18 July civil) relics both confirmed in ru.wikipedia. Relics date 5 July 1422 confirmed.
- 1919-1946 history. Confirmed against ru.wikipedia: decree 1 Feb 1919, opening 11 April 1919, Kronid among signers, Kuleshov filmed, Solikamsk 25 July 1941 to 19 Nov 1944, Holy Saturday 20 April 1946 (Pascha 21 April), move from Trinity to Dormition cathedral. The Florensky and Olsufiev head story is church tradition.
- Rogovich passages, confirmed in the archive.org OCR. (1) Letter: "безъ всякаго сомнѣнія, Господине, со дерзновеніемъ пойди противъ свирѣпства ихъ" (the book says a messenger brought it to the camp with a prosphora and a letter). (2) Klyuchevsky: "иди на безбожниковъ смѣло, безъ колебанія, и побѣдишь". Both English renderings are fair. Rogovich itself says Alexis offered a gold pectoral cross and Sergius said "от юности моей я не былъ златоносцемъ", which supports the Alexis quote.
- Quote source fields: longest is 220 characters (limit 255). Related arrays: miracles 1, teachings 1, quotes 3 (limit 50).

## Fixes (location, reason)

1. Biography, "A Letter on the Road": the vision. The Life says Sergius saw the battle from afar, and only later, after the enemy was beaten, named the fallen and prayed. The draft said he named them while they fell. Fixed. Also removed the claim that the vision is a "later layer" (no source read says so); the Note now says the sources do not say.
2. Biography, same section: block-quote cite said "from the Life". Rogovich gives the letter in his own account; cite now says "Sergius's letter to Grand Prince Dmitri, as quoted in Rogovich". entry.json quote source changed the same way (no "Life, ch. 22").
3. Biography, same section: "That is how the Life tells it" replaces "oldest form of the story" (the paragraph includes the vision). "In every version the story keeps the same picture" was an absolute claim and invented a patched robe at the scene. Rewritten to "In the Life itself...". "Some scholars" changed to Kuchkin by name, with the later-linkage point added. Dmitri's gift: the Life says a rich gift to the monastery (not to Sergius), and Dmitri reminds Sergius of his vow.
4. Biography, "Moldy Bread and Fresh Loaves": the draft said no one ever learned who sent the bread. The Life says the drivers named a wealthy Christ-loving man far away, so the brothers did not learn his name. Also: Sergius ate nothing for three days; he asked Daniil for spoiled bread as pay; one monk spoke for all and threatened to leave next day; the bread was warm. Fixed here and in miracle m08 and teachings.
5. Biography, "Do Not Call It My Spring": the draft said the father could not keep quiet. The Life says he did not dare spread it, could not stop praising God, and the story came out through a disciple. Sergius also warned it would harm him. Fixed here and in miracle m10.
6. Biography, "The Boy Who Could Not Read": "his teacher beat him in despair" and "wept in secret" are in neither source. The Life says his parents scolded, the teacher punished, and schoolmates mocked, and he prayed alone with tears. Fixed here, in m03, and in a did_you_know item. His brothers (Stefan and Petr) also learned fast; added.
7. Biography, "Logs on Their Shoulders": cut the quote "in the thicket where there was water". The Life's ch.5 says there was a source of water, but ch.12 says there was none nearby. Radonezh is a "village" in the Life, not a "small town". Parents died "a few years" after taking the habit, not "some years". "Without either of them saying so" invented a state of mind and is rewritten. Cut the unsupported "That friendship would one day matter to his brother."
8. Biography, same: tonsure age 23 now attributed to the Life (the other line says 20). Death age 78 attributed to the Pachomius revision.
9. Biography, "A Knock on the Cell Window": "from afar" meant indirectly, "as if telling parables", not at a distance. Fixed here and in teachings. "Two years or more" changed to the Life's "two years, perhaps more or less". Added how Sergius became hegumen (he refused for a long time; the brothers pressed him; Bishop Athanasius ordained him). Stefan "came from Moscow" instead of "came too".
10. Biography, "One Rule Is Missing": "oldest friend in the Church" is not in the sources (cut; "friend" is in Rogovich and the Life). The Patriarch's letter urged common life; the draft described Alexis as agreeing, and now says Sergius took the letter to Alexis. Alexis's order of events fixed (cross first, then the offer of bishop and later the throne; Sergius firm "as diamond"; Michael put on metropolitan robes).
11. Biography, "Do Not Be Afraid": the Mother of God scene now matches ch.26 (Sergius went out into the entry; Micah on the floor). His prayer asked intercession for the place, not protection of disciples.
12. Biography, same section: the vision of the birds is in the Life itself (ch.14), not only in Dimitry of Rostov. The draft, the Note on the Sources, miracle m16, and the gaps list were wrong. Rewritten from ch.14 (Simon saw only a gleam of the light; the promise is conditional on following his steps).
13. Biography, "A Face Not Like the Dead": "the brothers said" removed (the Life states it). The Epiphanius paragraph said he wrote "twenty-five years later" and gathered notes for twenty years. The Life says he began a year or two after the death, finished 26 years after, and drew on elders, a servant, Stefan, and Sergius's own words. Rewritten.
14. Biography, "Water Presses My Body": the Life says the grave was opened and, on 5 July, the relics were placed in a shrine; the year 1422 is modern scholarship. Wording adjusted here, in m18, and in relic_description. Added the fragrance. The Golubinsky letter of Jona (1449 or 1450) is now attributed to Golubinsky as his reading; 1452 kept as "some encyclopedias". Maxim the Greek's objection is stated as he made it (Sergius and the Moscow hierarchs held towns, villages, wealth); cut the editorial jab that followed it.
15. Biography, "A Lock of Hair, 1919": "the new Soviet government" changed to the Commissariat of Justice decree; added that Church representatives were present and that the foot bones had crumbled; Trubetskoy "buried at the Lavra". Cut "Russian historians have also studied the account" (the source wording is vague). Cut the claim in the Note, in relic_description, and in gaps that the 1919 protocol "reports bones of the whole body": this is not in the source. The protocol, as described by ru.wikipedia, records bones, robe fragments, and a skull with a lock of hair.
16. Biography, "Hegumen of the Russian Land": cut "the words his brothers remembered" (source says only spiritual instructions are known). The count "about fifty-five miracles" was inflated: chapters 34-88 are 55 chapters, but ch.34 is Nikon's death and ch.86 is a eulogy. Now "more than fifty chapters of miracles and tales", marked as known from titles with two chapters skimmed, in the biography Note, m19, and gaps. The siege (1608-10) comes from the edition's footnote. Ending paragraph rewritten to end on the image and the Mother of God's promise rather than summarize.
17. Biography, opening: the plowman's line "I came to see a prophet..." was said after Sergius came out of the garden and the monks pointed him out, not at the fence. Added that moment. Epiphanius's birth-sign comparisons now include more of the names he lists (Jeremiah, John the Baptist). "Great modesty" changed to "modesty and restraint" (the Russian says both).
18. Biography, Note on the Sources and How This Life Was Researched: rewritten. The Note now says that the "if God helps" wording is known from ru.wikipedia's quotation of Epiphanius (the raw earliest text was not read), who accepts which birth year, and what is known about the protocol. The research count is now "chapters 2-33 checked against the Russian text; 34-88 mainly by title". The "nine details" line is limited to "not found in the English text consulted" (nine NEW tags in the notes confirmed).
19. entry.json: m04 and the death passage state the 23 and 78 ages as the Pachomius line's; gaps and sources rewritten to match (birds, disciples as saints now supported by ru.wikipedia captions, protocol, Kulikovo, word count, chapter reading). Added a gap on Julian versus civil feast dates: feast_day_orthodox holds 25 Sept, and other drafts mix Julian and civil dates.
