A plowman walked a long way to see the famous hegumen of a forest monastery near Radonezh, and found a man in a patched robe digging a garden. "I came to see a prophet," he said, "and you show me an orphan." Then a prince rode in and bowed to the gardener. Sergius of Radonezh was a boy who could not learn to read, a hermit who shared his last crust with a bear, and the monk whose blessing a grand prince sought before he marched against the Horde. How did he become the Hegumen of the Russian land without ever ceasing to be poor?
