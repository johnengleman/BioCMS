## Findings (appended as work goes)

### Verified against raw Bulgakovsky 1891 (Russian text)
- Q1 pie, Q2 "царя на коне", Q3 nettle, Q4 pancakes (24 Dec 1761), Q5 rivers of blood, Q6 "Что ты плачешь...", Q8 mirror, Q9 coffee/Okhta, Q10 "кобылья голова", Q11 neighbours, Q12 gravestone: words confirmed. English renderings are our own; two were tightened (see fixes: Q8 mirror, Q12 stone "salvation... Amen").
- Q7 "да не троньте покойницу, что она вам сделала, прости Господи!…": occasion CONFIRMED. She said it in anger ("очень сердилась") when anyone called her Aksinya, because she held that Aksinya Grigorievna was dead. Not a funeral line. "Прости Господи" is an exclamation, so "ends in a prayer" was too strong.
- Q17 "Оставьте вы Ксению! Что она вам худого сделала?": confirmed only in Blagovest 2010 (answered "sadly", then "my Xenia has died and rests in the cemetery, but I, a sinner, am wholly here"). It is NOT an older form of Q7: the 1891 text has her angry, the modern Life has her sad. Wording was wrong in the draft ("older and harsher form").
- Q14 "Господь питает птиц небесных, а я не хуже птицы": occasion in Blagovest is the day she gave her house to Paraskeva (Paraskeva begged her not to give everything away). The draft put it in her old age. Fixed.
- Q16 and Q15: Blagovest confirms occasions (merchants' offers; a Sytny shopkeeper's sheepskin). Q18/Q19 (1988 and 1978 Acts): Russian wording confirmed in Blagovest.
- Dates: ROCOR Act dated 9 Sept O.S. 1978, glorification Sunday 11 Sept O.S. = 24 Sept 1978, Cathedral of the Sign, New York; testimonies collected since 1971 (Act). Moscow Local Council 6-9 June 1988, Trinity-Sergius Lavra, Patriarch Pimen; ru.wikipedia and azbyka give 6 June 1988 (24 May O.S.). Feast 24 Jan O.S. = 6 Feb; all conversions correct (+13 days).
- Birth/death: no "1790-1806" anywhere; birth_year and death_year null. OK.
- Smolensky church: Blagovest says 1796 (building in progress), azbyka 1786-90, cemetery article "by 1790", ru.wikipedia plate 1794-96, Bulgakovsky footnote: 100-year jubilee 1 Oct 1890. Conflict stays in the Note; body says only "late in the eighteenth century".
- Grebenka: the raw booklet NEVER names Grebenka. Footnote 1 only says "Физиология Петербурга, изд. Некрасова, 1845 г., ч. I, стр. 219", attached to her answering "Ась!". The draft said the 1845 book "mentions her in passing" and called it "Grebenka's book"; unsupported. Fixed to the booklet's own citation.
- Paul at 47: Blagovest says "умрет в 47 лет, что впоследствии и сбылось". Paul I died aged 46 (born 1754, died March 1801). Kept out of the story; entry.json m04 now marked legendary.
- Ivan Antonovich: the booklet says he sat in the Schlüsselburg fortress and was executed under Catherine II; Blagovest says killed (Mirovich attempt). Draft stays with notes: died at Schlüsselburg, 1764, no further identification.
