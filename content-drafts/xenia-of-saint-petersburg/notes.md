# Xenia of Saint Petersburg - research notes (tier B) - PARTIAL, appended as work goes

## 1. Identity
- Name: Ксения Григорьевна Петрова; Ксения Петербургская; blessed (блаженная), fool for Christ (юродивая). Husband's name she used: Андрей Фёдорович. [S1, S2]
- Born: unknown. Range "between 1719 and 1730" or "about 1731" [S1]; azbyka calendar says "between 1719 and 1731" / "ok. 1720-1803" [S2]. Her gravestone text says she lived 71 years [S1, S3].
- Died: unknown. Wikipedia: "end of 18th - start of 19th century", one scholar view 1802 [S1]. Bulgakovsky 1891: M. I. Belyaeva says "about 90 years ago" (so about 1800) [S3]. Blagovest 2010: no written record of exact time or circumstances of death or burial [S4]. Request said "1790-1806"; I did not find that range in a source yet.
- Place: Saint Petersburg, Petrograd side (Петербургская сторона). Buried Smolensky Orthodox cemetery [S1, S2, S3].
- Tradition: Orthodox (Russian Orthodox Church; also ROCOR). Not a Catholic saint.
- Feast: 24 Jan Julian = 6 Feb Gregorian (name day) [S1, S2]. NOTE: the request had the calendars as "6 Feb Julian / 24 Jan Gregorian". That is reversed. Source shows 24 Jan (Julian) = 6 Feb (new style). Also 24 May (6 June) = glorification day in the Moscow Patriarchate [S1: "24 мая (6 июня)"]; azbyka lists 6 June "Прославление"; and 11 July (moving) Synaxis of Saint Petersburg saints [S2]. ROCOR feast: 24 Jan old style and 11 Sept old style (glorification day) [S4 Act].
- Canonization: ROCOR Council of Bishops Act dated 9 Sept (O.S.) 1978; glorification 11 Sept O.S. = 24 Sept N.S. 1978 at the Synodal Cathedral of the Sign, New York [S4, S1]. Moscow Patriarchate: Local Council 6-9 June 1988 at Holy Trinity-Sergius Lavra, Patriarch Pimen; date used 6 June 1988 [S1, S2, S4]. So request's "canonized 1988" is right for Moscow, but ROCOR was first (1978).
- Tier: B.

## 3. Timeline (source strength: A = 1840s print; B = 1891 pious booklet; C = modern retelling; L = legend)
- c.1719-1731: birth, unknown. Father Grigory; mother unknown. Source of "noble family" is an inference ("one must suppose") [S3 B; S4 C].
- Marriage to Andrei Fedorovich Petrov, court choir singer holding colonel's rank. [S3 B; S4 C]. Blagovest says the couple lived 3.5 years and had no children, house bought with her dowry (C, no source shown) [S4].
- Husband's sudden death, she aged 26. [S3 B]. Blagovest: died without confession and communion. [S4 C]
- Legend: he fell dead at a ball; guardsman Ivan Ushakov (later Monk Feodor, Alexander Nevsky Lavra) left the world too. Blagovest itself calls this "far from the truth" because of dates; says the monk may have been their confessor. [S4, L]
- Funeral day: she put on his uniform (waistcoat, kaftan, trousers, cap) and followed the coffin, saying he had not died but "incarnated" in her. [S4 C]. In 1891: she said she was not Aksinya Grigorievna but Andrei Fedorovich. [S3 B]. 1847 gazette: she wore his linen, waistcoat, kaftan until it rotted on her body. [S3 A: 1847]
- She gave her house to Paraskeva Antonova (friend, non-commissioned officer's widow, a lodger). Relatives petitioned her husband's superiors for guardianship. They examined her, found her sane, and let her dispose of property. [S2, S4; C]
- 45 years of wandering (gravestone text) [S1, S3].
- Smolensky church: stone church built to replace a decayed wooden one; builders found bricks carried to the scaffold at night, saw Xenia. [S3 B, S2]. DATE CONFLICT: azbyka says built 1786-1790 [S2]; Wikipedia quotes the memorial plate as 1794-1796 [S1]; Bulgakovsky footnote says the church's 100-year jubilee was celebrated on 1 Oct 1890 [S3] (so built c.1790). Wikipedia's plate text may be a later plate. Treat 1794-96 as shaky.
- Prediction: death of Empress Elizabeth. Bulgakovsky: on 24 Dec 1761 she shouted "Bake pancakes, bake pancakes, all Russia will bake pancakes"; the Empress died next day, 25 Dec. [S3 B]. (Pancakes were memorial food.) The date is as in S3; do not add Gregorian dates without a source.
- Prediction: Ivan Antonovich (Schlusselburg). Three weeks before his death she wept daily: "There the rivers are full of blood..." [S3 B]. S2 and S1 date it 1764.
- Prediction: Emperor Paul, he will die at 47 years old [S4 C only]. Check: Paul's real age at death.
- 1790s-1800s: last years; people from rich merchant houses invited her to live with them; she said "Зачем мне без нужды страдать?" [S4 C].
- Death: unknown date; dream of the Mother of God announcing it is "tradition" [S4 C, L].
- c.1820: mass pilgrimage to the grave began "точно известно" per Blagovest [S4 C] (source not given).
- 19th c.: small chapel built on the grave (3rd quarter of 19th c.); 1902 large stone chapel by architect Alexander Vseslavin (Всеславин)  [S1, S2].
- Soviet: chapel closed 1940 and 1962, returned to the Church in 1983 [S1].
- 1978 ROCOR canonization; 1988 Moscow canonization (see section 1).

## 4. Character
- Gentle; once only seen in anger, chasing mean boys with her stick; the neighbourhood punished the boys. [S3 B incl. 1847 gazette; S4 C]
- Took only old copper kopecks, "царь на коне" (tsar on a horse), and gave them away at once. [S3 B, S2]
- Wore red jacket and green skirt, or the reverse, all year. [S3 B] The Preobrazhensky-regiment uniform colours link is modern guess [S4 C; S1 says "military uniform colours"].
- Prayed all night in the fields, bowing to the four directions; police watched her, then were satisfied. [S3 A: 1847 gazette]
- Merchants at Sytny market opened baskets to her because trade then went well. [S3 A: 1847]
- Went to the bath in her shirt and came out in the wet shirt. [S3 B]
- Disliked Evdokia Gaidukova's husband, called him "mare's head" (кобылья голова). [S3 B oral]


## 3b. Added timeline lines
- 1845: first print mention found: Grebenka, "Физиология Петербурга" pt I p.219, quoted by S3 for her answering "Ась!" when called Andrei Fedorovich. [S3 A]
- 1847: two articles in the Saint Petersburg police gazette (nos. 264 and 272) describe "Андрей Федорович" as a figure of past decades: "forty years or maybe a little more ago" she died [S3 A]. This is the earliest dated text I read. It puts her death near 1807 or earlier; the writer was vague.
- 1873: Russkaya Starina vol. VIII (July) story of a fire saved after a dream, cited by S3 (not read directly) [S3 B].
- 1890: Smolensky church 100-year jubilee on 1 Oct, per S3 footnote [S3]. S5 says stone church built by 1790 by Fr. Georgy Petrov, architect Alexei Ivanov.
- 1891: Bulgakovsky booklet; by 1904 it was in its 14th edition [S1 bibliography].
- 1903: House of Industry (Дом Трудолюбия) founded at the Smolensky church in memory of Xenia [S5].
- 1917+: after the Revolution, pilgrimage continued after the chapel closed in 1940 ("decision to demolish within a month", not carried out) [S5]. Reopened 1946-47; closed again 1960 or 18 Nov 1962 (sources differ: S5 cemetery page says 1960, S5 chapel page says 18 Nov 1962); returned 1983; re-consecrated 10 Aug 1987 by Metropolitan Alexy (Ridiger) [S5].
- Feb 2017: Hermitage showed an alleged lifetime portrait, painted c. end of 18th - start of 19th c. by soil analysis [S6]. LATER the Hermitage curator Gudymenko attributed it to blessed Marfa Yakovlevna Sonina [S1]. Treat as unsure.
- 6 June 2023: monument at the chapel (sculptors Vadim Sazonov, Nikolai Ivanov) [S1].
- First monastery in her name: Baran, Belarus, Aug 2002; first in Russia: Dolbenkino, Oryol region, 5 Mar 2010 [S1].

## 5. Quotations (Russian exact copies from S3, 1891, public domain; our English)
Q1. "Нет, пекли, а не хотите мне дать." - "No, you did bake one, and you do not want to give it to me." (pie with fish) [S3 / 1847 gazette]
Q2. "Дайте мне «царя на коне»" - "Give me the tsar on a horse." (her word for an old kopeck) [S3 / 1847 gazette]
Q3. "зелена крапива, а скоро завянет" - "The nettle is green, but it will soon wither." (to the merchant's wife Krapivina; she died soon after) [S3]
Q4. "Пеките блины, пеките блины, вся Россия будет печь блины" - "Bake pancakes, bake pancakes, all Russia will bake pancakes." (24 Dec 1761) [S3]
Q5. "Там речки налились кровью, там каналы кровавые, там кровь, кровь, кровь!" - "There the rivers are full of blood, there the canals are bloody, there is blood, blood, blood." [S3]
Q6. "Что ты плачешь, Андрей Федорович? Не обидел-ли кто тебя?" - passers-by: "Why are you crying, Andrei Fedorovich? Did someone hurt you?" [S3]
Q7. "да не троньте покойницу, что она вам сделала, прости Господи!…" - "Leave the dead woman alone. What did she do to you? Lord forgive me!" [S3]
Q8. "вот зеркало-то хорошо, а поглядеться не во что" - "This mirror is fine, but there is nothing to look at in it." (the mirror fell and broke; merchant Razzhivin) [S3]
Q9. "Ты вот кофе распиваешь, а твой муж на Охте жену хоронит" - "You sit drinking coffee, and your husband is burying his wife in Okhta." [S3]
Q10. "Ох, уж эта мне кобылья голова!" - "Oh, that mare's head of his!" (of Gaidukova's husband) [S3]
Q11. "Кто не принадлежит миру, тот принадлежит Богу" - "Who does not belong to the world belongs to God." (neighbours, in the 1847 gazette) [S3]
Q12. Tombstone: "Кто меня знал, да помянет мою душу для спасения своей души." - "Whoever knew me, let him remember my soul for the saving of his own." [S3 and S1; wording differs slightly between the two]
Q13. "Вот ты тут сидишь да чулки штопаешь, а не знаешь, что тебе Бог сына послал!" - "You sit here mending stockings, and you do not know that God has sent you a son." (Paraskeva; S2, modern retelling, not 1891) [S2 C]
Q14. "Господь питает птиц небесных, а я не хуже птицы." - "The Lord feeds the birds of heaven, and I am no worse than a bird." [S4 C, modern]
Q15. "Подари его тому, кто без него несчастен, – тому он принесет радость" - "Give it to the one who is unhappy without it; it will bring him joy." (refusing a sheepskin) [S4 C, modern]
Q16. "Зачем мне без нужды страдать?" - "Why should I suffer when there is no need?" (to merchants who offered her a home) [S4 C]
Q17. Answer to being called Xenia: "Оставьте вы Ксению! Что она вам худого сделала?" - "Leave Xenia alone! What harm did she do you?" [S4 C]
Q18. 1988 Council: "бывшую юродивой, почитавшуюся еще при жизни, и на протяжении XIX–ХХ веков – скорой помощницей и чудотворицей" (official text) [S4, Act of 1988, text quoted there] - "who was a fool for Christ, was revered in her lifetime, and through the 19th and 20th centuries as a quick helper and wonderworker."
Q19. ROCOR Act 1978: "бездомную странницу Петрова града" - "the homeless wanderer of Peter's city." [S4 Act of ROCOR]
Source notes: all words attributed to her are tradition and oral. The 1847 gazette is the oldest print; the 1891 booklet adds oral testimony of an 83-year-old woman.

## 6. Scenes (all tradition; mark as such)
1. Funeral: young widow in her husband's uniform walks behind his coffin and tells mourners that Xenia has died. [S4 C, S3 B]
2. Relatives ask the husband's superiors to stop her giving away her goods; the officers talk with her and find her sane. [S2, S4]
3. Night prayer in the open field on her knees, bowing to the four directions; the police follow her and go away satisfied. [S3 A]
4. Night bricks: workmen at the Smolensky church find bricks carried up to the scaffolds overnight. [S3 B, S2]
5. Sytny market: sellers open their trays so she will take a pie; sales rise. [S3 A: 1847]
6. Her one rage: she chases boys who threw mud, stick in hand, grey hair loose; the street punished the boys. [S3 A/B]
7. Bath: she climbs the shelf in her shirt, wets her head, and walks out in the wet shirt. [S3 B]
8. Pancakes: the evening before the Empress died. [S3 B]
9. A woman in plain dress stops a weeping colonel's wife on a bridge and tells her to have a requiem at Xenia's grave. [S3 B]
10. The old woman seen after her death on the Petrograd side, warning of danger (urban legend). [S4 C, L]

## 7. Miracles (all after-death items are popular reports; none documented by an investigation in my sources)
Lifetime (tradition, first told in 1847 or 1891):
- M1 Fire put out: she gave Evdokia Gaidukova a copper five-kopeck coin and said the fire would go out; Evdokia's house caught fire but the flames were put out before she reached it. Source: Belyaeva's oral story, printed 1891 [S3 B, popular].
- M2 Husband of the daughter: the bride-to-be learns the widower is burying his wife in Okhta; later she married him [S3 B, popular].
- M3 Mirror breaks [S3 B]; Krapivina death [S3 A, 1847 gazette]; pie [S3 A].
- M4 Tsarist deaths: Elizabeth (Dec 1761) [S3 B]; Ivan Antonovich 1764 [S3 B; S2]; Paul will die at 47 [S4 C; I did not verify his age at death. S-ru Wikipedia Paul I says born 20 Sept 1754 O.S.].
- M5 Paraskeva's child: Xenia tells Paraskeva Antonova she has a son; Paraskeva finds a woman killed by a carriage who gave birth, adopts the boy [S2 C, popular; not found in the 1891 text].
- M6 Children healed or trade improved when she touched them [S2 C; 1847 gazette has the weaker form: mothers were glad if she rocked a child, and she brought luck] [S3 A].
After death, in 1891 text [S3 B], popular, no Church inquiry named:
- Colonel's wife, two sons into cadet corps, after requiem at grave.
- Fake colonel, in truth escaped convict, exposed the day before wedding; ran away convict story (the "colonel" killed a real colonel and coachman). Wikipedia/azbyka summarize as help in escaping marriage with a runaway convict [S2].
- Captain A. N. B-v's son healed with earth from the grave in water.
- Doctor Bulakh got a post in Rzhev after a requiem; I. I. Is-ov got four offers the same day; P. D. A-ev, mint inspector, rubbed with grave earth, recovered next day; a baby girl named Xenia in thanks.
- 1873 Pskov-province fire: landowner dreams Xenia sprinkles the house; hay barn 20 sazhen away burned, house saved (cited from Russkaya Starina 1873).
- Lutheran German woman's headache and the shaking cupboard; light around the earth. [S3 B]
- Earth taken from her mound: the mound was rebuilt several times; a slab was chipped away; people dropped money, so a collection box was put out [S2, S5].
- Blagovest 2010 has many more 1912-13 and modern reports (not read in full) [S4 C].
Status: ROCOR 1978 Act says it collected testimonies from 1971 onward; 1988 Council says "в полном убеждении об истинности и достоверности чудес" (in full conviction of the truth and reliability of the miracles) [S4].

## 8. Other
- Why so little is documented: Blagovest and Wikipedia both say no documents on her life survive; first print of folk tales dates from 1840s [S1, S4]. The 1891 booklet had to admit: "Из какого звания происходила Ксения – неизвестно" (what station she came from is unknown) [S3]. Her rank/background is an inference from the marriage [S3].
- Name forms: Russian "Аксинья" appears in 1891 (S3: "не Аксинья Григорьевна, а Андрей Федорович"); the Church form is Ксения.
- Sources conflict: (a) birth 1719/1730/1731; (b) death c.1800/1802/1803; (c) church built 1786-90 (S2, S5) versus plate "1794-1796"; (d) feast calendar notes; (e) tombstone text (S3 vs S1 differ in wording); (f) 1891: "26 years" at husband's death, "45 years" wandering, "71 years" total, which sum is 26+45=71 so her age at death follows from the tombstone only [S3, S1]. If at 26 she was widowed in c.1744-1757 the dates vary by the birth guess.
- Why Elizabeth's death matters: Blagovest says Petrov died in Elizabeth's reign [S4 C]. This is a modern deduction, not documentary.
- Holy foolery in the 18th century: Troy Thomas (via Sanidopoulos 2018, intro only) says the Synod held holy fools suspect; Blagovest says the Spiritual Regulation of Peter I treated fools as frauds or possessed, so canonization was impossible in the Synodal period [S4]. Thomas is cited for the scholar S. Ivanov calling her the "transvestite saint" [from bec.txt intro; not verified at the source].
- Patronage: first woman saint glorified on Petersburg soil per Governor Beglov (2023 speech reported by S1: "first woman among saints"). Popular: help for housing, jobs, marriage, children, students. Grave called "all-people's reception room" [S4 preface, C].
- Marie Feodorovna (wife of Alexander III) honored her [S4 C, unverified source].
- Image candidates: Hermitage portrait 2017 (attribution disputed) [S1, S6]; icons show red skirt, green jacket, stick, with the Smolensky church and chapel behind [S1]. Commons category linked in S1 (not checked for licence).
- Coin: Niue 2012 and 2016 coins show her [S1].
- Troparion, tone 7 (Church text in S1): "Нищету Христову возлюбивши..." (not copied here).
- Possible link: Saint Feodor Ushakov (Alexander Nevsky Lavra) as confessor - legend, weak [S4].
- Other holy fool buried at the same cemetery: Anna Petersburgskaya (Lashkina) [S1].
