## Fixes (biography.html)

1. Opening, para 2. "In 1298 the Church named him one of its four great Latin teachers" overstated the source. The Catholic Encyclopedia says only that a decree of Boniface VIII (1298) ordered the four Doctors' feasts kept in the whole Church. Now "By 1298 the whole Church honored him as one of its four great Latin teachers".
2. Ch. 1 (Madaura, note 5). The spending beyond the family's means and the praise belong to the Carthage stay (Conf. II.3.5 "longe peregrinanti"), not to Madaura. Moved after the Carthage money. "Neighbors" changed to "people".
3. Ch. 1. "Patricius was proud of his clever son" (a feeling no source gives) changed to "ambitious for", as Butler vol. 8, pp. 410–411 ("whose only ambition was to make this son a scholar") and "animositate" in Conf. II.3.5.
4. Ch. 2, note 12 quotation. "their modest style" added "style". Latin "refugiebat modum eius". Now "their humble manner".
5. Ch. 2, Manichees. "defended its teaching" had no source. Cut. Note 8 now also cites Conf. IV.2.2 (the one woman, his faithfulness to her).
6. Ch. 9 (first para). "The party went back to Rome for the winter" had no source. Butler p. 446: after Saint Monica's death Saint Augustine went back to Rome and stayed there until the next year, landing at Carthage about September 388. Now says that.
7. Ch. 9, lead-in to the Possidius quotation. Proof sentence "Saint Augustine told him what happened next" removed (see copy-edit.md).
8. Ch. 10 (monastery). "Valerius gave him more than time" had no source (Letter 21 gives only the request, no answer). Now Possidius 5 (monastery "intra ecclesiam") and Sermon 355 in Weiskotten's note 2 to ch. 5 (Valerius gave him the garden). Note 72 now cites that note. "Struggled with Latin" changed to "less at home in Latin" (Possidius 5 "minus Latina lingua instructus").
9. Ch. 10 (table). Proof sentence "I and the others who were at that table experienced this" removed.
10. Ch. 10 (the gift of property, note 79). "to soften the request" is a motive Possidius does not give. Now "with the request". "Asked for the deed back" corrected: Possidius 24 says he sent word by his son that the deed be returned to the son.
11. Ch. 11, ambush on Possidius (note 83). "They seized him and his companions" is not safe: Weiskotten's English says he escaped with his companions, while the Latin text on the page is broken. Now "took his animals and baggage and beat him badly" (both agree).
12. Ch. 11, Crispinus (note 84). The judge was asked by "the Catholic bishop" of Calama, not by Saint Augustine; Augustine pressed for the debate and later worked for the emperor's mercy. Possidius 12 also says Crispinus was liable to the gold fine and that it was not exacted. Now "ordered to pay a fine in gold. The Catholic bishop of Calama then asked the judge not to collect".
13. Ch. 11. "in those years" removed from the limb-of-Christ line (Possidius 18 does not date it). "all his books" changed to "his books".
14. Ch. 11 (dying friend, note 96). "cried the same words" changed to "asked God the same question" (Conf. VIII.12.28 "quare non modo?" is the question, not the same sentence).
15. Ch. 12 (death). "He was seventy-six years old" cannot stand with a birth on 13 November 354 (75 years and a few months). Possidius says he lived 76 years. Now "in his seventy-sixth year".
16. Ch. 12 (Psalms, note 99). "shortest" is Weiskotten's reading of "paucissimi". Now "a few of David's psalms of repentance".
17. Ch. 8 (Ostia, note 59). "You are putting your mother here" changed to "Here you will bury your mother" (Latin "ponitis hic matrem vestram").
18. Ch. 8 and ch. 13. "The Holy Sacrifice" now carries its meaning: "the holy sacrifice of the Mass". Note 64 and note 103.
19. Ch. 13 (relics, note 104/105). "About the year 720" changed to "In the 720s" (Sciberras 721–725; Butler "about 720"). "A great marble shrine was begun" changed to "ordered" (Sciberras "commissioned"). The year of the papal confirmation is 1728 (Butler; 22 September 1728 in Sciberras).
20. Ch. 13 (Letter 211, note 106). The bio said Saint Augustine "praised their choice" and quoted "one soul and one heart in God" from section 2. Wrong: the letter is a rebuke (title and section 4), and the quoted call to unity is the first rule in section 5 ("let your hearts and minds be one in God"). Now "He rebuked them and set down rules... The first rule was to live in the house in unity, 'with your hearts and minds one in God.'" Note 106 now cites Letter 211.4–5.
21. Ch. 6. "He had always told himself" changed to "He had told himself" (Conf. VIII.7.18 "dicebas").
22. A Note on the Sources. Sciberras says no reliable document records the move to Sardinia, not to Pavia; scholars argued about the find before 1728. Rewritten. How This Life Was Researched: Letter 228 added.
23. Sources and notes. The list had the EWTN page of 16 January 2008, but notes 2, 76 and the list said 20 February 2008. The 20 February audience (Vatican text) is the one that gives "397 and 400" and the two dates of the City of God. List entry replaced; note 87 now also cites it.
