# Check: Augustine of Hippo (standard)

Originals kept as biography.html.before-check and summary.txt.before-check. Lint: no MUST FIX and no CHECK lines after the fixes.

## Fixes (biography.html unless noted)

1. Ch. 8 (Ostia), fever timing. The Latin (Conf. IX.11.27) is "vix intra quinque dies aut non multo amplius decubuit febribus": she took to her bed within five days (of the window talk) or a little more. The scene card and the draft read it as five days in bed. Now: "Within five days or a little more she took to her bed with fever." Death "on the ninth day of her sickness" and "his brother" present stay as they were. The cards for SC-08 and SC-09 and plan.md carry the old reading.
2. Ch. 4 (shore). The draft said he did not tell his mother and "she found out anyway". The Latin (V.8.15) says only that she mourned him as he left and followed him to the sea. The words "nec indicabas mihi nec matri" mean God did not reveal his purpose to either of them. SC-04 step 2 misreads this. Both claims cut.
3. Ch. 4, quotation. "hurrying me on to end all desire" (Pusey) did not match "raperes ad finiendas ipsas cupiditates". Now "carrying me off, to put an end to those very desires."
4. Ch. 4. "a place of prayer next to where the ship lay" and "sailed in the dark" cut. The card lists the hour and the building as not in the sources. Now "the place nearest his ship" and "That night he sailed in secret."
5. Ch. 2, quotation. "unholy loves sang" (Pusey) against "sartago flagitiosorum amorum ... circumstrepebat". Now "a cauldron of shameful loves sizzled all around me."
6. Ch. 2. "untrained believers" (the Latin says "multos imperitos", with no word for believers) cut. The same fact stays in the bishop's words.
7. Ch. 2. "begged" changed to "asked". The bishop's books are now "nearly all" (omnes paene). The Latin has "paene".
8. Ch. 3. "away from Thagaste" cut to "away" (the card lists where he was as not in the sources). "a crowd of students" changed to "students" (no source for a crowd).
9. Ch. 5. Faustus was "the most famous teacher the Manichees had". The Latin says only "nominatus apud eos". Now "a famous Manichee teacher". Age "thirty" changed to "about thirty".
10. Ch. 6. "on the spot" cut (not in the card).
11. Ch. 7. "outside Milan" cut (the card gives no place for the country house). Possidius says Ambrose baptized Augustine, not "them". "discovered the music of the church" changed to "wept at" (the passage says he wept). "a few years off" changed to "years away".
12. Ch. 8. "touched eternal wisdom" dropped "modice" (a little). Now "though only a little". The grave beside her husband is no longer "in Thagaste" (IX.11.28 says only beside her husband). The friends at Ostia heard "something like it", not "the same thing". "A friend named Evodius" changed to "Evodius" (the card gives no role).
13. Ch. 9. Possidius quotation (ch. 4). The English dropped the words after "to console him" ("saying that the priest's office, though he deserved more, is close to a bishop's"). The words are restored in the quotation. The paragraph after it no longer repeats them.
14. Ch. 9. "before he knew the sailors' work" came with no explanation. Letter 21 (section 2) uses the picture of a man who found fault with sailors before he knew their work. Now stated. "most of what we know about Augustine the bishop" changed to "much of what we know about the daily life of Augustine the bishop".
15. Ch. 10. "His first great fight" changed to "One early fight". The line "The date was 28 August, and it would come back" cut (it repeated the date and hinted at a sign; the notes say it is a coincidence). "the man who had hidden from bishops" was false (Valerius hid him; he avoided churches with no bishop). Now "the man who had feared the bishop's chair came to sit in it". "he remembered the table best" changed to "he wrote about the table" (inference). "more than once" cut (Possidius ch. 23 reports one address). The line on strangers and friends is now a saying Augustine took to heart (Possidius ch. 19 gives it as someone's remark). "a second church" changed to "a rival Christian body".
16. Ch. 11. "The last fight was the longest" was false (the Donatist struggle ran far longer). Now "His last great fight".
17. Ch. 12. Homily 7: "flatters" changed to "caresses" (Browne). "given once" changed to "given once for all". "out of love" (repeated) changed to "from love". "one of his shortest rules" cut. "everything he had ever written" changed to "his books" (the Retractationes cover his books).
18. Ch. 13. The answer to Honoratus is now attributed and fits Letter 228 as Possidius gives it ("in a long letter ... must not leave his people while they still need him"). "adds only" changed to "adds". "never claimed such power" changed to "did not claim such power for himself". "service for his soul" changed to "for the peaceful rest of his body" (Possidius ch. 31). "buried in the city" cut (Possidius gives no place). The Possidius ch. 31 quotation dropped "well-nourished" (enutritus); now restored. "The library survived" stated as fact; cut here, since ch. 14 attributes it to Butler. "July of the next summer" changed to "July 431"; "Four years later" changed to "In 435".
19. Ch. 14. "The Augustinian Order's own historian" changed to "A friar of the Augustinian Order wrote about the relics" (Sciberras is a friar and postulator, not the Order's historian). "listed every bone" changed to "listed the bones". "Pope Paul VI came to pray in 1960" was wrong, since he was a cardinal then and became pope in 1963. "Kings bought his bones" changed to "A king". "Possidius ended his Life with" changed to "Near the end of his Life Possidius wrote". The words come before the poet's lines and the closing prayer.
20. Ch. 15. "every sentence is addressed to God as 'you'" cut as too strong. "never formally canonized" changed to "No formal canonization is recorded" (no source goes beyond "canonized by acclaim"). "at the end of his Life" changed to "near the end". "asked God to take him before his city fell" changed to "to free his city or else to take him" (the prayer has three parts).
21. Ch. 1 and the opening. "He never claimed that the night changed him" changed to "The night did not change him" (card: nothing in the story changes). "three movements that split the Church" changed to "troubled the Church" (the Manichees were not inside the Church).
22. A Note on the Sources. "Possidius never mentions Monica" changed to "does not name Monica and does not tell the garden". Possidius ch. 2 mentions "his mother" once, without a name.
23. How This Life Was Researched. "more than twenty sources" changed to "about twenty" (some were read only in part or by search summary). "twenty-seven miracle reports, most ... at the relics of Saint Stephen" overstated both numbers (the list includes non-miracles and many items not at Stephen's relics); now "more than twenty ... most Augustine's own accounts, many at the relics of Saint Stephen". The quotation statement said "translated fresh from the Latin". Now "put into plain English ... from the Latin and from public-domain translations", which is true for Possidius ch. 31 and the letters.
24. Sources. The New Advent Confessions is Pilkington's translation. I read the page's "About this page" block. The notes and cards say Dods, which is wrong, because Dods did the City of God. Added "Translated by J. G. Pilkington". Letters 21 and 211 are also New Advent pages, and they say "Translated by J.G. Cunningham". Added.
25. summary.txt. "Thirty years later" changed to "About thirty years later" (the theft was about 370; the Confessions about 401). "a bishop who read in silence" changed to "a bishop's sermons", as in the biography.

## Modern translations compared with the originals

- Conf. II.4.9, II.6.12, II.8.16, II.9.17, IV.6.11, V.8.15 (block), VI.3.3, VIII.12.29, IX.6.14, IX.10.26 (Monica). The Latin on the cards matches thelatinlibrary.com. The English matches the Latin, apart from item 3 above. The IV.6.11 "because he was dead" became a separate sentence, and the meaning stands.
- Conf. III "loved to love" matches "amare amabam". VI sent-away companion matches VI.15.25. III.11.20 and III.12.21 (dream, bishop) match. VI.3.3 parentheses made into sentences are fine.
- Letter 21 paraphrases match sections 1-4 (see item 14). Letter 211 "one soul and one heart in God" matches section 2. Homily 7 matches section 8 (item 17). Possidius ch. 31 now matches after item 18. Possidius ch. 4, 15, 22, 24, 25, 29 match the Latin and Weiskotten.
- Sermo 256 and City of God XIV.28, XIX.13 match the notes' raw text and the Latin.

## Confirmed unchanged

Garden "late summer 386" (the Sources note credits Butler and the Catholic Encyclopedia). Monica dies on the ninth day of her illness, with his brother present. "exulted and triumphed" (IX: "exultat et triumphat"). Ambrose and Easter come from Possidius and Butler. No years for the undated scenes. No last words. The Pavia letters have no stated form. The Orange link is credited to Butler alone. Letter 211 is dated 423.
