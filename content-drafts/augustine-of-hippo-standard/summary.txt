At nineteen, a student in Carthage opened a book by Cicero and found himself longing for "the deathlessness of wisdom." Then, for years, he ran the other way. He joined a sect that talked of truth and did not have it. He lied to his mother on a harbor shore and sailed away in the night while she stayed behind, praying and weeping. He even prayed, "Give me chastity and self-control, but not yet." Saint Augustine of Hippo became one of the great teachers of the Church, but first he had to face his own divided will. What would it take for a restless heart to find rest?
