## Listed for the checker (not changed, or changed but needing a check)

1. **Madaura and the father's spending (note 5).** Conf. II.3.5 ties Patricius's overspending, and the neighbors' praise, to the far stay at Carthage ("longe peregrinanti"), not to Madaura. I made the sentence neutral ("on his son's studies") so that it no longer makes that link. Please confirm.
2. **"to soften the request" (note 79).** Possidius 24 says the man sent the coins with the request; he gives no motive. This may be an added motive.
3. **Proof sentences.** "“I and the others who were at that table experienced this,” Saint Possidius wrote." (end of note 81) and "Saint Augustine told him what happened next." (before the block quote, note 69). Both prove the story inside the story. I kept them because one ends a chapter section and one sets up the quotation. Cut them if you agree.
4. **Quotation that reads badly:** "“You are putting your mother here,” she said." (Conf. IX.11.27, "ponitis hic matrem vestram"). Perhaps translate again, for example "Here you will bury your mother." I did not edit it.
5. **"The Holy Sacrifice"** (notes 64 and 103) is used without a meaning. A young reader may not know that it is the Mass. A fix needs your choice of words.
6. **"the shortest penitential psalms"** (note 99) follows Weiskotten's English. The Latin "qui sunt paucissimi de poenitentia" may mean "the very few psalms about repentance". Please decide.
7. **The relics ruling (note 105):** "In 1728 the bishop of Pavia declared the relics genuine, and on 22 September Pope Benedict XIII confirmed his ruling." The year of 22 September is only implied (1728). Please confirm.
8. **Valerius as "old" and the agent visit "at once" (note 68):** not changed. These are listed only because the sentence around them was edited.
9. **Isaiah:** Isaiah is now named, from Conf. IX.5.13. `plan.md` still says "Isaiah written as 'the prophets'". That note is now out of date.

## Resolved by the checker (2026-10-07)

1. Madaura (note 5). Confirmed: Conf. II.3.5 ties the spending and the praise to the far stay at Carthage ("longe peregrinanti"), not to Madaura. The sentence now sits after the Carthage money and says "the boy's studies far from home". "Neighbors" is now "people".
2. "to soften the request" (note 79). Cut. Possidius 24 gives no motive. Now "with the request". The request itself is exact too: the man sent word by his son that the deed go back to the son.
3. Proof sentences. "I and the others who were at that table experienced this" removed. "Saint Augustine told him what happened next" removed; the lead-in to the Possidius block quote now says Possidius lived beside him for almost forty years and tells what happened next (he is the quoted speaker).
4. "You are putting your mother here". Latin "ponitis hic matrem vestram" (Conf. IX.11.27). Now "Here you will bury your mother."
5. "The Holy Sacrifice" now "the holy sacrifice of the Mass" at the grave of Saint Monica, and "the Mass" at the burial of Saint Augustine.
6. "shortest penitential psalms". Possidius 31 Latin "paucissimi de poenitentia" can mean "very few". Weiskotten reads "shortest" in his note. Now "a few of David's psalms of repentance", which fits both readings.
7. Papal ruling. Butler (vol. 8, p. 505) says the bishop's ruling and Benedict XIII's confirmation were in 1728, "in the same year"; Sciberras gives 22 September 1728. The sentence stands.
8. Valerius "old" and "at once": confirmed (Possidius 8 "aged"; Possidius 3 "ultro atque confestim").
9. Isaiah confirmed (Conf. IX.5.13). plan.md updated.
