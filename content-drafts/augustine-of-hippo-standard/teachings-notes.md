## Departures from the outline, with reasons

- Section 5 heading is "Augustine and the God Within" (the outline allows a plain heading that names the devotion).
- Section 7 link 3: no date is given for Gregory's letter, because the notes give none. "In the time of Charlemagne" replaces a century count for the same reason.
- "As one fire kindles another" is a short phrase, not a block quote, because the notes hold only the facts-only English of Pius XI and no Latin.
- The Orange conclusion and Boniface II are paraphrased, not block-quoted, because both translations are facts-only.
- Marcellinus is called "a Roman governor" (the notes say "proconsul") to avoid an unexplained Church or Roman term.
- The "tired mother" sentence in the prayer section is the essay's own picture, marked as such ("in his teaching"); it states no fact about Augustine.
- Hermits of St Augustine: 1256 (meeting 1 March, bull 4 May), as the outline and T-S14 require. The biography already reads 1256.
- Possidius's count of ten disciples made bishops is given with three names from the biography notes; no other names.
- Left out as planned: relics, Pavia, the child by the sea, Stephen's healings, patronages, the four doubtful sayings, Anselm, Lombard, Aquinas, Bernard, Isidore, Bede, Alcuin (CLAIMED only), Dominicans and Servites, Teresa, Pascal, Newman, Barlaam and the 14th-century councils, seed-reasons.
- Romanides's claim that Orange condemned Augustine is not stated; the note on the sources says his view is given as his opinion.
