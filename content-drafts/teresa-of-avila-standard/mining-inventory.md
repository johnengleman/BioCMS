# Saint Teresa of Ávila: mining inventory of the large old collections

Sources mined: the Procesos de beatificación y canonización (BMC 18, 19, 20, ed. Silverio de Santa Teresa), the Rótulo of 1609–10 (in BMC 20), Ribera's Vida (1590, book 5, chapters 5–8), and Yepes's Vida (book 4).
Each line is one miracle account (or one group of the same account told by several witnesses). The mark at the end of the line says what was done.

Marks:
- new, added as mNN: new account written into miracles.html (ids m69 to m128).
- already in the list (mNN): the account was already in miracles.html. Where the line says "details merged", a new detail from this source went into the existing account.
- same account as ...: told again by another witness. Counted once, under the line it points to.
- skip (reason): repeat, too thin, or a general statement with no story.
- not added (limit): a sworn or named account that did not fit under the limit of 60 new accounts.

Totals
- New accounts added: 60 (m69 to m128), in five groups: 23 in life, 4 visions, 28 after death, 5 put forward for canonization. (The count by group is m69–m91, m92–m95, m96–m123, m124–m128.)
- Existing accounts that received a merged detail: 27 (m11, m17, m19, m22, m25, m32, m34, m38, m39, m43, m44, m45, m46, m47, m48, m49, m50, m53, m55, m56, m59, m60, m61, m64, m66, m67, m68).
- Lines marked "skip": 95.
- Lines marked "not added (limit)": 192. Many of these are the same account in two or three sources (for example the Baeza, Vallejo, and Ardales stories appear in the Procesos, Ribera, and Yepes). The Procesos part holds the best list of the accounts that remain.
- Lines marked "new, added": 73. The 60 accounts appear on more than one line when several witnesses told the same account.

Coverage notes
- Procesos volumes 1 and 2, and the Rótulo and the 1609–10 hearings in volume 3, were read by keyword windows (milagro, curó, sanó, fiebre, reliquia, and similar). A passing mention with no story may have been missed.
- Pages are from the archive.org scans. Roman page numbers in the Rótulo are approximate. Ribera page numbers are from the scan's page markers and may be off by one.
- Yepes was read in an Italian translation and faster than the Procesos. Ribera chapters 5–8 were read in full.
- Ribera's other chapters (visions, her death) and Yepes's other books mostly tell events that are already in the existing list; they were not itemized.
- Volume 3 was read for the Rótulo (arts. 80–96, the visions, the death signs), the Salamanca hearing (pp. 1–67), and the Alba and Madrid hearings (pp. 170–300). The Valladolid, Burgos, Cuenca, Málaga, and Alcalá hearings of 1609–10 were not itemized.
