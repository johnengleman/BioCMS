Saint Teresa of Ávila wanted one thing all her life: to enjoy God forever. Yet for nearly twenty years she could not give herself to Him. She was so charming that many people came to the convent parlour to talk with her. She gave up prayer and called it humility. Then, close to forty, she threw herself down before an image of the wounded Christ and said she would not get up. What followed were visions that frightened her confessors, convents founded with a few ducats, a book held by the Inquisition, and an obedience she kept even against her own prayer. How did a woman who prayed so badly for so long learn to call God her Friend?
