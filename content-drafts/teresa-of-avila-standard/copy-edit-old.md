11. **Listed item 9 (m41).** Procesos I, p. 171: Saint Teresa showed her own body in a vision and told Blessed Ana to look and see that it was not corrupted. The text now says so.
12. **Listed item 10 (the August night).** The Life (ch. 5 §17–18) says the feast came round, she wanted to confess, her father refused, and "that very night" the fit came. The biography said "before the feast", and now says "When the August feast of Our Lady came". m01 already said "On the night of the August feast". The two now agree.
13. **Other fixes from the same reading.** (a) The copy editor's change to the Salamanca rain ("Did Blessed Anne think...?") put a narrator's question in her mouth. It is now Saint Teresa's own words, from Blessed Anne's sworn account (Obras vol. 5, Foundations ch. 19 note): "Ask Him yourself, if you think He will do it so fast because I say so." (b) "She said no command in her whole life..." had no clear speaker. It was Saint Teresa who said it (Procesos I, pp. 169–170). It now says "Saint Teresa said that no command...". (c) "The nuns heard him beg God not to take her yet" is now "Some of the nuns heard him say that he begged the Lord not to take her yet" (Ribera p. 334: "unas hermanas oyeron"). (d) Her age at her mother's death: the Life says "about twelve years old, a little less"; the text now says "about twelve years old, or a little less".
