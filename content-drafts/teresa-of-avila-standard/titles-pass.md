# Titles pass (2026-10-05)

Files changed: biography.html, summary.txt. Originals: *.before-titles. miracles.html and entry.json were not touched.

## Added "Saint" or "Blessed"
- Saint Teresa (every use of "Teresa" and "Teresa of Jesus", headings and cite lines included; birth name given once: "Saint Teresa was born Teresa de Cepeda y Ahumada").
- Saint John of the Cross (also in the years he was John of St Matthias; the old name is given once as a fact).
- Saint Paul VI (now "Pope Saint Paul VI" in one place).
- Saint Jerome, Saint Augustine, Saint Joseph (also the name of her convent, Saint Joseph's), Saint Peter, Saint Paul, Saint Bartholomew, Saint Michael, Saint Matthew, Saint Francis (feast day), Saint Catherine (eve), Saint Francis Borgia, Saint Ignatius of Loyola, Saint Francis Xavier, Saint Isidore the Farmer, Saint Philip Neri, Saint Peter's (place).
- "Blessed" for Blessed Anne of Jesus (beatified 2024) and Blessed Ana de San Bartolome (beatified 1917). Both were checked on Wikipedia; they are Blessed, not canonized saints.

## Left plain, and why
- Antonio of Jesus (Antonio de Heredia), Jeronimo Gracian, Domingo Banez, Francisco de Ribera, Julian de Avila, Gaspar Daza, Luis de Leon, Rubeo, Pedro de Castro, Juan de la Miseria, Mariano Azaro: not canonized or beatified as far as I know.
- Ana de Mendoza (Princess of Eboli), Ruy Gomez, Philip II, the Duke of Alba, Felipe Sega: not saints.
- Popes Gregory XIII, Gregory XV, Paul V, Sixtus V, Pius XI: not saints.
- Teresa's family (Alonso, Beatriz, Rodrigo, Lorenzo, Teresita, Juana): not saints.
- The other nuns (Mariana, Mariana de la Encarnacion, Juana del Espiritu Santo, Maria de San Francisco): not canonized or beatified as far as I know.
- Saint Peter of Alcantara does not appear in this text.

## Not changed on purpose
- Everything inside quotation marks, including "St Michael's day" in the Foundations quotation.
- Work titles ("Life of Saint Teresa of Jesus"), the Sources list, and heading ids.
- Headings that the title made too long were shortened (for example "Saint John of the Cross Begins the Reform", "Saint Teresa Named a Doctor of the Church").
- Two sentences were split because the titles made them over 30 words.
