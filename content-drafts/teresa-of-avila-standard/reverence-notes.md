# Reverence pass — Saint Teresa of Ávila (standard)

Date: 2026-10-05. Writer: Fable 5.1. Backups: `biography.html.before-reverence`, `miracles.html.before-reverence`, `summary.txt.before-reverence`.

Rules followed: `reverence-pass.md` (nine points, the Saint Francis notes, and the coordinator's rule of 2026-10-05: follow the source's own words about who acted; write "God healed" only where the source names God). Every restored detail below was checked in the Lewis translations of the *Life* and the *Foundations* (archive.org djvu text), or rests on a scene card or notes line that quotes the source.

`summary.txt` was read and left unchanged. It needed nothing: God, Christ, and the saint's obedience are already at its center, and it has no hedge or cold line.

## biography.html

Changes of substance (facts restored or moved). Everything else is wording, proper names of holy things, or the real actor as the subject.

1. **Opening, paragraph 3.** "She could not do both, and the failure made her" (a failure as actor) → "She could not do both. In the end she gave herself wholly to God, and everything that followed grew from that." No new fact; the chapters that follow tell this.
2. **Coma.** "They gave her the sacrament of the sick" → "the Anointing of the Sick, the Church's sacrament for those near death". Added "Then the Lord brought her back to herself, as she wrote" and "Holy Communion". Source: *Life* ch. 5 §18–19 (Lewis: "They administered the Sacrament of Extreme Unction"; "I communicated with many tears"; Spanish "quiso el Señor tornase en mí", scene card SC coma, scenes-1.md).
3. **Saint Joseph.** Kept her own words for the actor ("He gave it back"), per the coordinator's rule. Added one sentence she wrote: Saint Joseph was the guardian of Jesus on earth, and in heaven Jesus grants him whatever he asks. Source: *Life* ch. 6 §9 (Lewis: "as He was Himself subject to him upon earth… so now in heaven He performs all his petitions").
4. **Parlour.** "Sister Saint Teresa" → "Saint Teresa". "One day she saw Christ before her in the parlour with a stern face" → "One day she was with one of these friends, and Christ showed Himself to her with a stern face." The checker had flagged "in the parlour" as not in the text; *Life* ch. 7 says she was with a certain person and "our Lord was pleased to show Himself". Dropped the unsupported place.
5. **Wounded Christ.** "Nothing happened on the spot. She only wrote that she believed it helped her" → "She wrote that she believed this did her great good, and that from then on she grew better." Source: *Life* ch. 9 §3. Tone only.
6. **Vision of hell (new paragraph, before the angel with the spear).** The caller named this vision as part of the heart of her life; it was in the notes (notes-own-words.md, line 29), in miracles.html, and in *Life* ch. 32 §1–2, §7, but not in the biography. Added eight sentences: the Lord's will that she see the place she had deserved; the furnace passage, the mud and vermin, the hollow in the wall; one moment, never forgotten; "one of the grandest mercies of our Lord"; it destroyed her fear of trouble; she gives thanks to the Lord her Deliverer. All checked in Lewis ch. 32. **This is the one addition that goes beyond tone**; revert it if the owner judges it out of scope.
7. **Transverberation.** "She knew how this would sound" → "She wrote that it was a caress of love between the soul and God. For anyone who doubted it she had one prayer: …". Source: *Life* ch. 29 §17 (Lewis: "It is a caressing of love so sweet which now takes place between the soul and God, that I pray God of His goodness to make him experience it who may think that I am lying").
8. **End of visions chapter.** "The large easy convent had made her" (a building as actor) → "God had done all this in her inside that large easy house."
9. **Saint Joseph's, the storm of doubt.** "the Sacrament" → "the Most Holy Sacrament"; "and the doubt left at once" → "She wrote that the devil fled at that moment and left her calm and at peace." Source: *Life* ch. 36 §9 (Lewis: "I promised before the most Holy Sacrament… the devil fled in a moment, and left me calm and peaceful").
10. **Raptures in choir.** "her raptures caught her in public" → "the Lord's raptures came upon her in front of others"; "receive Communion" → "receive Holy Communion". Source: *Life* ch. 20 §5 ("the moment I felt that our Lord was about to repeat the act").
11. **Duruelo.** "basin of blessed water" → "holy water". Source: *Foundations* ch. 14 §6 (scene card: "by the holy water").
12. **Toledo firewood.** "someone they never identified put a small bundle of wood in the church" → "someone laid a small bundle of wood in the church. She never knew who it was, and she wrote that the Lord had moved him to do it." Source: *Foundations* ch. 15 §13 (Lewis: "somebody, I know not who he was, moved by our Lord, laid a faggot in the church").
13. **Pastrana.** "The prioress is said to have cried out" (forbidden hedge) → "The Carmelite chronicle remembers the prioress's cry". Source: *Foundations* ch. 17 n. 17 (Zimmerman, from the *Reforma*); Obras vol. 5 n. 4 "es fama".
14. **Seville.** "made up her mind" (lint: meta-commentary) → "decided… and she felt very little regret". Same fact.
15. **Interior Castle.** "She had seen the soul as a castle" → "God had shown her the soul as a castle… and Himself in the innermost room." Source: Yepes, quoted in the Stanbrook *Interior Castle* introduction (miracles m13; notes-own-words.md line 94).
16. **Letter to the King.** Removed from the body "The sources record no answer from the King." Moved to "A Note on the Sources" as "No reply from the King to her letter of 4 December 1577 is recorded in our sources."
17. **Pedro de Castro.** "She said a Te Deum before she thanked him" → "Before she thanked him she said a Te Deum, the Church's hymn of thanks to God." Source: letter of 19 Nov 1581 (notes-extra-letters.md line 121).
18. **Deathbed, 3 October.** "Then the Sacrament came through the door" (a thing as actor) → "Then the priest came through the door with the Blessed Sacrament." Ribera p. 335; the priest's coming was already in the text.
19. **Death, 4 October (added).** After "in the arms of Blessed Ana de San Bartolomé": Blessed Ana's sworn account that she saw the Lord at the foot of the bed in a great light with many saints and angels waiting to take Saint Teresa's soul to glory; she asked Him to take her at once; she never again felt grief at the death. Source: Procesos I p. 170 (notes-extra-letters.md line 70; miracles m37; check-miracles.md confirms p. 170). Restores God's action at the death, with the teller named.
20. **Grave and relics.** "a scent coming from the grave" → "a sweet fragrance rising from her grave"; "The body was whole" → "Her body was whole and uncorrupted"; "as a relic" → "as a holy relic"; "a piece of her flesh" → "a relic of her flesh". No fact changed.
21. **1585 removal.** "They swore the nuns to silence under censures" → "They ordered the nuns to keep silence under censures." The miracles checker had already corrected this against Procesos I pp. 101–102, 171; the biography is now aligned.
22. **Isabel de Montoya.** "The cures reported from her relics were many" → "Many cures were worked through her relics" (Lovat's own wording, "wrought by relics"). Added "A sister told her to ask Saint Teresa's help" and "walked to Communion"; "the Host at Mass" → "the Sacred Host at Holy Communion". Source: Rótulo art. 108 (notes-extra-letters.md line 107; miracles m51). God is not named as actor because the article does not name Him.
23. **Popes and patronage.** "Pius XI" → "Pope Pius XI". "ask her help… pray to her" → "ask her prayers… ask her to pray for them."
