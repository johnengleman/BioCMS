# Seraphim of Sarov: notes, focus WITNESSES

Researcher notes, 2026-09-30. Language of sources: Russian (my English is a translation). Not read: see "Not read" at the end.

## 1. Identity (witnesses scope only)
- Prokhor Isidorovich Moshnin (Мошнин; some sources spell Mashnin, Машнин) [S2 ch.1, S4]. Monk name Seraphim, tonsured 13 Aug 1786 [S1].
- Birth: popular date 19 July 1759 in the oldest Lives [S1, S2]. Corrected to 1754 after Kursk confession books of 1768 (age 14) were published in 1903; day is probably 19 June 1754 (40th day = feast of Apostle Prochorus, 28 July) [S4 sub 1754]. CONFLICT, see section 8.
- Tradition: Orthodox (Russian). Died 2 Jan 1833 (Julian) at Sarov [S1, S2 ch.10].
- Research tier A. This file covers life events and eyewitness Lives only.

## 3. Timeline
Dates Julian. Source years follow S1/S2 unless noted.
- 1754 (19 June?) Birth in Kursk to merchant Isidor and Agafia Moshnin; siblings Paraskeva (b. 1749) and Alexei (b. 1751) [S4]. NEW (English): he was the younger of three children, not the second of two.
- c.1757 A local holy fool meets Agafia with three-year-old Prokhor and says the boy will be "a strong intercessor before the Holy Trinity" [S4, cites Diveyevo sources]. Mark: tradition.
- c.1757 or 1762 Father Isidor dies, leaving the stone church of St Sergius in Kursk unfinished; Agafia finishes it [S1; S2 ch.1 gives 1762 as death date; S4 says Prokhor was about 1.5 years old by Ioasaf].
- Age ~7 Prokhor wanders up the half-built bell tower, falls to the ground, stands up unhurt. Mother "ran down thinking to find him dead" [S1, S2 ch.1].
- Age ~10 Long, serious illness. In a dream he sees the Mother of God promise to visit and heal him [S2 ch.1]. A procession with the Kursk "Znamenie" icon is turned through the Moshnin yard by rain and mud; Agafia carries out the sick boy; he is held under the icon and recovers [S1, S2 ch.1]. NEW detail: the rain and the route through the yard are the reason for the "chance" visit.
- Youth Works in brother's shop (leather straps, tar, rope, bast shoes, iron); later he joked at Sarov: "we traded in goods that give more profit" [S2 ch.1]. Reads Gospel and spiritual books to friends on holidays. Befriends a Kursk holy fool [S2 ch.1].
- 1777/1778 Leaves home at 17-18 with a release from the Kursk town society. Farewell: they sit, he bows to her feet, she gives him a copper cross, which he wore openly on his chest to the end [S2 ch.1]. Three merchant friends planned to enter a monastery with him [S2 ch.1].
- Before Sarov He walks to Kiev for the relics (Kiev Caves). S3 says the Maiak Life (Georgy) adds a blessing from Elder Dosifei [S1, S3].
- 20 Nov 1778 Arrives at Sarov, eve of the Entry of the Mother of God, under Abbot Pachomius; novice [S1].
- 1778-80 Obediences: bell ringer, prosphora baker, carpenter; carves cypress crosses; makes an altar of cypress for the hospital church with his own hands [S1].
- 1780 Falls ill (dropsy; Avel says about three years) [S1, S4 quoting Avel 1848]. He refuses doctor and bleeding: "I have given myself to the True Physician of souls and bodies, our Lord Jesus Christ and His Most Pure Mother" [S1]. Treasurer Joseph serves vigil and liturgy for him and brings him Communion in his cell. The next night a "divine visit"; he soon recovers [S1]. Georgy's Life: the Mother of God appears and lays hands on his head [S3]. Ioasaf: a deep hollow remained in his side where the fluid drained; Seraphim showed it and his hair fallen off like felt; the hair hung on a nail in his cell [S4 quoting Ioasaf 1849]. Only Ioasaf has the hollow and the hair; treat as reported by one witness.
- 13 Aug 1786 Tonsured Seraphim by Pachomius; spiritual father and sponsor Isaiah [S1, S2 ch.3].
- Dec 1787 (S1) or Dec 1786 (Avel, from archive) Ordained hierodeacon by Bishop Victor of Vladimir [S1, S3]. Serves daily; spends whole days in church; visions during services [S1]. Georgy gives the Great Thursday vision of Christ with heavenly hosts, in Seraphim's own words [S3].
- c.1790s A hermit cell five versts from the monastery for night prayer [S1].
- 2 Sep 1793 Ordained hieromonk by Bishop Theophilus of Tambov [S1].
- Autumn 1794 With swollen legs, asks Pachomius for the desert life. Georgy's manuscript adds: the abbot sent a retired soldier to hit him on the road; Seraphim fell, rose, turned the other cheek; the man did not strike again [S3, from Tikhvin/Lavra manuscript]. Source is a manuscript through a third party. NEW.
- 1794-1804 Hermit in the forest cell. Digs garden beds, cuts wood, reads Gospel, Apostle, Ladder, Basil, Ephraim, Philokalia; ate only potatoes and vegetables, then for about three years only "snit" (a wild herb) [S1, S2 ch.4]. Comes to the monastery on the eve of Sundays and holy days, takes bread for a week, returns at vespers [S1]. First week of Great Lent he stayed in the monastery and ate nothing until Saturday [S1].
- c.1796 Offered the post of archimandrite at Alatyr; begs Isaiah to refuse it for him; another Sarov monk, Abraham, goes [S2 ch.4].
- 1000 days and nights on the stone: one stone in the cell by day, one in the forest by night, hands raised, praying "God, be merciful to me a sinner" [S1, S2 ch.4]. Hair matted like felt, leg disease for life [S1]. Secret: the abbot Nifont, answering a bishop of Tambov, wrote in a draft that nobody knew of "the standing of 1000 days and nights on a stone" [S2 ch.4, from monastery papers]. NEW: the draft reply from Nifont is in the Sarov archive; Elagin cites it.
- Hermit trials: demons lift him in the air and strike him on the floor [S1]; a tree falls on him and bends his back (earlier than the robbers) [S2 ch.4].
- 12 Sep 1804 Robber attack, forest garden/woodcutting. Three peasants demand money. Elagin: he held an axe, could have fought, remembered "all who take the sword shall perish by the sword", put the axe down, crossed his arms: "do what you need" [S2 ch.4]. S1 has him in his vegetable garden. They strike him with the axe butt, beat him, tie him, drag him to the porch, wreck the cell and find only an icon and some potatoes, then flee [S1, S2 ch.4]. Next day he crawls to the monastery during liturgy, bloody, teeth knocked out [S1]. Five months in the monastery (S2). He asks the abbot not to punish the attackers: rather he would leave the monastery [S1, S2]. The men were serfs of landlord Tatishchev from Kremenki; a fire later burned their homes and they came to ask his pardon [S2 ch.4]. He walked bent for life, leaning on an axe, hoe or stick [S2 ch.4].
- 1807 Abbot Isaiah (his spiritual father) dies; Seraphim goes silent about three years (S1) [S1].
- Silence: if someone came to the hut he did not come out; if he met someone in the forest he fell face down until they passed. A brother brought food weekly; they communicated by signs on a tray: bread or cabbage [S2 ch.5]. NEW detail.
- 9 May 1809 (S1) or 1810 (Avel, from archive) Returns to the monastery under Abbot Nifont [S1, S3].
- 1810-1815 Cell enclosure (zatvor): five years almost no visitors; no fire in the cell; hair so matted it was cut off "like a cap"; wore chains; kept an oak coffin in the entry and prayed at it; food left in the entry [S1, S2 ch.5]. Daily rule: a Gospel a day by weekday, a thousand bows, Jesus Prayer [S1].
- Sep 1815 Receives the first outside visitor, the former governor of Tambov [S1]. Seventeen years total in seclusion, partly open [S1].
- 25 Nov 1825 Leaves the cell for the forest on the feast of St Clement. Builds a nearer hut by the Sarovka river, two versts from the monastery. Carries a sack of stones; to the question why: "I torment him who torments me" (after Ephraim the Syrian) [S1]. Daily visitors afterward [S1].
- 9 Dec 1825 Prayer with the Diveyevo sisters in the far hermitage; the start of the mill community (feast of Conception) [S2 ch.7].
- 1825-1827 Builds posts and timber for a mill for the Diveyevo mill community with Sarov horses and Diveyevo sisters; mill grinds 7 July 1827; he sends six sisters to live at it; names Paraskeva Stepanovna as senior, Fr Vasily Sadovsky as confessor [S2 ch.7]. The sisters dig a ditch around the vision site for three years ("canal of the Mother of God"); gooseberries planted on the rampart [S2 ch.7].
- Jan 1831 Anthony Medvedev, hegumen of Vysokogorsk, comes terrified of death; Seraphim says "Divine Providence entrusts you with a vast lavra" (Trinity-St Sergius), "Do not forsake my orphans", "Pray for my parents Isidor and Agafia" [S4 quoting Chichagov 378-381]. Anthony became Lavra abbot and later Philaret's friend and was a main route for the Life reaching print [S3].
- 1831 Cholera: Seraphim cures a Likhachev peasant with the Umilenie icon, holy water and prosphora [S4 quoting Chichagov 395-396].
- 5-9 Sep 1831 Nikolai Motovilov, near paralysed for over three years, is carried to Sarov; 9 Sep he is set under a pine by the Sarovka. Seraphim: "I am not a doctor"; asks whether he believes; then "if you believe, you are already healed" [S4 quoting Motovilov]. Motovilov later served Diveyevo as benefactor and wrote the Conversation [S3, S4].
- 1832 (spring, Lent season) Officer O. V. Ladyzhensky, on his way to China, visits with his sisters; Seraphim grips his sore wrist and the pain goes; he tells him three times "we will not see each other again" [S2 ch.10].
- 25 Dec 1832 (Christmas, one week before death) Bogdanov visits; Seraphim stands leaning on his oak coffin holding a lit wax candle, "extremely cheerful", addressing him as "your God-loving one" [S2 ch.10].
- Late 1832 Tells a brother to blow out a candle: "so they will not see me" [S2 ch.10]. Measures his grave beside the altar of the Dormition cathedral [S1, S2 ch.10].
- 1 Jan 1833 Last liturgy at the hospital church of Sts Zosima and Savvaty. Lights candles at all icons, kisses and blesses everyone: "Save yourselves, do not be discouraged, be watchful; today crowns are being prepared for us." Leaves by the north door [S2 ch.10]. Sends 200 rubles to Diveyevo sister Paraskeva Ivanovna to buy bread, as the sisters had run out [S2 ch.10]. In the evening Brother Pavel hears him singing Easter hymns [S2 ch.10].
- 2 Jan 1833, about 6 a.m. Brother Pavel smells smoke at Seraphim's door; novice Anikita breaks the hook; linen and books on the bench are smouldering. During liturgy a boy whispers the news. The brothers find Seraphim on his knees before the Umilenie icon, in his white robe, copper crucifix on his chest, arms crossed, head bowed, warm. Eyes closed, face "alive with prayer"; the book before him partly scorched [S1, S2 ch.10]. Seraphim had told Pavel: "while I live there will be no fire; when I die, my death will be revealed by a fire" [S2 ch.10].
- Fr Theoktist, sent on his way the day before, has his sleigh fastened break; stops at Diveyevo; the sisters ask him for the memorial service, as Seraphim said "so you will serve in Diveyevo" [S2 ch.10].
- 3-10 Jan 1833 Body lay open eight days in the Dormition cathedral; thousands came; candles by the coffin went out from the heat; buried by Abbot Nifont beside Mark the recluse, with the enamel icon of St Sergius from the Lavra [S2 ch.10]. No speeches were made [S2 ch.10].
- Later, S1 puts the death day as 2 Jan, with the mourning "of people of all ranks from neighbouring provinces" [S1].
- 1834 Autumn Motovilov travels to Kursk to collect memories of Seraphim's family and buy 2000 apple trees for the Diveyevo orchard [S3, quoting Motovilov's 1861 memo]. He wrote notes on Seraphim in 1835-36 [S3].
- 1837 Sergius finishes his Life; 1838 submitted; blocked by Metropolitan Seraphim of St Petersburg, who distrusted the miracles; Philaret of Moscow defended it; Bishop Arseny of Tambov, who had known Seraphim, reviews it in Oct 1840; printed Dec 1841 [S3, S4].
- 1844 Georgy's Life in "Mayak"; 1848 Avel's "Short Spiritual Ladder"; 1849 Ioasaf's "Skazaniya" [S3].
- 1863 Elagin's Life [S2]. 1903 canonization (church-miracles agent).

## 4. Character
- Joy and greeting: he kissed visitors on the lips and said "Christ is risen" at any time of year [S2 ch.5].
- Humility: bowed to the ground to everyone, "poor or rich," kissed the hands of lay people [S1].
- Refused his portrait: "Who am I, poor Seraphim, to allow a portrait?" [S1]. Brother Avel nevertheless printed the first lithograph in October 1843 [S3].
- Called himself "poor Seraphim" ("убогий") [S1; vosp annotation].
- Would not take offence: the robber story and his demand that the abbot not punish the men [S1, S2].
- Humor: on the Ladyzhensky brother's contempt for icons he smiled and said, like a friendly tease, that he, "like you, was mistaken about icons" in the Life of John the Great [S2 ch.10]. NEW.
- Physical strength: he was strong enough to defend himself with an axe in 1804 [S2 ch.4].
- Hospitality to the simple: the peasant whose horse was stolen is told to "guard yourself with silence" and go to a named village [S2 ch.5]. Reported only by hearsay in Sarov.
- Hidden austerities: wore chains, carried stones, kept a coffin; disliked bragging, said "Not to us, Lord, not to us" [S1].
- Memory: he knew the Lives of the Saints in the Menaion so well he retold whole sections [S1].
- Critics: some called him a stumbling block ("some are scandalized by you"); he replied "I am not scandalized that some profit from me and others are scandalized" [S1]. Abbot Isaiah (later, 1849) wrote that Ioasaf's claims "are not in keeping with the truth known to us" [S3]. Metropolitan Seraphim of St Petersburg distrusted miracles in the Life [S3, S4].
- Visited by a brother who asked why they do not live as strictly as the ancient ascetics: "Because we have no resolve" [S2 ch.10].

## 5. Quotations
Original Russian, exact from the source. My English is a translation.
1. "я предал себя Истинному Врачу душ и телес, Господу нашему Иисусу Христу, и Пречистой Его Матери, Преблагословенной Деве Марии." (to Pachomius, who offered a doctor, 1780) [S1]. English: "I have given myself to the True Physician of souls and bodies..."
2. "сей подвижник благочестия сорок семь лет стоял на столпе, а мои труды похожи ли на его подвиг?" (on the stone) [S1]. English: "That ascetic stood forty-seven years on a pillar, and is my labor anything like his?"
3. "когда умиление есть, то с нами Бог." [S1]. English: "When there is tenderness of heart, God is with us."
4. "делайте, что вам надобно" (to the robbers, Elagin's version) [S2 ch.4]. English: "Do what you need."
5. "я, по Ефрему Сирину, томлю томящаго мя." (why he carries stones) [S1]. English: "I torment him who torments me, as Ephraim says."
6. "Не нам, Господи, не нам, но имени Твоему даждь славу" [S1]. Psalm 113, his habit when praised.
7. "Кто я убогий Серафим, чтоб мог позволить снять с себя портрет?" [S1]. English: "Who am I, poor Seraphim, to let a portrait be taken?"
8. "Мы всегда и во всех случаях должны стараться отсекать вины тщеславия в самом начале." [S1]. English: "We must cut off the causes of vanity at the very start."
9. "Я не соблазняюсь тем, что мною одни пользуются, а другие соблазняются." [S1]. English: "I do not mind that some profit from me and others are scandalized."
10. "Нам запрещают жить в пустыне; но первый наш отец, Иоанн Предтеча, был пустынник." [S1]. English: "They forbid us the desert, but our first father John the Forerunner was a hermit."
11. "я, матушка, всю монастырскую жизнь прошел и никогда, даже мыслью, не выходил из монастыря." [S2 ch.7]. English: "I have gone through all monastic life and never, even in thought, left the monastery." (to sister Paraskeva who wanted to leave)
12. "в монастыре живущие борются с противными силами, как бы с голубями, а в пустыне, как со львами и леопардами." [S1]. English: In a monastery you fight the enemy like doves, in the desert like lions and leopards.
13. "В общежительной обители – говорил он – легче справиться с семью девами, чем с одною вдовою." [S2 ch.7]. English: "In a community it is easier to manage seven maidens than one widow." Humor, on separating widows from maidens at Diveyevo.
14. "спасайтесь, не унывайте, бодрствуйте: нынешний день нам венцы готовятся" (last liturgy) [S2 ch.10]. English: "Be saved, do not be discouraged, stay awake; today crowns are being prepared for us."
15. "пока я жив, пожара не будет; а когда я умру, кончина моя откроется пожаром" [S2 ch.10]. English: "While I live there will be no fire; when I die, my death will be revealed by a fire."
16. "вот так и меня не увидят" (after the brother blows out the candle) [S2 ch.10]. English: "So they will not see me either."
17. "если бы решимость имели, то и жили бы так, как отцы, древле просиявшие" [S2 ch.10]. English: "If we had resolve we would live as the fathers of old."
18. "Вот мои родные, а для живых родных я уже живой мертвец" (pointing to icons of Christ and the Mother of God, 1831) [S4 quoting 1863 ed. p.201]. English: "Here are my family; to my living relatives I am already a living dead man."
19. "Нет лучше монашеского жития" [S4 quoting 1863 ed. p.201-202]. English: "There is nothing better than the monastic life." Said with raised hands to Kursk merchants.
20. "радость моя, молю тебя, стяжи дух мирен, и тогда тысячи душ спасутся около тебя" [S4 (vosp. annotation also)]. English: "My joy, I beg you, acquire a peaceful spirit, and thousands around you will be saved."
21. "Да ведь я не доктор." and "А, если вы веруете, – то вы здоровы уже!" (to Motovilov) [S4 quoting Motovilov]. English: "I am not a doctor" and "If you believe, you are already healed."
23. "брат Павел за простоту своего сердца без труда войдет в царствие Божие: он никогда никого не судит и не завидует никому" (on his neighbor Pavel) [S2 ch.10]. English: "Brother Pavel will enter the Kingdom easily for the simplicity of his heart: he never judges anyone."
24. "Христос воскресе" (his greeting to every visitor) [S2 ch.5].
25. "Царя, который меня прославит, того и я прославлю" [S4 sub 1754, unsourced prophecy]. English: "I will glorify the tsar who glorifies me." Tradition only; no early witness cited.
Count: 24 quotations (the numbering skips 22).

## 7. Miracles (witness-reported; status "recorded" means in early Lives or the Sarov archive)
- c.1761 Kursk: fall from the bell tower, unhurt. Reported: mother; S1, S2. Status: recorded Life, popular.
- c.1764 Kursk: healing under the Znamenie icon. Reported: mother, Prokhor himself; S1, S2. Recorded Life. Dream of the Virgin in S2 only.
- 1780 Sarov: recovery after Communion; Virgin appears (Georgy). S1, S3. Recorded Life.
- 1804: healing after the robbers "by the intercession of the Mother of God," as he told one brother [S1].
- 1825-27 Diveyevo: vision of the candle and ditch. Reporter: sister Maria Ivanovna [S2 ch.7]. Recorded Life.
- Bear feeding. Reported by Matrona Pleshcheeva in S2 ch.7, where she tells the sworn secret after eleven years. CONFLICT: S3 reports a Moscow RGB file (Philaret's fund) in which Pleshcheeva on her deathbed said Ioasaf taught her to say she had seen it; Abbot Isaiah (7 Dec 1849) wrote that Ioasaf's "miracles" do not match the truth known at Sarov [S3]. Treat as contested.
- 1831 Cholera cure of a Likhachev peasant [S4 quoting Chichagov].
- 1831 Anthony Medvedev prophecy of the Lavra, and the monk who wept because Seraphim told him "you will soon part from your hegumen" [S4 quoting Chichagov].
- 9 Sep 1831 Motovilov healed. Reporter: Motovilov. Recorded; Motovilov's own memo [S4].
- 1832 Ladyzhensky brother's wrist cured when Seraphim gripped it [S2 ch.10]. Reporter: sister Ekaterina Ladyzhenskaya.
- 1832 Prediction to the Vladimir boy (later monk Vasily): he would enter a monastery and be freed from serfdom; fulfilled [S2 ch.10].
- 1832-33 Foreknowledge of his death: candle, Pavel, grave measured, fire at his door [S2 ch.10].
- Horse thief case [S2 ch.5], hearsay.
- 1861 Philaret: "some places were removed" at the Synod; the vision and healing passages were softened [S3, S4].

## 8. Other
Conflicts between sources
- Birth year: 1759 (S1, S2) versus 1754 (S4: Kursk Ilyinsky church confession book of 1768, age 14, published 1903). Birth day: 19 July (S1/S2) versus 19 June (Georgy in Mayak 1844) [S4]. Use 1754 with a note, as S4 argues.
- Cell move: 1809 (S1) versus 1810 (Avel) [S3].
- Hierodeacon ordination: Dec 1787 (S1) versus Dec 1786 (Avel) [S3].
- Father: Georgy writes "Moshkin", S1/S2 "Moshnin", S4 "Mashnin" [S3, S4]. Brick works: S2 says Isidor owned brickyards; V. Stepashkin finds he was a hired builder, with no brickyards [S4].
- Georgy's manuscript gives the first leave of Kursk at 15, S1/Maiak at 18 [S3].
- Dating of the Georgy Life: Rudi says it is an edit of Sergius's longer text; the editor of S3 doubts that [S3].
- Ioasaf Life: partly invented according to Abbot Isaiah, Philaret, and Chichagov, but Chichagov's chronicle still reuses material that was confirmed by Motovilov [S3].
- The "Conversation with Motovilov" on acquiring the Holy Spirit is disputed: published 1903 by Nilus; authenticity doubted by Sanidopoulos; others trace it to Motovilov [S5, S3]. Do not present it as an eyewitness account without that note.
- Authorship of the Life attributed to Motovilov (ed. Strizhev 1993) was disproved [S3].

Witness notes useful for the writer
- Four early Lives by people who knew him: Sergius (15 years at Sarov), Georgy (5 years), Avel (3), Ioasaf/Ivan Tikhonov (12) [S3]. All four left Sarov and wrote at the Lavra, Bethany or Nizhny, not at Sarov [S3].
- The eyewitness Bishop Arseny of Tambov (1840): visited Seraphim in his hermit cell and "even in his special prayer closet which could hold one person standing," in Seraphim's last year [S3 quoting Arseny].
- First attempted printing: Philaret's letters to Anthony give a view of the censorship fight [S3, S4].
