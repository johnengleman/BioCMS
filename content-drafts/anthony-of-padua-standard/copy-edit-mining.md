## Word changes the checker may want to see

- "the Father" / "the same Father" (the source's name for Saint Anthony) is now "Saint Anthony" in four places: the shipwreck account, c15 (Margarita), c19, d06.
- Church words: "Compline" is now "night prayer" (shipwreck); "refectory" is now "dining hall" (c02); "oratory" is now "chapel" (x12); "habit" is now "robe" (x17, d22, d27). Two short explanations added, each in a sentence of its own: "An antiphon is a short verse sung in church." (x08, first use of the word) and "The octave is the eighth day after a feast." (d10).
- l27: "The ancient enemy" is now "The devil" (the account's heading and quotation name him).
- l24: "the guardian" is now "the friar in charge of the house" (the same friar named two sentences before).
- x06: "After three days the Virgin kept her promise of freedom" came before any promise. Now "The Virgin promised her freedom and kept that promise after three days." The promise was already in the text.
- d13: "Then he came to the tabernacle" is now "At Padua he came to the tabernacle". The first paragraph of d13 already places the visit at Padua.
- Closing note: "the Liber miraculorum" is now "the collection of about 1367", the name the rest of the list uses for it. This adds the number 1367 once.
- "light" for eyesight is now "sight" in three items of c17 (Theodoric, Auriema, Flos de Gemma). d18 keeps "the light of his eyes" because it plays on "the light of faith".

## Listed, not fixed (a fix needs a fact from the source)

1. "Chest" (c11 onward: "went up to the chest", "the keepers of the chest", "under the Saint's chest") is never explained. A reader does not know it is the tomb. One sentence at c11 would fix it, if the checker confirms the wording (for example, the stone chest that held his body).
2. l22: "a woman in the same country". The country belongs to the account before it in the source, not to l21 (Rome). Name the country or drop the phrase.
3. l32: "The husband climbed to the same window to test it. From then on he was a special friend of the servant of God." The text does not say what he heard. If the source says he heard the voice too, add it.
4. d23: "She found the table still set as if no one had slept there." A table and sleeping do not fit together. Check the Latin (beds?).
5. x15: "The date was 15 April, as a tablet ... shows." It is not clear which event the date belongs to (the attack, the rescue, or the first words).
6. x18: "The damage was counted at a full million." A million of what? Give the unit if the source has it.
7. x08: "The purse ... fell through the sail" is a hard picture to see. Check the Latin.

9. d16: "Then grapes grew on them, flowered, and ripened." Grapes do not flower; the source order may be "flowered, bore grapes, and ripened". Check the Latin.
