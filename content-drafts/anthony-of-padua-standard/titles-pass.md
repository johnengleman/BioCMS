# Titles pass, Saint Anthony of Padua

Done on 2026-10-05 for `biography.html` and `summary.txt`. Originals are `*.before-titles`. `miracles.html` and `entry.json` were not touched. Quotations, block quotes, the sources list, and the work title "Life of Blessed Anthony" are unchanged. The check script shows no MUST FIX.

## Names left plain, and why

- Pope Pius XII, Pope Gregory IX, Pope Benedict XVI: not canonized.
- Jean Rigauld, Julian of Speyer, Bartholomew of Trent, Brother Elias, Brother Gratian, Friar Monaldo, Tiso, Prince Pedro, Ezzelino, Bartolomeo Corradini: not canonized or not known as saints.
- Place and church names with "St" keep their form: St Vincent (the house), St Jacques, St Junien, St Mary's, St Benedict's, and "St Anthony" (the small place near Coimbra).
- Old Testament and Gospel figures (Abraham, Gideon, Pilate, Judas, Lazarus) keep their names. The Virgin keeps her title.

## Choices to know about

- Fernando: given once as a fact ("His baptismal name was Fernando"). Everywhere else in the life he is "Saint Anthony", including the headings. Heading ids are unchanged, because entry.json may refer to them.
- Name change at Olivais: "he took the name Anthony and asked to be called by it" and "As a friar called Anthony he was not". The bare name stays here because it is the name itself, not a reference to the man.
- The five martyrs of Morocco are now "Saint Berard, Saint Otto, Saint Peter, Saint Adjutus, and Saint Accursius" (canonized 1481). Please confirm you want the title on all five.
- "Life of Blessed Anthony" (Rigauld's title) is unchanged. Descriptive references ("An early Life of Saint Anthony", "the first Life of Saint Anthony", "his Life of Saint Anthony") now carry the title, including the `<cite>` lines.
- "Father Francis" (the Pius XII paraphrase) became "Saint Francis". "Blessed Anthony" in the 1231 law became "Saint Anthony".
- The Tony rhyme now reads "asks Saint Anthony, called Tony in the rhyme, to turn around".
- Sentences the title pushed over 30 words were split: the Pentecost opening, the burial-place sentence at Padua, and the sentence on the first Life.
- Heading "Fernando Becomes a Friar Named Anthony" became "Saint Anthony Becomes a Friar".
