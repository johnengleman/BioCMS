In 1220 a young canon in Portugal, who knew the Bible by heart, heard that five friars had been killed for Christ in Morocco. From then on he wanted one thing: to give his whole life to Christ at once, as they had. He put on their robe, gave up the name his parents had chosen for him, and sailed for Africa to die. The world knows him now as Saint Anthony of Padua, a Doctor of the Church and the saint people ask for lost things. But what does God do with a life offered to him all at once, when he has other plans for it?
