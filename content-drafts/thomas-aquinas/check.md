# Thomas Aquinas: check and fixes (2026-10-01)

Originals kept as `biography.html.before-check`, `summary.txt.before-check`, `entry.json.before-check`.

Checked against raw text: Fontes vitae fasc. 1 (Calo, Tocco, Gui) and fasc. 4 (Naples process 1319, Documenta, Latin bull) from the archive.org djvu text; the Latin Summa, SCG, De ente, Creed and Commandments sermons, Lauda Sion, Adoro te and the Corpus Thomisticum index from corpusthomisticum.org; Rickaby 1905 (archive.org); the FSSPX translation of the bull; Aeterni Patris and Cum hoc sit (Vatican); Conway; Wikipedia (Aquinas, Canonization, Homo unius libri).

## Errors fixed

1. **Straw chain (biography ch. 1 and ch. 10, Note on the Sources, quote 20 source, gaps, summary).** The Naples process (art. LXXIX, pp. 377-378) says Reginald confessed the story in his last illness to John del Giudice of Anagni, and John told Bartholomew at Anagni shortly before Boniface VIII was seized (Sept. 1303); Bartholomew then told Tocco and Benedict XI. The draft said Reginald told Bartholomew directly, and the Note called the 1303 chain "not checked". Now stated and attributed.
2. **Order of the straw scene (ch. 1, ch. 10).** In the Latin, "non possum" and the short "straw" reply come first; the visit to the countess follows; the oath and the full sentence ("...respectu eorum que vidi") come after the visit. Ch. 1 had the oath at the chapel; reordered. Ch. 10 now says so, and no longer calls the answer "three-word". "Shook him" by the cape changed to "pulled him sharply" ("traxit per cappam").
3. **Catherine of Morra (ch. 2, miracles m12).** The process calls her "neptis" (niece, daughter of Thomas's sister Mary), not great-niece. Fixed.
4. **Misplaced quotation (ch. 8).** "I had thought long on a question... a fine answer" is said in the cardinal-legate scene (Calo; Tocco), not at Louis's table. At the table Thomas begs the king's pardon (Tocco). Fixed and the cardinal scene added.
5. **Mule at the bier (ch. 13, miracles m31).** "Tocco, and only Tocco" was wrong: Calo, Tocco and Gui all tell it. Same for the stars and the comet at his death (m27) and Paul of Aquila's dream (m28). Labels corrected.
6. **Invented or unsupported detail removed.** "Albert turned to the room" (Tocco: "fertur... dixisse", no gesture); "a night when" for the candle (Tocco: in his room, dictating on the Trinity, to his scribe); the monks' wood "embarrassed him" (he protested); Francesca "could not enter because women were not allowed" (Tocco: "non audebat"); "two of her women" (two others); the funeral sermon "closed the secret" (now Tocco's own link to the belt); "Tocco chose his words with care".
7. **Dumb ox "it is said" (ch. 5, Note).** Tocco states the nickname as fact; "fertur" attaches to Albert's words only. Reworded.
8. **"What is God?" (ch. 1, ch. 2, Note, gaps).** It is in Calo only, not "marked as legend by the old writer". Reworded and attributed.
9. **Bartholomew's portrait (ch. 9).** He saw Thomas outside the cloister twice (once at Capua); the day's order and the "cheerful, mild, gentle" face are what others told him; "one meal a day" is John Blasi's, not James of Caiazzo's; "cared nothing for fine food or clothes" is Tocco's testimony. Reassigned.
10. **Isaiah scene (ch. 8).** Reginald pleaded first, then Thomas named Peter and Paul, then forbade him to tell. Order fixed.
11. **Consistory (ch. 14).** One speech to the cardinals, not "a second consistory... then more". Merged.
12. **Bull (ch. 14).** After the Veni Creator the pope announced the canonization and the bull was issued the same day (Wikipedia; the bull is dated 18 July 1323); the draft said the bull was "read". Child cured "without an operation" (bull), not "the surgeon's knife".
13. **Patronage 1880 (ch. 1, ch. 14, entry patron).** Cum hoc sit names universities, academies, lyceums and Catholic schools; "every Catholic university" overstated. Reworded.
14. **Absolutes and inflation.** "Kings asked his advice" (only Louis IX is sourced); "argued every question by first setting out the strongest case" (each article opens with objections); "one of his first works" (De ente, date not checked); "Os iusti ... almost the same words" as SCG's opening (now "a verse that begins the same way"); "Tocco, Calo and Gui... none doubts" (age at the habit, family's fight); "never used a boastful word" now attributed to the witness; Peckham named as the Franciscan; Jeremy Taylor 1649 now "the earliest attribution found"; the Fondi date conflict (Conway 1304, Wikipedia 1348) added to the Note; "36 quotations from his works" in the research paragraph now says they include listener reports.
15. **Prayers (Note, prayers_note, gaps).** Corpus Thomisticum lists Creator ineffabilis under unknown authors, and prints Concede michi as "authenticitate dubia", only from Tocco's last (1323) recension (Wilmart 1932 judged it spurious). The earlier note ("not found in Tocco") was explained by the Fontes edition carrying an earlier recension.
16. **Miracles (entry.json).** m17 tooth: it was an extra tooth that grew in overnight, told by Tocco in 1319 from Count Thomas of San Severino and Ptolemy. m25 star: John Coppa alone testified; his brother Bonfilius (dead by 1319) is named as a second witness. m41: Gui no. 87 (archbishop's leg ulcer, then fever) and no. 89 (Mary, wife of the pope's nephew Arnald, dropsy) are sourced, not "unsourced". m42: the child was baptized before it revived. m44: "the only raising of the dead" was wrong (Gui no. 89 also). m40: they called on Mary, Dominic, Peter Martyr, and last Thomas. m26: Abbot Nicholas reported hearsay.
17. **Summary and tagline.** Straw saying attributed to the sworn account; summary kept at 120 words. Did-you-know "two months" changed to "less than two months".
18. **Ending (ch. 14).** "the question that began to be answered for him in the chapel" was a claim about his private experience. Now a plain line about the veil. Corpus Christi hymns sentence tied to the Fossanova profession of faith, not to "senses fail".
