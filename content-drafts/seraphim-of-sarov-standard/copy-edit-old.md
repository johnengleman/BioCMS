## Listed, not fixed

- biography.html, ch. "Saint Seraphim Opens His Door to Everyone": "His first biographer counts some seventeen years shut away by 1825". From 1810 to 1825 is fifteen years, and the heading before it says fifteen. This is a fact question for the checker, not a wording fix.
- biography.html: the opening says "Robbers had beaten him years before, and he was bent for life"; ch. 3 says "a falling tree also struck him and bent his back". Both are facts from the sources. A reader may wonder which one bent him. Not changed.
- biography.html, ch. 1: "fools for Christ" is not explained in the biography (miracles.html explains it). Adding a definition is outside a word-only edit, so I left it for the writer.
- biography.html, ch. 1: "to pray at the bodies of the saints in the Caves monastery". Kept "bodies" because the word "relics" is explained only later, in the 1903 chapter.
- miracles.html, m49: "the icon of the Mother of God called Tender Mercy". The biography and m25 call Saint Seraphim's icon "Tenderness". It may be the same icon (Umilenie) in another translation; I cannot confirm, so I did not make the names match.
- miracles.html, m29: "Father Philaret of Glinsk". He may be one of the Glinsk elders that the Russian Church glorified. I was not sure, so I left the name without "Saint" (titles-pass rule).
- Quotations that differ between the two files (not edited): the Mother of God's words are "This one is of Our kin" (biography) and "This is one of my kind" (miracles m06); Saint Seraphim's words to Pavel are "my death will be revealed by a fire" (biography) and "a fire will reveal my death" (miracles m25); the Mantirov and dropsy quotations also have different wording in each file. A checker may want one translation for each.
- Quotation that reads hard (not edited): Saint Isaac the Syrian, "Love the leisure of silence before the feeding of the hungry in the world."
- miracles.html, m07: the script still flags "I, poor Seraphim, will pray" as a bent sentence. It is inside a quotation, so it stays.
