# Seraphim of Sarov: scene outline

Source keys carry a file prefix: OW = notes-own-words.md, W = notes-witnesses.md, CM = notes-church-miracles.md. [W-S2 ch.10] = Elagin 1863, ch.10. Dates are Julian.

## 1. The driving question

Seraphim wanted God alone, and for thirty years he sought Him by going away from people: the forest, a stone, silence, a locked cell with a coffin. Illness, a beating that bent him for life, and brothers who found him "hard to look at" [OW-S2] stood in the way. In 1825, at the word of the Mother of God, he opened his door, and the man who had fallen face down to avoid a passer-by kissed every visitor and called each one "my joy": what he had been given was "not for you alone, but through you for the whole world" [OW-S1 Q13].

He is famous as Russia's best-loved saint, whose glorification in 1903 drew hundreds of thousands and the tsar himself [CM-S2; CM-S4]. He lived the desert life of the ancient fathers in an age when "they forbid us the desert" [W-S1 Q10]. And he bowed to the ground before rich and poor alike [W-S1].

## 2. Portrait

A Kursk merchant's son who kept the merchant's talk: grace is "capital", virtues are goods "that give more profit" [OW-S1 Q3-Q4]. Strong enough to fight three men with an axe, he chose not to [W-S2 ch.4]. Severe with himself (chains, stones, coffin, a thousand bows a day) [W-S1; OW-S2], he rebuked others "meekly" [OW-S2]. Visitors met joy: a kiss, "Christ is risen!" in any season, "my joy" [W-S2 ch.5; CM-S2]. He teased: he asked Motovilov about the perfume "on purpose" [OW-S1 Q12]; he joked about "seven maidens and one widow" [W-S2 ch.7]; the Diveyevo sisters said he bore persecution "even laughing" and joked at them [OW-S3]. He refused credit and portraits: "Who am I, poor Seraphim?" [W-S1 Q7]. Some brothers resented him [OW-S2; OW-S3].

With God: standing prayer, the publican's prayer, the Jesus Prayer "walking and sitting" [OW-S3 Q19]. "When there is tenderness of heart, God is with us" [W-S1 Q3]. "The Lord seeks a heart full of love for God and neighbor; that is the throne where He likes to sit" [OW-S1 Q14].

## 3. The opening

Hook: a broken picture, silence then joy.

> For three years after his spiritual father died, the hermit of the Sarov forest spoke to no one. If anyone came to his hut, he did not come out. If he met someone on a forest path, he fell face down and lay there until they had gone by. Once a week a brother brought food, and their whole conversation was what lay on a tray: bread, or cabbage. Fifteen years later the same man, bent almost double, stood at his door for crowds of strangers, kissed each one, and greeted them in every season with the Easter words: "Christ is risen! My joy!"

Facts: [W-S2 ch.5; W-S1; W-S2 ch.4; CM-S2]. Answered in chapter 7 (the door opens), fully in chapter 10 ("through you for the whole world"), closed in chapter 13.

## 4. Chapters

1. **The boy under the icon** — SC-01, SC-02. Ends: he leaves with his mother's copper cross.
2. **The novice who refused the doctor** — SC-03. Ends: a priest, he asks for the forest.
3. **A thousand nights on a stone** — SC-04. Ends: three peasants hear he has money.
4. **He laid the axe down** — SC-05. Ends: Isaiah, his spiritual father, dies.
5. **Bread or cabbage, no words** — SC-06. Ends: he is ordered back inside the walls.
6. **A coffin in the doorway** — SC-07. Ends: one visitor in 1815; ten more years behind the door.
7. **The door opens at last** — SC-08. Ends: he is given a mill community of poor girls.
8. **A rich man made poor** — SC-09. Ends: the mill grinds; some brothers object.
9. **Hard to look at** — SC-10. Ends: a crippled landowner is carried to the Sarovka.
10. **Light in the falling snow** — SC-11. Ends: "not for you alone"; he begins to speak of his grave.
11. **Crowns being prepared** — SC-12, SC-13. Ends: eight days of crowds, then the grave.
12. **The tsar carries the coffin** — SC-14. Ends: the shrine is opened and the relics vanish.
13. **A candle lit at Diveyevo** — SC-15. Ends: back to the opening.

## 5. The scenes

### SC-01 — The icon in the rain, c. 1764 (Kursk, the Moshnin yard)
- What happens: rain turns a procession with the icon of the Sign through the yard; his mother holds the sick boy under it; he recovers.
- Why it is here: the Mother of God enters the story; she returns at each turn.
- Have: [W-S1; W-S2 ch.1; OW-S2]; age, icon name [CM-S4; CM-S1].
- Need: Elagin's Russian on the dream and the rain detour; Sergius's wording of the healing.
- Testimony: Elagin ch.1, procession passage.
- Where to look: W-S2 ch.1; W-S1.

### SC-02 — The copper cross, 1777 or 1778 (Kursk)
- What happens: after years in his brother's shop he takes leave; he bows to her feet; she gives him a copper cross.
- Why it is here: he leaves the trade; the cross is on his chest at death.
- Have: [W-S2 ch.1; OW-S1 Q4].
- Need: Elagin's exact farewell; any words of his mother.
- Where to look: W-S2 ch.1.

### SC-03 — The True Physician, 1780 (Sarov)
- What happens: swollen with dropsy, he refuses the doctor; Joseph brings Communion; the Mother of God comes with John and Peter. Later he has Ksenia put her hand in the hollow left in his body.
- Why it is here: he first stakes his life on God alone.
- Have: [W-S1 Q1; OW-S2; OW-S3; CM-S1; W-S3]; hollow [OW-S3; W-S4].
- Need: Chichagov's text of the vision and of Ksenia; words of the Mother of God here.
- Where to look: OW-S3 early chapter.

### SC-04 — A thousand nights on a stone, c. 1794–1807 (forest)
- What happens: hands raised on a stone, "God, be merciful to me a sinner"; a brother objects; he answers with Symeon's 47 years.
- Why it is here: the summit of his search for God alone.
- Have: [OW-S2; W-S1 Q2, Q3; W-S2 ch.4]; Nifont's "nobody knew" [W-S2 ch.4]; 17 hours a day [CM-S4].
- Need: Sergius's full Russian exchange with the brother; wording of Nifont's draft.
- Testimony: Sergius 1841, told by Seraphim himself.
- Where to look: W-S1; W-S2 ch.4.

### SC-05 — The axe laid down, 12 Sept 1804 (forest cell)
- What happens: he puts the axe down: "Do what you need." Next day he crawls into the liturgy, bloody. He would rather leave Sarov than see them punished; later they ask pardon.
- Why it is here: hard part; the bent back; forgiveness in action.
- Have: [W-S2 ch.4 Q4; W-S1; OW-S2; CM-S1].
- Need: Sergius's words of his plea; Elagin on the fire and the pardon.
- Where to look: W-S2 ch.4.

### SC-06 — Bread or cabbage, 1807–1810 (forest)
- What happens: total silence; face down before passers-by; the weekly tray.
- Why it is here: the withdrawal that 1825 reverses.
- Have: [W-S2 ch.5; W-S1].
- Need: Elagin's exact tray passage; any reason given for the silence.
- Where to look: W-S2 ch.5.

### SC-07 — The coffin in the entry, 1810–1825 (monastery cell)
- What happens: no visitors, no fire, a Gospel a day, a thousand bows; prayer at an oak coffin.
- Why it is here: total enclosure; the coffin waits for his last Christmas.
- Have: [OW-S2; W-S1; W-S2 ch.5].
- Need: how food reached him; name of the first visitor (1815).
- Where to look: W-S1.

### SC-08 — The door opens, 25 Nov 1825 (cell, the Sarovka)
- What happens: he asks God to confirm the step; the Mother of God gives him Diveyevo; he walks out with a sack of stones and greets the crowds.
- Why it is here: answers the opening.
- Have: [OW-S2; OW-S3; CM-S4; W-S1 Q5; W-S2 ch.5; CM-S2].
- Need: wording of his request for confirmation; one named, dated visitor and the greeting.
- Where to look: OW-S3; W-S1.

### SC-09 — Mantirov gives everything, 1823–1827 (Sarov, Diveyevo)
- What happens: he heals Mantirov's legs, then says: give all you have to the Lord. The money builds the mill; the sisters carry timber.
- Why it is here: a miracle that changed the life; joy becomes a community.
- Have: [OW-S3 Q24, Q25; CM-S4; W-S2 ch.7].
- Need: year of the healing; Mantirov's answer and gift.
- Testimony: Chichagov's Mantirov passage.
- Where to look: OW-S3.

### SC-10 — "Some are scandalized", 1825–1832 (Sarov)
- What happens: brothers resent him and Diveyevo; he does not mind; he laughs.
- Why it is here: the cost of the open door.
- Have: [OW-S2; OW-S3 Evdokia; W-S1 Q9].

### SC-11 — Light in the falling snow, Nov 1831 (forest clearing)
- What happens: the aim of life is the Holy Spirit; he grips Motovilov's shoulders; his face outshines the noon sun; peace, warmth, the perfume, the smile.

### SC-12 — Leaning on his coffin, Christmas 1832 (cell)

### SC-13 — Found kneeling, 1–2 Jan 1833 (Sarov)

### SC-14 — The tsar carries the coffin, 17–19 July 1903 (Sarov)
- What happens: bones, not a whole body; the tsar's "true joy"; the crowds; Anna Ilyeva, blind 19 years, sees.

### SC-15 — The candle, 1 Aug 1991 (Diveyevo)

## 6. What we leave out

- The bear: Pleshcheeva later said Ioasaf taught her the story [W-S3; CM-S4].
- Prophecies on Russia, the "Diveyevo secret", the tsar's letter: Motovilov via Nilus, "extremely doubtful" [OW-S6].
- Holy fool's prophecy at age three; soldier sent to strike him: late or third-hand [W-S4; W-S3].
- Levitation, the abbesses, the light seen at Glinsk: thin or legend [OW-S3; CM-S1].
- 1903 Old Believers punished: weak and harsh [CM-S7].
- Spring stories, the empress and the heir: miracles section [CM-S6].
- Putin, Soyuz, Pope Francis, nuclear patronage: other sections [CM-S4; CM-S5].
- "Nastavleniya": the Fathers, not his words [OW-S4].

## 7. Accuracy notes

- Birth 1754 (Kursk records) vs 1759 (early Lives, grave). Use 1754 with a note.
- Return 1809 or 1810; deacon 1786 or 1787; tonsure 13 or 18 Aug 1786.
- Conversation: "recorded by Motovilov, published 1903"; authenticity debated [OW-S5; OW-S6].
- "Acquire a peaceful spirit": modern compilations only [OW-S8; W-S4].
- The hollow: thigh (Chichagov) or side (Ioasaf, called unreliable) [W-S3].
- Fall at seven: "from the height of the building", no bell tower [CM-S4].
- Relics opened 17 Dec 1920, not "miraculously not found" [CM-S3; CM-S4].
- Crowds 1903: 100,000 to 300,000.
- Names: Prokhor Moshnin; Agafia; Mantirov (Manturov, Mantorov); Motovilov.
