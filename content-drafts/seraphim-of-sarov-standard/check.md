# Fact check: Saint Seraphim of Sarov (heart draft, 2026-10-06)

Files checked: `biography.html`, `summary.txt`. Backups: `biography.html.before-check-heart`, `summary.txt.before-check-heart`. The earlier report is `check-old.md`. Sources: the saved texts in `sources/` (Elagin ch. 1–5, 7, 10; Chichagov Zhitie and Letopis ch. 21, 25; Sergius; Khronika; Beseda; Rannie; the Synod Act; ru Wikipedia; Sanidopoulos pieces; Zolotov; Orthodox America). Lint: no MUST FIX (after the fixes below).

## Errors of fact fixed in the text

- Ch. 1, the shop: "On feast days he read the Gospel aloud to his friends." Elagin ch. 1, para 8 says he read spiritual books and sometimes gathered friends to listen. Now "books about the faith".
- Ch. 3, Saint Alexandra: "the Queen of Heaven herself would remind him of it". Chichagov para 21 says she herself would "instruct" him ("наставить"). Now "would tell him what to do". Her title is confirmed from ru Wikipedia (venerable, glorified 2000; world name Agafia Semyonovna Melgunova, a colonel's widow; died 13 June 1789). Added to note 22 and the Sources list.
- Ch. 7, Mantirov: "the skirts of his coat" is Mantirov's coat (Chichagov para 69, "фалды сюртука"). Fixed.
- Ch. 8: "Now one of them told him" tied the later complaint to the earlier group of brothers. Sergius para 28 says only "one brother". Fixed. The two monks "who had doubted him" had "reproached" him (Chichagov para 97, "зазирали"). Fixed.
- Ch. 8: Ksenia "bare" name next to Ksenia the church-woman. The refusal scene now says "Ksenia Mikhailovna". The two are different people in the sources (Chichagov para 16, a church-woman; Chichagov para 75, the head of Diveyevo).
- Ch. 9, Motovilov: "more than an inch and a half of snow" for "более вершка" (a vershok is 1.75 inches). Now "an inch and three-quarters".
- Ch. 9: "the old monk remembered the shop in Kursk" invented a memory. Now "he spoke of his own life" (Beseda para 71).
- Ch. 9: "the prayer of poor Seraphim" was a paraphrase of his own words, without quotation marks. Now quoted: "the humble request of poor Seraphim" (Beseda para 109).
- Ch. 2: Sarov silence: "Before long he stopped coming" to the monastery. Elagin ch. 5, para 4 puts this near the end of the three years. Fixed.
- Ch. 4: "the bishops wanted to make him head of a monastery". Elagin ch. 4, para 76 says "the Church authorities". Fixed.
- Ch. 4: "that could mean only one thing" (absolute). Softened.
- Ch. 6: "he kissed their hands" for all lay people. Sergius para 28 says "many". Fixed.
- Ch. 10: Pavel's call at the door stopped before the end of the quotation: Elagin ch. 10, para 100 adds "The elder must have gone out to the forest." Added inside the quotation marks.
- Ch. 10: "The book before him was a little scorched." The book is mentioned only after the brothers sorted the cell, and "the book before which he died" (Elagin ch. 10, para 101). Rewritten, in plain words ("died").
- Ch. 11: the emperor wrote "on their report" (the bishops'). The Act says he wrote on the Synod's report (26 January). Rewritten.
- Ch. 11: "wanted the question of glorification as a saint settled". The Act: he wished the Synod's work toward glorification to be brought to a conclusion (19 July 1902). Rewritten, with a plain explanation of "glorify".
- Ch. 11: the crowd count (100,000; 200,000 to 300,000) belonged to the celebrations (17 to 20 July; Sanidopoulos gives the larger count for 18 July), not to the single day of 19 July. Reworded.
- Ch. 11: "found among Motovilov's papers and printed" the same year. Nothing says the year of the find; Strizhev says Nilus received the box in 1902 and printed in May 1903. Reworded.
- Ch. 12: "In 1927 the Diveyevo convent was closed. Sarov was shut too." The Sarov monastery was shut in 1926 (Sanidopoulos, Translation); Diveyevo in 1927. Fixed.
- A Note on the Sources: the Kursk register was not "published in 2002"; an article of 2002 quotes it (Khronika para 518). The bear story rests on a sister and a monk, not one sister (ru Wikipedia). Fixed.
- How This Life Was Researched: Elagin's Life is "the fullest", not "the second" (Sergius 1841, Georgy 1844, Ioasaf 1849 came before). Count raised from 22 to 23 sources (ru Wikipedia on Alexandra).

## The copy editor's 14 items

1. Two Ksenias: different people. Fixed as above.
2. Coat: Mantirov's. Fixed.
3. "poor Seraphim": now quoted. Fixed.
4. "town society of Kursk": Elagin ch. 1, para 11 says he took the release "following the Church and civil rules of the Spiritual Regulation". Now said plainly.
5. "would one day show him": see Saint Alexandra above.
6. "wanted the matter settled": see ch. 11 above.
7. "like Saint Seraphim's mother": confirmed (both named Agafia: Sergius para 3; Chichagov paras 20 and 22). Kept.
8. "desert hermits": confirmed. Sergius para 16: "in imitation of the ancient desert-dwellers" (пустынножителям). Kept.
9. Far and near huts: confirmed. The far hut is the old forest hut; the near hut is the new one by the spring (Sergius para 26; Chichagov para 78; Beseda para 49). Kept.
10. Grave: the sources say "at the side of the altar" (Elagin ch. 10, paras 91 and 106; Sergius para 32). They do not say outside. Now "at the side of its altar" and "on the right side of the cathedral's altar".
11. The book: fixed (above).
12. "His heart was not in it" is in Elagin ch. 1, para 8. Kept. "remembered the shop": fixed.
13. Odd quotations: "Do what you need" is "делайте, что вам надобно". Now "Do what you need to do." The other is "Я не соблазняюсь тем, что мною одни пользуются, а другие соблазняются." Now "I am not upset that some people profit from me and others take offense."
14. Same day: confirmed. Sergius para 26, Chichagov paras 74 and 77, and Khronika para 831 give 25 November 1825 for both the vision and the first walk to the forest. Kept.

## The writer's plan.md items

- Agafia's burial, February 1800: confirmed. Khronika para 518 quotes the parish register of the Akhtyrka church (the entry reads "29 February 1800", a date that did not exist; the Khronika also doubts her age) and says Elagin's mention of a visit to her grave is a mistake. We follow the register, as before.
- Pachomius: the blessing, the age of seventy, the death in 1794, and the promise are in Elagin ch. 4, paras 4 to 7 and Chichagov paras 25 to 28. The 1789 deathbed charge of Saint Alexandra is in Chichagov paras 20 to 22 and Elagin ch. 3, paras 6 to 8. Confirmed.
- Mantirov: expelled, walked, died 7 July 1858, sixty years old, buried 9 July on the left side of the Christmas church. Confirmed (Letopis ch. 21, paras 17 to 18; ch. 25, para 29, 30).
- "Spoke very fast" is gone; "woke the brothers" is correct (Sergius para 8: будильщик).
- Translations: all checked against the Russian. No change except the three above.
