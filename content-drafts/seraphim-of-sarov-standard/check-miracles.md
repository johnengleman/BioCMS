# Check of miracles.html (Seraphim of Sarov)

Original saved as `miracles.html.before-check`. Fixes made in place. Quotations and facts were checked against the raw text of Sergius 1841, Elagin ch. 1, 4, 5, 7, 10, Chichagov 1903, the Khronika zhizni, Russian Wikipedia, the Synod Act, Strizhev, the early-Lives article, OrthoChristian 147491 (Malleev-Pokrovsky), Sanidopoulos (Brookline, spring, relics, candle, authenticity) and Orthodox America 105.

## Removed

- `demons-attack-the-hermit`: Sergius tells it as a temptation by the devil, and Chichagov adds that God allowed it. No source calls it a miracle, and the notes list it under hermit trials, not miracles. Cut.
- `m55` (healed peasant, "drink from my spring"): the quotation belongs to the anonymous Moscow woman of m54. The source gives no peasant. The words now stand in m54. List is now 63 accounts, not 65.

## Facts corrected

- `holy-fool-foretells-prokhors-future`: source was Russian Wikipedia, which does not tell it. It is in the Khronika zhizni (c. 1757, citing Diveyevo sources). Source line and wording fixed. The prophecy words follow the chronicle.
- `m02`: Russian Wikipedia says the tower did not exist in 1761. It does not call the detail "legend". Cut "treats the detail as legend".
- `m04`: quotation completed ("our Lord Jesus Christ and His Most Pure Mother"). Communion was "brought to his cell" (Sergius), not brought by Joseph. Ioasaf's hollow is in the side, and Chichagov's is in the thigh: the status line now says so.
- `m06`: "told one brother only" was an absolute claim. Sergius says he told one of the brothers. Sergius puts him in the garden and Elagin in the forest: added to status.
- `appearance-in-1815`: Chichagov says the Lord commanded it by a new appearance of the Mother of God. He does not give her words. Sergius says only "the former governor", not that Seraphim opened the door himself. Fixed.
- `m07`: "My joy" restored to the quotation. Year "about 1823" kept, with the Wikipedia source and the note that Chichagov gives none. The money is tied to the Nativity church only, as Chichagov says.
- `m08`: Wikipedia calls the Diveyevo instructions "tradition" and does not mention a "plan". Status fixed.
- `m09`: the candle stood behind the sisters' cell at Diveyevo, not behind Seraphim's cell. Elagin says the earth was found dug at daybreak and that Seraphim then blessed the ditch.
- `tree-bows-to-the-sisters`: Chichagov's reason is to show everyone that God approved his care for the community. Gury and Georgy found him cutting a fallen pine.
- `m10`: Anastasius is a monk who was to ask the Mother of God to give the water healing power. He was not told to ask the water for healing.
- `m11`: order of events fixed (words first, then the water). Source says the illness left at once and never returned.
- `m12`, `m13`: Evdokia and Ksenia Kuzminichna told their own stories. Added who sent whom, the rake, and Pavel's hands on the face. Removed "go out and rake".
- `m14`: details of the route fixed (four houses, small gate, lead the horse out in silence). Added the Sarov rumor.
- `m15`: the witness is the princess's nephew, not the princess. He saw it, was told to keep silent until Seraphim died, and told it afterward. Removed "We have not read her full account".
- `m16`: the two nuns had died. Seraphim prayed three days. Kept the short toll-house explanation (accurate).
- `m17`: "sworn secret" is not in Elagin. Seraphim told her to wait eleven years after his death. Ioasaf 1849 also tells it (Wikipedia). Moscow file and Isaiah letter confirmed in the early-Lives article.
- `m18`: Brookline says "virgin martyrs and monastic saints". Added Chichagov's note that Evdokia Efremovna recorded 25 March 1831.
- `1830` account: Chichagov's words are "Beloved! Ask of me whatever you wish." Restored "My beloved".
- `m19`: Anthony was troubled by thoughts of his death, not "afraid". Chichagov says "a vast lavra", and in March 1831 he was named to run the Trinity-St. Sergius Lavra (the chronicle says vicar). The monk wept on the road home. "Do not abandon my orphans when your time comes" restored in full.
- `m20`: the cure came after Seraphim sent the man round the monastery to pray in the cathedral. The man told it himself (initials E.V.).
- `m21`: Motovilov was brought on 5 September and set under the pine on 9 September, not carried "between 5 and 9".
- `m22`: wording tightened. Fathom note kept (about two meters).
- `m23`: the sisters Ekaterina and Anna told it. Ladyzhensky was sent to China with a Church mission. "Officer" and "wrist" were not in Elagin; he was wounded in the hand in the Turkish war.
- `m24`: Vasily told it himself. Added the age, the countess and the later monastery.
- `theoktist-will-serve-in-diveyevo`: Seraphim first said "serve here" and Theoktist refused. The sleigh broke the next morning, not the same day.
- `m26`: Wikipedia quotes the words said with the packet. Source for the "fourth sovereign" letter is Sanidopoulos's relics article. "No early witness records" changed to "the sources we read name no early witness".
- `m27`: Strizhev calls one note by Motovilov "extremely doubtful", not the whole body of texts.
- `m28`: the source says Seraphim would return after a time of unbelief and preach repentance. Added.
- `m29`: Chichagov puts it on the morning of 2 January, after matins, and gives the disciple as the reporter.
- `m30`: the Russian says the hand opened ("razzhalas'"), not clenched. Mitrofan said he saw it himself.
- `m31` to `m37`: added the details the journal gives (crooked arms, five years blind, "more than 25 years", three years of rheumatism, the convent of Anastasia, malaria from 10 June).
- `m43`: the two men held him; they did not carry him in. Immersed three times.
- `m44`, `m46`, `m47`, `m48`, `m50`: details restored from the source (she says she was healed too; the Zobnik vision words; Bondarenko's earlier improvement at the spring; the girl on the day of uncovering; bread ran short at the monastery, not on the road).
- `m45`: the groundskeeper is the source. Removed "after his healing".
- `m52`: Sitnov refused to stop work and mocked Seraphim. Tur's coworkers told it. Right side paralyzed.
- `m53`: Russian Wikipedia cites Witte for the heir, not Mosolov (his citations are on something else). Wording follows Witte. Dr. Philippe stays as Sanidopoulos's claim.
- `m54`: added the copper cross and the inner voice.
- `m56`: the cement-and-bulldozer story has no Kontsevich credit on the page. It says "military zone", not "nuclear zone". The spring did not "reappear": a new spring gushed in a vision, and the work stopped on orders from Moscow. Rewritten to match.
- `seraphims-candle-lit-at-diveyevo-1991`: the day 1 August comes from Wikipedia (arrival of relics), not from the candle article. Title now says 1991. Added Margarita's age and the 1927 closing.
- After-death introduction: "begin on the night he died" changed to "begin with his death".
