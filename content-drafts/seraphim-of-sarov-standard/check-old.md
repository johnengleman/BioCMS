# Check: Seraphim of Sarov (standard mode, Tier A)

Originals kept as `biography.html.before-check` and `summary.txt.before-check`. No entry.json in this test. Block quotes were checked against raw azbyka text fetched with fetch_text.py (Sergius 1841, Elagin ch.1, 4, 5, 10, Chichagov 1903, the Beseda) and the Synod Act (synod.com). Lint shows no MUST FIX and no CHECK.

## Quotations
- Elagin tray passage (ch.5): the Russian was checked in the raw text. The English is faithful. The block quote stays as written.
- Sister Evdokia's testimony (Chichagov): the translation is faithful. Fixed "the Sarov monks" to "the men of Sarov" (the Russian says "Саровцы") and "knowing all about it himself" to "knowing it himself" (the Russian says "сам зная это"). The lead-in "never forgot... long afterward" was invented and is now "remembered... afterward".
- Antiphon (Sergius): the old line "Those in the desert have a ceaseless longing for God" dropped half of the Russian ("мира сущим суетнаго кроме"). It now reads "Those who live in the desert, apart from the vain world, have a ceaseless longing for God." The Slavonic is slightly ambiguous, and "apart from the vain world" is the safer reading. Also fixed "as he walked", which Sergius does not say. He says many brothers often heard him singing it.
- Sergius on the door opening: the translation is faithful (headaches, vein or air, "never treated", the rule of a sign from above, the request for confirmation, 25 Nov). Two fixes. "Never let a doctor treat him" is Sergius's own claim, so it is now attributed. "Walked out of the monastery" is now "walked out of his enclosure" (Sergius: "вышел из затвора в лес").
- True Physician block quote: the second sentence ("if your love thinks fit...") is in Chichagov only. Sergius has the first sentence only. The cite now names Chichagov 1903. "Holy father" (отче святый) was restored.
- Other block quotes (Sergius icon, Ksenia, Elagin axe, Mantirov, Elagin death, Bogdanov, Motovilov light and charge, Nifont draft, Sergius "tenderness of heart"): confirmed against the raw Russian.
- Motovilov: the text said he would "swear to every word". The Beseda says he is ready to swear to the light he saw. Fixed. "The old man had no way of knowing it" was the narrator stating Motovilov's claim as fact. It is now attributed to Motovilov.
- Chapter 1: "Whatever God gives... is given 'not for you alone'" overgeneralized the quote. It is now "What he had been shown".

## Invented or overstated detail removed
- The brother said his prayer "aloud, as monks do at a closed door": not in Elagin. It is now "as was the custom".
- "Happened every week the same way": not in Elagin. Cut.
- "Walked back through the snow": the snow applies to winter only. Now "went back to the monastery".
- "Heavy iron cross": Elagin says "large". Fixed.
- "Asked the brothers one thing": Elagin says he often said it. Fixed.
- "A brother in the passage could hear him": Elagin says "in the entry, through the door, one could hear". Fixed.
- "Behind her" on the 1780 vision: the card puts the apostles behind her only on the Sarovka. Cut.
- "For a long time... Years later": Chichagov says only "afterwards". Fixed.
- "Pounced", "dry and short", "stubbornly", "fled every human face": mood words, cut or softened.
- "Young officer" Ladyzhensky: "young" is not in the sources. Cut.
- "Nobody punished them" (the robbers): Elagin says Seraphim asked that no one punish them, not that no one did. Cut.
- "The first memorial service", "the book open", "the mill ground its first grain": not in the sources. Fixed.
- Anna Ilyeva is not from Simeon's journal in the source. Reworded.
- "Which no one had ever seen him do" (candles at every icon): Elagin says "not noticed before". Softened.
- The house "beside an unfinished church" (opening): not sourced. Now "a merchant who was building a church".
- "Came with her" for Margarita's candle: the card lists this as not in the sources. Now "She kept a candle".

## Names, dates, numbers
- Sergius "written in 1841": he finished the Life in 1837, and it was printed in 1841. Fixed in the story and in "How This Life Was Researched". He "heard much" is now "parts".
- Merchant society of Kursk: Elagin says the town society. Fixed. "Seventeen" is now "about seventeen" (Sergius says the 18th year).
- Isaiah "spiritual father for twenty years": not sourced. Now "ever since".
- "First time in fifteen years" next to "seventeen years in seclusion" contradicted itself. Now "for the first time since it began".
- The grave "better part of seventy years": it was 70.5. Now "seventy".
- "In 1927 both Diveyevo and Sarov were closed": the notes give 1926 or 1927 for Sarov. Now "In 1926 and 1927".
- 19 July called "the day of his glorification": the Synod glorified him on 29 Jan. Now "the feast of the uncovering of his relics".
- "The convent he built": Diveyevo's founder is named in the text as Agafia Melgunova. Reworded.
- Snit: "ate nothing at all except" conflicted with the weekly bread from the monastery. Now "lived mostly on".
- Greeting "my joy" to everyone: the text then said "others he hailed as Little father". Now follows the Synod Act's own words, "Little father! Little mother! My joy!". The opening, ch.7, the ending, and summary.txt are adjusted.
- Varvara (laughing sister): she cried after being sent away from Diveyevo, not over "a punishment". Fixed.
- Governor Bezobrazov: Elagin says "then governor", Sergius says "former governor". The text keeps Elagin. Not changed.

## Check 2 — readability revision

Copy kept as `biography.html.before-check2`. I compared `biography.html` with `biography.html.before-revision` paragraph by paragraph, and I compared every split quotation with the Russian on the scene cards (scenes-1 to scenes-3). Lint shows no MUST FIX and no CHECK (9,078 words, 577 sentences, average 15.7).

Confirmed with no change:
- The ten split block quotes keep the meaning of the Russian. Nothing was added or dropped, and all earlier corrections are still in force (holy father, "men of Sarov", "knowing it himself", "apart from the vain world", "enclosure", the Chichagov cite, Ksenia's thigh, the Motovilov "swear" wording).
- In-text changes: "devout ascetic" (подвижник благочестия), "We might sin" (как бы не погрешить нам) and the Tambov bishop are acceptable. Vershok is 1.75 in, and seven inches for a quarter arshin is correct.
- All moved attributions cover the same facts as before. Descriptions that replace names are accurate (a bishop or the governor of Tambov, chief bishops of St Petersburg and Moscow, a leading bishop, a Diveyevo sister, a priest as confessor, an earlier recluse, the Trinity monastery, Lutheran wife, a village forty versts away).
- Church-word explanations are accurate (tonsure, tax collector, relics, the Holy Synod, "the Church does not require a saint's body to stay whole", head of a monastery, priest-monk).

Fixes made:
- Ch. 2, the "True Physician" quote: "give this poor man" changed to "give me, a poor man", because the Russian is "меня, убогого" and the first person must match the rest of the quote.
- Ch. 2: "A vigil is a service of prayer that lasts into the night" changed to "An all-night vigil is a long prayer service held at night" (the old wording was vague about the kind of service).
- Ch. 3: "Years later a bishop asked Abbot Nifont" changed to "Later a bishop of Tambov asked...". The notes say only "later", and the Tambov bishop had been dropped.
- Ch. 7: restored "for the first time since it began" for the 25 November 1825 walk out of the cell. The revision had lost this earlier checker fix.
- Ch. 12 (Bogdanov): "These dried crusts are fresh" changed to "This dried bread is fresh". The Russian is сухари (rusks), and the text already calls them "dried bread". "Crusts" was not in the source.
- Ch. 10: "more than an inch of snow on us" changed to "more than an inch and a half". A vershok is 1.75 in, so "an inch" understated it.
- Ch. 10: "He said the old man could not have known it" now reads "Motovilov said...", so it is not confused with Seraphim.
- Ch. 6: "Again and again he said to the brothers" changed back to "He often said" (Elagin says "often"). "he often prayed beside it" restored.
- Opening paragraph 1: "Every Sunday" restores the Sunday that the revision dropped.
- Ch. 1: restored "The plans were said to come from a famous architect" (notes-own-words: S3 says Rastrelli planned it; the name stays cut). Restored "When everything was ready, mother and son sat together" (lost in the revision). Replaced "horse-yokes" with "arches for horses' collars" for the Russian дуги ("bow-yokes" on the card).
- Ch. 9: "He told two of the monks" now ends "why", so the sentence is complete.
- Ch. 13: "followed them through the city" changed to "followed them", because the source says only that they followed them to the cathedral.
- Headings (on the coordinator's instruction, over the 8-word limit): "A Sick Landowner Is Healed and Gives Everything Away" became "A Healed Landowner Gives Everything Away" (id `a-healed-landowner-gives-everything-away`). "Seraphim Teaches a Young Friend About the Holy Spirit" became "Seraphim Teaches a Friend About the Holy Spirit" (id `seraphim-teaches-a-friend-about-the-holy-spirit`).

Left as the reviser wrote it (a judgment, not an error): the two-sentence "I have given myself" repetition in the True Physician quote, which stands for the Russian "и"; the cut surnames and the unnamed novices Anikita and Ioann (now "a novice" twice, with no claim that they are one person).

Ready.
