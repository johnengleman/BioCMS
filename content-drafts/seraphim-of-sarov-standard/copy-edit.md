## Main kinds of fix

- A thing doing what only a person can do: "The promise … had come back to find him", "Honor came looking for him there", "Its report found …", "the church his money had built".
- Grammar and unclear pronouns: "No doctor could tell what it was; they supposed …", "how the old man had sat by his own sickbed", "He did a novice's work" right after Joseph was named, "ask for his blessing" after the governor, "kiss his hand" (Bogdanov), "in the place he had measured" (Nifont or Saint Seraphim).
- Church words whose meaning came after the word: "The Church calls a saint's remains holy relics" stood as a sentence on its own, long after "holy relics" in the opening. The meaning now comes with the first use: "Today his remains rest in a convent at Diveyevo … and the Church honors them as holy relics." The same kind of fix: "abbot", "Lavra", "Lives", "rule" (of prayer), "the altar" for the sanctuary, "found" (which reads as the past of "find").
- Sentences over 30 words split; ", and" glue between unrelated facts split; runs of three "He" sentences broken.
- Headings: "A Winter Day in the Forest Clearing" did not say what happens. It is now "Saint Seraphim and Motovilov Talk in the Snow" (id changed to match). "Saint Seraphim Reposes at Prayer" is now "Saint Seraphim Dies at Prayer", so the Church word "reposed" is used once, in the text ("Saint Seraphim had reposed in the Lord"), as reverence point 7 asks. Revert if the owner prefers "Reposes" in the heading.

## Listed for the fact checker (not changed, or changed only in wording that needs a check)

1. Two women named Ksenia. The Ksenia who heard the story of the 1780 healing ("a woman who looked after a church", "Mother Ksenia") and Ksenia Mikhailovna, head of Diveyevo in 1825. A reader will think they are one person. If they are two, give the first one a short distinguishing word from the source (for example her surname or her role at Diveyevo).
2. "Then he filled the skirts of his coat with dried bread and told him to carry it to the guesthouse." Whose coat? Most likely Mantirov's. If the source confirms it, write "Mantirov's coat".
3. "And the Lord had granted the prayer of poor Seraphim at once." "Seraphim" stands without "Saint", outside quotation marks. If "poor Seraphim" is his own phrase in the Conversation, put it in quotation marks. If not, write "had granted his prayer at once".
4. "He also obtained his release from the town society of Kursk." Readers will not know what this means. If the source supports it, say it plainly (for example, the town's permission for him to leave).
5. "One day the Queen of Heaven herself would remind him of it." It was "as the Queen of Heaven herself would one day show him", which did not say what she would show him. I read it as the 1825 vision ("Why do you want to abandon the commandment of My servant Agafia"). Please confirm.
6. "made it known that he wanted the question of Saint Seraphim's glorification as a saint settled". It was "wanted the matter settled", which did not say what the matter was. Please confirm against the note 117 sources.
7. "Before she became a nun, her name had been Agafia, like Saint Seraphim's mother." The words "like Saint Seraphim's mother" are a link I added. Both names are already in the text. It stops the reader confusing the two Agafias. Remove it if the checker thinks it is too much.
8. "as the desert hermits of the ancient Church had done". I added "desert" so that the meaning of "desert" comes before "they forbid us the desert". The Russian word for these hermits is "pustynnozhiteli", literally "desert dwellers". Please confirm.
9. "the farther of his two huts" (9 December 1825, was "his far hut") and "the nearer of Saint Seraphim's two huts" (the clearing, was "Saint Seraphim's near hut"). Please confirm the far hut is the old forest hut and the near hut is the new one by the spring.
10. "measured his own grave beside the altar" and "buried him beside the altar". A reader will think the grave is inside the cathedral. If it is outside, by the altar wall, say so.
11. "The book before him was a little scorched." No book is named earlier in the death scene. If the source says the book lay open before him, say that.
12. "His heart was not in it." (the shop) and "Then the old monk remembered the shop in Kursk." Both are feelings or memories. Please confirm the sources support them (citations.md 1b).
13. Quotations that read oddly in English. I did not change them. "Do what you need" (to the robbers; perhaps "Do what you have come to do"). "I am not scandalized that some profit from me and others are scandalized."
14. "On the very day he left his cell, the Mother of God called him back to the promise …". The text already says the vision at the spring came on the day he left his cell. Please confirm the vision of 25 November and the walk to the spring were the same day.
