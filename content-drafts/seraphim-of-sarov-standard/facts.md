# Fact sheet: Saint Seraphim of Sarov (checked 2026-10-06, heart draft)

The miracle list and the teachings essay must use these exactly. Note numbers point to `biography.html`. Dates before 1918 are Julian.

## Names and titles
- Born Prokhor Moshnin (Isidorovich); father Isidor, a merchant who built stone churches; mother Agafia (Fotievna). Brother Alexei, sister Paraskeva; Prokhor the youngest of three [4].
- Monk's name Seraphim ("fiery"), tonsured 13 Aug 1786 by Father Pachomius; Isaiah sponsor and spiritual father; Joseph his first teacher (novice master and treasurer) [19, 20].
- Saint Alexandra (Agafia Semyonovna Melgunova), a colonel's widow, first head of Diveyevo; died 13 June 1789; "venerable", glorified 2000 (ru Wikipedia; not "Saint" from memory) [22, 24].
- Saint Anthony (Medvedev): "prepodobny" in the Khronika (para 784) and Rannie (para 17); head of the Trinity-Saint Sergius Lavra [100, 116].
- Emperor Saint Nicholas II ("holy Tsar", Sanidopoulos, Translation) [117, 118].
- Not saints in the sources, so no title: Father Pachomius, Isaiah, Joseph, Nifont, Mikhail Mantirov, Anna Mantirova, Ksenia Mikhailovna (head of Diveyevo), Ksenia the church-woman (a different person), Nikolai Motovilov, Pavel, Theoktist, Bogdanov, Ladyzhensky, Evdokia.
- Authors: Hieromonk Sergius (Vasiliev), first Life, printed 1841, about fifteen years at Sarov; Nikolai Elagin, the fullest Life, 1863; Saint Seraphim Chichagov, Life and Letopis, 1903.

## Dates and numbers
- Born 19 July 1754 in Kursk (the Church's year; the old Lives say 1759). Father died when he was about three; fall from the church at about seven; illness and healing at about ten [4 to 7].
- Left home at about seventeen; reached Sarov 20 Nov 1778 [10, 12].
- Illness 1780, about three years, at least half in bed; healed after Holy Communion and a vision [14 to 18].
- Hospital church altar blessed 17 Aug 1786 [19]. Holy Thursday vision as a hierodeacon, about two hours [21]. Ordained priest 2 Sept 1793 [25].
- Alexandra's request to Pachomius, June 1789; she died 13 June; the way back is twelve versts (about eight miles) [22 to 24].
- Pachomius, seventy, blessed his going to the forest and died in 1794; Saint Seraphim went to the hut 20 Nov 1794 (sixteen years to the day after Sarov); the hut is five versts (a little over three miles) from the monastery [26 to 29].
- Mother Agafia buried in February 1800 (parish register, quoted in a 2002 article; Elagin's visit to her grave is wrong) [32].
- A thousand days and nights of prayer on stones [33, 34].
- Robbers: 12 Sept 1804, three serfs of Kremenki; skull broken; five months in the monastery [38 to 45].
- Isaiah stepped down 1806 and died 4 Dec 1807, in his 67th year; three years of silence [46, 48].
- Return to the monastery 8 to 9 May 1810 after fifteen years in the forest; five years of seclusion in the cell, door opened autumn 1815 (the Tambov governor) [52, 56]; left the cell 25 Nov 1825 [61].
- 25 Nov 1825: vision at the Sarovka, same day as the first walk to the forest; Pachomius's promise was thirty-one years before [61 to 64].
- New hut by the spring about two versts from the monastery [65]. 9 Dec 1825: prayer with the sisters in the far hut [67]. Mill grinding 7 July 1827; six sisters sent to it [74].
- Mantirov: land about forty acres (fifteen desyatinas) [73]; died 7 July 1858, aged sixty, buried 9 July [115].
- Motovilov healed 9 Sept 1831 [85]. The talk in the snow, Thursday in November 1831; snow about seven inches on the ground [86].
- Ladyzhensky visit 1832 [101]. Bogdanov 25 Dec 1832 [103]. Candle for each person [98]. Death: found 2 Jan 1833, about six in the morning; the fire in the cell was the sign [110 to 112].
- Funeral: eight days open in the Dormition cathedral; buried by Abbot Nifont at the side of the altar, on the right [114].
- Commission 1892 to 1894, ninety-four cases [117]. Remains examined 11 Jan 1903; emperor's note 26 Jan; Synod's Act 29 Jan 1903 [117]. Relics uncovered 19 July 1903 [118].
- Shrine opened 17 Dec 1920; relics taken to Moscow 1922; Sarov monastery shut 1926; Diveyevo closed 1927; Sarov off the maps 1946 [119]. Relics found in Leningrad, autumn 1990; handed back 11 Jan 1991; procession from Moscow 28 July 1991; reached Diveyevo 1 Aug 1991 [121, 122].
- Mother Margarita (1899 to 1997): twenty-eight in 1927, ninety-two in 1991 [120, 122].

## Approved wordings of quotations (our translations)
- To Pachomius, 1780: "Holy father, I have given myself to the True Physician of souls and bodies, our Lord Jesus Christ, and to His Most Pure Mother. If your love thinks fit, then for the Lord's sake give me, poor as I am, the heavenly medicine: the Communion of the Holy Mysteries." [15]
- The Mother of God, 1780: "This one is of Our kin." [17]
- Ksenia, church-woman, 1780 story: see [18] (the hollow, "my whole fist went in").
- Pachomius to Alexandra: "But here is the monk-deacon Seraphim. You know his spirit, and he is young. He will live to see it. Entrust this great work to him." [23]
- Pachomius: "I am grieving for the sisters of the Diveyevo community. Who will watch over them after me?" [27]
- "They forbid us the desert, but our first father, John the Forerunner, was a hermit." [30]
- "God, be merciful to me a sinner." [33]
- Nifont's reply: "and of the standing of 1,000 days and nights on a stone, no one knew." [34]
- To a brother: "Otherwise human strength would not have been enough. I was strengthened and comforted within by that heavenly gift which comes down from above, from the Father of Lights." "When there is tenderness of heart, God is with us." [36]
- To the robbers: "Do what you need to do." [39]
- "Love the leisure of silence more than feeding the hungry of the world." (Isaac the Syrian) [49]
- "When we remain in silence, the enemy, the devil, can do nothing against the hidden man of the heart." [50]
- "I torment him who torments me." (Ephraim the Syrian) [65]
- The Mother of God, 25 Nov 1825: "Why do you want to abandon the commandment of My servant Agafia, the nun Alexandra? Leave Ksenia with her sisters. But the commandment of this servant of Mine you must not abandon. Strive to fulfil it entirely, for she gave it to you by My will." [63]
- Greeting: "Little father! Little mother! My joy!" [66]
- "In a community it is easier to manage seven maidens than one widow." [76]
- "I, mother, have gone through the whole of monastic life, and never, even in thought, have I left the monastery." [76]
- To Mantirov: "By the grace given me by the Lord, you are the first I heal!" [70]; "Is it Seraphim's business to kill and make alive?" [70]; "Here, my joy, give the Lord everything you have, and take poverty on yourself of your own free will!" [71]
- "I am not upset that some people profit from me and others take offense." [79]
- Evdokia: "Everyone knows how the men of Sarov disliked our father Seraphim on our account..." [82]
- Motovilov talk: "The true aim of our Christian life is to acquire the Holy Spirit of God." [88]; "I come from Kursk merchants. Before I was in the monastery, we used to trade in the goods that brought us the most profit." [88]; "We are both in the Spirit of God now, you and I. Why don't you look at me?" [90]; "It is not for you alone that you have been given to understand this, but through you for the whole world ... The Lord seeks a heart filled with love for God and for neighbor. That is the throne on which He loves to sit, and on which He appears in the fullness of His heavenly glory." [97]
- To Anthony: "Do not forsake my orphans when your time comes." "Remember my parents Isidor and Agafia in your prayers." [100]
- "Here are my family. To my living relatives I am already a living dead man." [100]
- To Ladyzhensky: "We will not see each other again." (three times) [101]
- To Bogdanov: "If they scold, do not scold back. If they persecute, bear it. If they slander, praise." [103]
- Last day: "Save yourselves. Do not lose heart. Stay awake. Today crowns are being prepared for us." [107]; "While I live there will be no fire. When I die, my death will be revealed by a fire." [109]
- Emperor, 26 Jan 1903: "with a sense of true joy" [117].
- Mantirov's burial place: "Here the ground is holy. The little feet of the Queen of Heaven passed here!" [115]

## Settled source disagreements (as in "A Note on the Sources")
- Birth year 1754 (Church, Kursk records) against 1759 (gravestone, early Lives).
- Mother's death: register, February 1800 (we follow it) against Elagin's story of finding her dead in the 1780s.
- Enclosure: fifteen years (Elagin, 1810) against seventeen (Sergius, 1809).
- Robbers: garden (Sergius) against cutting wood (Elagin). The second vision after the beating is told once.
- Sarov closed 1926 (Sanidopoulos) against "a quarter-century before the 1950s" (Zolotov): we say 1926.
- Crowd on 19 July 1903: more than 100,000 (ru Wikipedia) and 200,000 to 300,000 (Sanidopoulos, for 18 July).
