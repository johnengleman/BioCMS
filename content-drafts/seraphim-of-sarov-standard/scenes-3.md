## SC-11 — Light in the falling snow, Nov 1831
- Status: full
- When and where: Thursday, Nov 1831 (day not given), grey day, snow a quarter-arshin deep on the ground, thick snow-grain falling. On Seraphim's "near" clearing by his near hermitage, opposite the Sarovka river, at the foot of a hill close to the river bank [Beseda, opening].
- Who was there: Seraphim; Nikolai Motovilov (b. 1809, healed by Seraphim's prayer, per the Beseda's introduction and W-S4). No one else is named.
- What happened, in order:
  1. Seraphim seats Motovilov on the stump of a tree he has just cut; he himself squats facing him [Beseda].
  2. He says the Lord has shown him that since boyhood Motovilov wanted to know the aim of the Christian life; Motovilov says this worry had troubled him from age 12 and no one had satisfied him; the old man could not have known it [Beseda].
  3. Seraphim talks at length: the aim is acquiring the Holy Spirit; prayer, fasting and vigil are means [Beseda; OW-S1 Q1-Q2]; the Spirit as "capital", the merchant image [OW-S1 Q3-Q4].
  4. Motovilov says he still cannot be sure he is in the Spirit. Seraphim grips him hard by the shoulders: "We are both in the Spirit of God now" [Beseda].
  5. Motovilov cannot look: lightning from his eyes, face brighter than the sun, eyes ache. Seraphim says he is as bright himself.
  6. Seraphim bends and whispers: he did not even cross himself; he only asked in his heart, silently, that the Lord let him see the descent of the Spirit with bodily eyes [Beseda].
  7. Question and answer in turn: peace, sweetness, joy, warmth, fragrance. Seraphim comments on each with Scripture [Beseda].
  8. Seraphim says this is given "not for you alone, but through you for the whole world", then sends him off in peace [Beseda].
- Details: only the snow, the stump, the squatting posture, the grey day. Motovilov says the light spread "several fathoms" around and lit the snow cover and the falling grain. The vision did not stop through the whole talk; Seraphim spoke in one posture. Motovilov says he would swear to it on oath [Beseda, close].
- Words spoken (exact):
  - Seraphim: "Мы оба теперь, батюшка, в Духе Божием с тобою. Что же ты не смотришь на меня?" We are both now in the Spirit of God, you and I. Why do you not look at me?
  - Motovilov: "Не могу, батюшка, смотреть, потому что из глаз ваших молнии сыпятся. Лицо ваше сделалось светлее солнца, и у меня глаза ломит от боли." I cannot, father, for lightning pours from your eyes. Your face is brighter than the sun and my eyes hurt.
  - Seraphim: "Не устрашайтесь, ваше Боголюбие!" Do not be afraid, your God-lover.
  - Seraphim, on warmth: "Да ведь мы в лесу сидим. Теперь зима на дворе..." Why, we sit in a forest. It is winter outside. (Motovilov: warm "as in a bathhouse when water is thrown on the stove"; "такая, какая бывает в бане, когда поддадут на каменку".)
  - Seraphim, smiling, on fragrance: "И сам я, батюшка, знаю это точно так же, как и вы, да нарочно спрашиваю у вас". I know it just as you do, I ask you on purpose. Then: snow melts neither on him nor under them, so the warmth is "не в воздухе, а в нас самих" (not in the air but in us).
  - Seraphim: "не для вас одних дано вам разуметь это, а через вас для целого мира". It is not for you alone that you are given to understand this, but through you for the whole world.
  - Seraphim: "Господь ищет сердца, преисполненного любовью к Богу и ближнему, – вот престол, на котором Он любит восседать". The Lord seeks a heart full of love of God and neighbour: this is the throne on which He likes to sit.
- Inner life: his silent prayer, step 6 above, quoted in OW-S1 Q8: "Господи, удостой его ясно и телесными глазами видеть то сошествие Духа Твоего..." (Lord, make him worthy to see clearly, with bodily eyes, the descent of Your Spirit). He credits the Mother of God's intercession and calls the grace a comfort to Motovilov's "contrite heart".
- Testimony (Motovilov, Beseda, public-domain, about 120 words):
  > Тогда отец Серафим взял меня весьма крепко за плечи и сказал мне: – Мы оба теперь, батюшка, в Духе Божием с тобою. Что же ты не смотришь на меня? Я отвечал: – Не могу, батюшка, смотреть, потому что из глаз ваших молнии сыпятся. Лицо ваше сделалось светлее солнца, и у меня глаза ломит от боли. ... Представьте себе, в середине солнца, в самой блистательной яркости его полуденных лучей, лицо человека, с вами разговаривающего. Вы видите движение уст его, меняющееся выражение его глаз, слышите его голос, чувствуете, что кто-то вас руками держит за плечи, но не только рук этих не видите, не видите ни самих себя, ни фигуры его, а только один свет ослепительный, простирающийся далеко, на несколько сажен кругом, и озаряющий ярким блеском своим и снежную пелену, покрывающую поляну, и снежную крупу, осыпающую сверху и меня, и великого старца.
  Our English: Then Father Seraphim took me very firmly by the shoulders and said: "We are both in the Spirit of God now, you and I. Why do you not look at me?" ... Picture, in the middle of the sun, in the full brilliance of its noon rays, the face of the man who is talking to you. You see his lips move and the look of his eyes change, you hear his voice, you feel that someone holds you by the shoulders, yet you see neither those hands nor yourself nor his figure, only a blinding light stretching several fathoms round, lighting the snow cover on the clearing and the snow-grain falling on me and on the great elder.
- What changed: Motovilov receives the answer to his boyhood question and a charge to carry it to others; the text itself does not mention Seraphim's grave.
- Not in the sources: no wind, no birds, no time of day beyond the "noon" comparison (the text gives no hour for the talk), no description of Seraphim's clothes or of the stump's place, no tears. The "begins to speak of his grave" ending is not in the Beseda; do not tie it to this scene. The text is "recorded by Motovilov, published 1903", with authenticity debated; say so. Motovilov's healing at the bridge is W-S4 Q21 (9 Sept 1831), not in this text.
- Sources: Beseda; OW-S1 Q1-Q15; OW-S5; OW-S6; W-S4.

## SC-12 — Leaning on his coffin, Christmas 1832
- Status: full
- When and where: Christmas Day 1832 (25 Dec Julian), Sarov. Morning in the hospital church before early liturgy; later that day, the evening, in Seraphim's cell, in the entry where the coffin stood [Elagin ch.10].
- Who was there: Bogdanov, an official (service in the government; asks "continue my service or live in the village?"); his daughter Vera, whom Seraphim blessed. Elagin gives only "г. Богданов" with no first name or patronymic; I found no other name in the chapter. Bogdanov told the story to Elagin (or Elagin's informant) in his own words.
- What happened, in order:
  1. Bogdanov reaches the hospital church before the service; Seraphim sits on the floor at the right choir. Bogdanov asks a talk; Seraphim blesses him, goes into the altar: "после, после" (later, later).
  2. After liturgy Seraphim greets him: "молитвами Пресвятой Богородицы все благо будет". Asked for a time, he says there are two feast days, no time is needed, quoting James: if the Lord wills and we live. He blesses Bogdanov and Vera and gives them both rusks (сухари).
  3. Bogdanov comes to the cell with prepared written questions. Seraphim meets him in the entry, takes the candles and oil Bogdanov brought for others, blesses the talk.
  4. Questions on service, family inheritance, teaching children, what to read ("the Gospel, four portions a day, and the life of Job"), illness. Seraphim answers quickly, with stories and Scripture. In the evening he first says "беседу лучше оставить" (we had better leave the talk); Bogdanov begs and he continues.
  5. On learning of the children: "где мне, младенцу, отвечать на это против твоего разума? Спроси кого поумней" (rebuke for a worldly thought, Bogdanov says).
  6. At parting he thanks Bogdanov for visiting "his poor self", tries to kiss his hand, bows to the ground, gives rusks to share, and says: "гряди с Богом! Эти сухари свежие: только что из печки". (Come with God! These rusks are fresh, straight from the oven.)
- Details: Seraphim stood leaning on the oak coffin he had made for himself, holding a lit wax candle, "чрезвычайно весел" (extremely cheerful). He began answers by calling Bogdanov "ваше боголюбие" [Elagin ch.10]. Bogdanov wrote his questions beforehand because Seraphim spoke very fast.
- Words spoken (exact): "Он стоял, опершись на дубовый гроб, приготовленный им для самого себя, и держал в руках зажженную восковую свечу." (Bogdanov's/Elagin's narration). "Болезнь очищает грехи. Однако же воля твоя." Illness cleanses sins; but it is your choice. "Укоряют – не укоряй: гонят – терпи; хулят – хвали." If they scold, do not scold back; if they persecute, bear it; if they slander, praise.
- "So they will not see me either": this belongs to a separate undated passage in Elagin ch.10 ("несколько месяцев до кончины"), not to Bogdanov's visit. A brother came; Seraphim instructed him, then said "дунь на свечку". The brother blew; the candle went out. Seraphim: "вот так и меня не увидят". Elagin adds that Seraphim told some people he wrote to: "сами-то они меня не увидят". Do not place this scene at the coffin on Christmas day; Elagin does not.
- Grave: "Перед новым же 1833 годом о. Серафим сам отмерил себе могилу сбоку алтаря Успенского собора, на том самом месте, которое, по выходе из затвора, отметил, положивши на нем камень." Before New Year 1833 Seraphim himself measured his grave beside the altar of the Dormition cathedral, at the place he had marked with a stone after leaving enclosure [Elagin ch.10].
- Pavel's account: Elagin gives Brother Pavel only later (SC-13). Pavel's only part here: Seraphim told him death was near and Pavel did not know whether he meant his own death or the end of the world [Elagin ch.10].
- Testimony (Bogdanov via Elagin, public-domain, about 35 words): "Во все время нашей беседы о. Серафим был чрезвычайно весел. Он стоял, опершись на дубовый гроб, приготовленный им для самого себя, и держал в руках зажженную восковую свечу." Our English: During our whole talk Father Seraphim was extremely cheerful. He stood leaning on the oak coffin he had prepared for himself and held a lit wax candle in his hands.
- What changed: nothing in the visit itself; it shows the dying elder's joy. A week later he was dead.
- Not in the sources: Bogdanov's first name, the hour of the visit, the room's look, the candle's size. Not a word about what Seraphim felt at the coffin. Do not write "he knew then it was the last Christmas" as Bogdanov's thought. Elagin separately says that on this same Christmas Seraphim took Communion at Abbot Nifont's liturgy and asked him once more to be laid in the coffin.
- Sources: Elagin ch.10; W-S2 ch.10.

## SC-13 — Found kneeling, 1-2 Jan 1833
- Status: full
- When and where: 1 Jan 1833 (Sunday), then about 6 a.m. on 2 Jan, Sarov, the hospital church of Sts Zosima and Savvaty and Seraphim's cell next to Pavel's [Elagin ch.10].
- Who was there: Brother Pavel (neighbour who sometimes acted as cell-attendant); novice Anikita; novice Ioann; the brothers; Hieromonk Theoktist of Vysokogorsk; Diveyevo sister Irina Vasilievna; sister Paraskeva Ivanovna at Diveyevo [Elagin ch.10].
- What happened, in order:
  1. 1 Jan: Seraphim comes to the church for the last time, lights candles at all the icons himself (not seen before), takes Communion. He says goodbye to all, blesses and kisses each, saying: "спасайтесь, не унывайте, бодрствуйте: нынешний день нам венцы готовятся". He leaves by the north door. All notice his bodily weakness; in spirit "бодр, спокоен и весел".
  2. After liturgy he sends 200 rubles with Irina Vasilievna to Paraskeva Ivanovna to buy bread, because the Diveyevo sisters had run out.
  3. He tells Theoktist "ты ужо отслужишь здесь" (you will serve here); Theoktist declines: "ну, так ты в Дивееве отслужишь". Theoktist leaves that day.
  4. Pavel sees him go out three times to the place marked for his grave and look at the ground. That evening Pavel hears him singing Easter hymns: "Воскресение Христово видевше...", "Светися, светися новый Иерусалиме...", "О, пасха велия...".
  5. 2 Jan about 6 a.m.: Pavel smells smoke in the entry, knocks, the door is hooked from inside. Anikita tears the hook off. Linen and books on a bench smoulder; there is no flame. They put it out with snow.
  6. During liturgy a boy slips into the church and tells some of them. The brothers come. Pavel and Ioann feel through the dark cell and find him. A candle is brought.
  7. He is kneeling before the icon of the Mother of God "Умиление" in his white smock, head bare, copper crucifix on his neck, arms crossed on his chest. They try to wake him. He is dead.
  8. Theoktist, on the way, has his sleigh fastening break without cause, stops at Diveyevo and finds the sisters in tears; they ask him for the memorial service. "Ты в Дивееве отслужишь" comes true.
- Details: his book before him was scorched. His body was warm. The brothers carried him to the cell of Hieromonk Eustathius, washed his forehead and knees, dressed him, laid him in the oak coffin, and took him to the cathedral. Candles near the coffin went out from the heat of the crowd on the burial day. Eight days open in the Dormition cathedral; buried by Abbot Nifont beside Mark the recluse; no speeches (all in Elagin ch.10). Elagin's note 10 says earlier editions had his hands on a small lectern with the head on them; this edition follows "единогласный рассказ" of Sergius, Georgy, Ioasaf and Motovilov (kneeling, arms crossed).
- Words spoken (exact): Seraphim, to Pavel earlier: "пока я жив, пожара не будет; а когда я умру, кончина моя откроется пожаром". While I live there will be no fire; when I die, my death will be revealed by a fire. Seraphim, of Pavel: "брат Павел за простоту своего сердца без труда войдет в царствие Божие: он никогда никого не судит и не завидует никому". Pavel, 2 Jan: "отцы и братия! слышан сильный дымный запах. Не горит ли что около нас?" Fathers and brothers! A strong smell of smoke. Is something burning near us?
- Testimony (Elagin ch.10, public-domain, about 90 words): "...нашли его самого. Принесли зажженную свечку и увидели, что старец, в обычном своем белом балахончике, стоял на обыкновенном месте молитвы пред иконою Божией Матери Умиления на коленях, с открытою головою, с медным Распятием на шее, с руками, сложенными крестообразно на груди. Полагали, что он уснул; стали осторожно будить его – но ответа не было: старец окончил подвижническую жизнь свою... Глаза его были закрыты, лицо оживлено богомыслием и молитвою. Тело старца было тепло, как будто бы дух его только еще сию минуту оставил храмину свою." Our English: ...they found him. A lit candle was brought, and they saw the elder, in his usual white smock, kneeling at his usual place of prayer before the icon of the Mother of God "Tenderness", head bare, a copper crucifix on his neck, arms crossed on his chest. They thought he slept and tried gently to wake him, but there was no answer. The elder had ended his life of struggle. His eyes were closed, his face alive with thought of God and prayer. His body was warm, as if his spirit had left its dwelling only that moment.
- What changed: the elder is dead; the bread money, the Easter hymns and the Diveyevo memorial all point back to the Christmas joy and ahead to Diveyevo.
- Not in the sources: no one saw him die; Elagin's "that moment" is a comparison, not an eyewitness claim. The cause of the smouldering is "вероятно" (probably) a candle drip or fallen candle. The text does not say the monks wept in the cell, how many came, or what the icon looked like. The light seen at Glinsk (OW-S3) is legend; skip.
- Sources: Elagin ch.10; W-S2 ch.10 Q14-Q16; OW-S3.

## SC-14 — The tsar carries the coffin, 17-19 July 1903
- Status: thin
- When and where: 17-19 July 1903 (Julian), Sarov [Trans; CM-S3; CM-S7].
- Who was there: Nicholas II and the royal family; Grand Duchess Elizabeth, who wrote an eyewitness account of miracles; between 200,000 and 300,000 pilgrims on 18 July (CM-S3 count; ruWiki's older estimate in the notes is 100,000+; the figures conflict) [Trans; CM-S2; CM-S4].
- What happened, in order:
  1. On 18 July relics were moved to the Dormition cathedral; the night of 18-19 July was an all-night vigil (Trans, "in all the churches of Holy Russia").
  2. 19 July: relics uncovered, placed in the cypress coffin given by the tsar and tsarina. The tsar was one of the bearers in the procession [Trans; CM-S5].
  3. Healings recorded by Hieroschemamonk Simeon in a daily journal begun in 25 June [OC, para before "Here are but a few extracts"]. Anna Ilyeva is not among the journal extracts I read; the OC account of her comes in the narrative before the journal passage.
- Details on Anna Ilyeva (OC, narrator Malleev-Pokrovsky; facts-only, own words): a widowed Cossack woman from Semirechensk, blind for 19 years. After she was immersed in the spring, "the blindness fell away from her eyes like scales" (OC's English, paraphrase here). No date is given in the part I read, and no words of hers.
- Words spoken: none exact from 19 July. The tsar's "true joy" is a written note of 26 Jan 1903 on the Synod's report ("I have read this with a sense of true joy and profound compunction", CM-S2), not a 19 July remark. I did not find Nicholas II's diary of 19 July 1903. A web search gave no text. The outline's "Need" is unmet.
- Inner life: nothing.
- Testimony: none to copy; OC and Trans are facts-only.
- What changed: the Church had a canonised saint (Synod Act 29 Jan 1903, CM-S2); the crowd saw the tsar carry the relics.
- Not in the sources: no diary, no letter text (the contents of the "letter for the fourth sovereign" are unknown, Trans), no quote from the tsar on the day. "Bones, not a whole body": the 11 Jan 1903 exam found a skeleton, but that is from ruWiki in CM-S4, not read again here. Do not say the crowd chanted "Christ is Risen"; Trans says only that it was foretold.
- Sources: Trans; OC; CM-S2; CM-S3; CM-S4; CM-S5; CM-S7.

## SC-15 — The candle, 1 Aug 1991, Diveyevo
- Status: thin
- When and where: 1 Aug 1991 (New Style; = 19 July Old Style), Diveyevo convent [ruWiki; Candle].
- Who was there: Mother Margarita (1899-1997), 92, the last surviving nun of Diveyevo from before the 1927 closure [Candle]; crowd "при большом стечении народа" (a great gathering) [ruWiki].
- What happened, in order:
  1. Before he died, Seraphim gave the Diveyevo nuns a candle and said that with it they would one day receive him again [Candle].
  2. The candle passed from one generation to the next until it was in Mother Margarita's hands [Candle]. How it survived 1927-1991 is not stated.
  3. 11 Jan 1991, the relics were found and handed over in Leningrad. 6-7 Feb, they were carried to Moscow. 28 July, the procession left Moscow; 1 Aug they were received at Diveyevo [ruWiki; ROCA].
  4. In 1991, when the relics came to Diveyevo, Margarita lit the candle to receive them back [Candle].
- Details: she was 28 when the convent closed in 1927 [Candle]. ROCA (spring 1991) tells of the procession plan to reach Diveyevo 1 Aug, "the anniversary", and of the Moscow procession with gold-vested priests and thousands in snow [ROCA].
- Words spoken: none found. The seized and rediscovered relics: opened 17 Dec 1920, found in Leningrad 1990 (CM-S4); the notes also record the mittens embroidered with his name.
- Testimony: none. Candle is facts-only and 237 words long; it gives no quotation.
- What changed: the convent, closed in 1927, receives him back as he said it would.
- Not in the sources: the time of day, the place in the church where she lit it, her words, whether she carried it in the procession, how the candle was kept. The Candle page says the relics were "miraculously discovered in St. Petersburg"; CM-S3/CM-S4 show the real history (opened 1920, stored in a museum), so do not say "miraculously found" or "vanished" without it.
- Sources: Candle; ruWiki; ROCA; CM-S3; CM-S4; CM-S8; CM-S9; W-S4.
