Saint Francis of Assisi grew up rich. He was the merchant's son who paid for his friends' feasts, and he dreamed of becoming a knight and a great prince. Then a love took hold of him that he could not keep quiet about. He told his friends he would marry a bride nobler and more beautiful than any they had seen, and he would not say who she was. Years later he had put away every kind of envy but one: he envied anyone poorer than himself. What was this love that made a rich young man glad to own nothing at all? And what would it cost him to keep it?
