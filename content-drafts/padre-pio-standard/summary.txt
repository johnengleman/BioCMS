A month after he became a priest, a sick young friar in a hill village of southern Italy found red marks in the palms of his hands. He asked the parish priest to pray with him that they would go away. He wanted to suffer with Christ, he said, but all in hiding. Saint Pio, whom the world knows as Padre Pio, then spent fifty-two years in one small friary on a mountain. Doctors examined him, crowds climbed to his confessional, and the Church he obeyed tested him again and again. When the whole world can see a man who longs to stay hidden, what does he love, and what does it cost him?
