- Bishop (later Cardinal) Raffaello Carlo Rossi, Cardinal Lercaro, Cardinal Bertone, Padre Agostino Gemelli, Pietro Parente, Carlo Maccari: not canonized.
- Wanda Poltawska and all doctors, helpers, and townspeople: not canonized.
- Jesus, Mary, Our Lady: keep their own titles.
