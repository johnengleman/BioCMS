## Fixes made
1. m02 and biography ch. 14: "went down to the crypt" and "went down to ... tomb" were not in the source (the card says: "Not in the sources: the ward room, the hour..."). Now "went to Saint Pio's tomb and prayed the Rosary there".
2. m02: added the Voce chronology (a Capuchin Church source) as teller of the 21 January breathing support, the mother's prayer, and the improvement. The devotional website now tells only what the Voce does not (Rosary, septic shock, the old man).
3. m01 and biography ch. 13: "a friar" now "Friar Modestino of Pietrelcina", from 1 November (caccioppoli; notes A).
4. Biography ch. 13: "could find no natural explanation" changed to "All five doctors on the Church's medical board judged the cure scientifically inexplicable" (the Church's own wording is "scientifically inexplicable", not "natural"). Ch. 14: "could not explain the cure" changed to "found the healing complete, lasting, and scientifically inexplicable" (Voce).
5. Biography ch. 5: "Don Peppino Orlando was a priest" is not in any source, and no source says he told the account. Now: a spoken account handed down from Saint Pio's talk with his friend Don Peppino Orlando. One sentence added to the Note on the Sources (no named source or date).
6. Biography ch. 6, Bignami: the pass had lost "tincture of iodine" and "perhaps without his knowing" (notes-extra-life.md E13). Restored as Bignami's view ("He thought that ...").
7. Biography ch. 10, Maccari: the pass had dropped his one-word summing up. Restored: "summed up his impression in one word: pitiful" (it is the visitor's recorded verdict, told as an event).
8. Biography ch. 12: "None of it could hold him" (a thing as actor, and an odd line beside the anointing). Now "They gave oxygen and injections, and a priest anointed him. At about half past two ... went to God."
