## Changes near a fact (for the checker)

- Biography ch. 4: "fifty-two degrees" is now "fifty-two degrees Celsius". The unit is in notes-extra-primary.md (X7: "52 C") and in the miracles list. Without it a reader may think of Fahrenheit.
- Biography ch. 3: "in his novitiate" is now "in his first year as a friar". This is a plain word for the same year (January 1903 to January 1904).
- Biography ch. 13: "One approved miracle was needed for beatification" now explains the word: "Before the Church could beatify him and call him Blessed, it needed one approved miracle."
- Biography ch. 6: the sentence that explains the Holy Office moved up to its first use (Festa's 1925 report). It was two paragraphs too late.
- Biography ch. 7: the 21 August sentence now comes before Saint Pio's second letter to the mayor. "Two weeks later" became "Two weeks after his first letter" so the dates read in order. No date changed.
- Biography opening: "God did not grant that prayer" is now "In the end God did not grant that prayer". Ch. 3 says "God heard that prayer, at least in part", and the two lines contradicted each other.
- Miracles, Bignami: "were finished by suggestion" is now "were completed by suggestion". The notes (E13) say "completed".
