## Listed for the checker (not fixed)

1. **"Sometimes he went to the altar “with fever on me,” because he could not stay away."** A first-person quotation is spliced into a third-person sentence. I did not change the quotation. The checker could quote the whole sentence of the letter, or retell it without quotation marks.
2. **"while he waited for a Mary to bring him a word of comfort"** (Nina Campanile scene). "a Mary" is unclear to a reader. Check the diary's words.
3. **"“Let happen what will,” Saint Pio said."** The quotation reads badly in English. The plan gives "Avvenga quel che voglia avvenire"; "Come what may" may be a fair translation. I did not change it.
4. **"signed a paper saying that everything would be done first on his advice"**. The meaning of "first" is unclear: "above all" or "before anything else"? Check the source.
5. **"kept from Mass until he left"** (Philippe's requests). Unclear: kept from public Mass, or from all Mass? Until he left the friary?
6. **"A cardinal in Rome gave the letter to Angelo Battisti"**. notes-extra-life.md (line 94) says that Fr Andrzej Deskur helped send it to Battisti. Check "a cardinal".
7. **"Then a letter came from Poland"** was changed to "came from Saint John Paul II", because the next sentence says that he was in Rome. Confirm where the letter was written.
8. **Summary: "He wanted to suffer with Christ, he said, but all in hiding."** The quotation in the life is "I want to suffer, even to die of suffering, but all in hiding". It does not say "with Christ". Confirm that this is a fair reading, or drop "with Christ".
9. **"Saint Pio stayed by her bed"**: I removed "to the end" because it contradicted the fainting scene. Confirm.
10. **"It was the quiet corner of this land that he had asked for in 1923."** These are borrowed words from his letter (note 58), without quotation marks. They could be marked as a quotation.
11. **"How many sins you left in the world when you suspended Padre Pio from confessions for three years!"** The quotation says three years. The suspension in the life runs from June 1931 to 1933–34. The quotation was left as it is; the checker may want a note.
12. **"The light blinded him."** (after 5–7 August 1918). The meaning is unclear. Check the letter.
13. **The doctors paragraph** names three new people (Romanelli, Bignami, Festa), and Gemelli comes next. The rule allows at most two new names in a paragraph. I removed no names, because names are facts.
14. **Sentence 41** ("He accepted his superiors' measures without a word…") is now in the narrator's voice. Before, it was given as the words of the decree on his virtues. Confirm that note 69 supports it.
15. **New explanations of Church words**: guardian, Transfiguration, ecstasy, the last sacraments, sacristy, Second Vatican Council, and Blessed. These are definitions, not new facts. Confirm the wording.
16. **"Fifty-eight years had passed since the afternoon under the elm."** I kept this line after the "Fifty-eight" scene on purpose. It does explain the scene. But the scene of 1910 is many chapters back, and the line closes on an image, not on a lesson. Cut it if the owner prefers.
17. **Order at the end**: Pope Saint Paul VI's words of 1975 come after the beatification of 1999 and the canonization of 2002. I did not move the paragraph.
