# Fact check: St. Padre Pio of Pietrelcina (Tier A)

Checker: not the writer. Originals kept as `biography.html.before-factcheck`, `entry.json.before-factcheck`, `summary.txt.before-factcheck` (the older `*.before-check` files are from the writer's first pass). Quotations were checked against raw text fetched with `fetch_text.py` or `curl | pdftotext` (realtasannita.it, sangiovannirotondofree.it, padrepio.it, Voce PDFs, Dicastery page, vatican.va, it/en Wikipedia, Wikiquote, EWTN, Irish Catholic, CARF, capuchins.org, OSV, ACI Prensa) and against the McCaffery text. Not WebFetch summaries.

## Confirmed as written
- 22 Oct 1918 letter: every Italian clause in the biography matches the raw text. The opening cry ("Mio Dio, che confusione...") is on realtasannita.it only. The "waterfall" and "punishment is just" lines are not on either website; they are in Lotti, Voce Oct 2018.
- Canonization chain: De Martino decree 21 Dec 1998, beatification 2 May 1999; Colella illness 20 Jan 2000, decree 20 Dec 2001, canonization 16 June 2002 (Dicastery text). EWTN's 2000 dates stay out of the text.
- 2008 exhumation (en.wikipedia raw, it.wikipedia "night between 2 and 3 March"): fair condition, partly skeletal skull, manicured hands, no stigmata, silicone mask, 24 Apr Mass for 15,000, April 2010 move.
- March 1918 discharge (15 Mar, home 17 Mar per Fabiano; 16 Mar per Voce) and the 1915 date of Agostino's three questions (Lotti, Epist. I p. 669).
- Block quotes: mirror, 1911 palms, drop of water, Cerase 1914, 5 Aug 1918 wound, Benedetto 1919, Romanelli, Mass/sun, books/prayer, roses, Paul VI. Also the phrases "Sì, Eccellenza", "Opero solamente per ubbidirvi", "Guai a voi", "Vi siete scottata la mano", "Mi dispiace di P.P.", JPII 1987 and 1999 lines.

## Fixes (location, reason)
1. Ch. 1, watch scene: removed "One afternoon in the Capuchin friary". McCaffery gives no time or place (pp. 68-69). Also changed "for two years it forbade him" to "later, for two years", because the two-year ban was 1931-33, not the 1923 finding.
2. Ch. 2: removed "Mamma Peppa", "food on the table every day, and very little cash", and the farm at Piana Romana (only caccioppoli.com). Replaced the "bell at dawn" with the EWTN wording (bell rang for prayer). Removed "by oxcart" to Morcone; EWTN puts the oxcart after the 1904 vows.
3. Ch. 3: "Pius V" changed to "the pope-saint Pius" (EWTN says Pius V, en.wikipedia says Pius I; listed in the Note). "From about 1905" changed to 1904 (it.wikipedia). Removed "the only food" (now "could digest little but milk and cheese"). Cut the friar's levitation claim from the story; it stays in the miracles list as a report.
4. Ch. 3 and 13: removed "under the elm trees" (search-summary and caccioppoli only). Hut at Piana Romana now sourced to it.wikipedia ("good for his lungs").
5. Ch. 4: the telegram came to Pannullo from the friars; he did not send it.
6. Ch. 5, Cerase paragraph: the line "pray for their conversion" about bad priests is Cerase's own (Epist. II p. 110), which Padre Pio approved; the draft gave it to him. Fixed here and in the teachings entry.
7. Ch. 5: visions began "not long after his novitiate" (EWTN), not "in the novitiate". "At his lowest point" removed (no context in Wikiquote).
8. Ch. 5, Gemma Galgani: "Scholars have found" became "It has been pointed out" (one sentence of it.wikipedia). Added to the Note and the read-next sentence in the share kit.
9. Ch. 6: "fevers and sweats" at Foggia removed (caccioppoli only). Corpus Christi 1918 state: "lasting" softened to "lasting for some time" and no longer "he said" (Fabiano's wording). "Only Paolino and Padre Pio were left" now "only two priests" (Fabiano). Added the year to 30 May 1918.
10. Ch. 6 and 7: "choir loft" changed to "choir" (no source says loft). Section id is now `the-morning-in-the-choir`; share-kit section_id updated.
11. Ch. 7, Orlando account: "Years later" was wrong; the source says "soon after". Paolino heard "eight or ten days" later (his own memory).
12. Ch. 7, bleeding paragraph: the "Thursday to Saturday" and "Temo di morire dissanguato" lines are in the same 22 Oct letter (Lotti Sept 2018 p. 1094; EWTN no. 510), not "by the end of the year". The "How sweet and bitter" letter is dated only "after the stigmata".
13. Ch. 8: "The first to look carefully" became "One of the first". Benedetto's report was written on Ash Wednesday 1919 (Ruppi), not necessarily seen that day. Romanelli: "head surgeon" became "chief physician"; the "five visits" and "Festa kept coming" removed (caccioppoli only). Bignami: "kept open with a chemical" became "completed ... by a chemical" (it.wikipedia). Festa's 48 C: the draft said the thermometer could not register Festa's readings; it.wikipedia says that of the Naples military doctors and gives 48-48.5 C for Festa. Gemelli: "Gemelli had none" removed (disputed whether he had a mandate). Rossi: "in the friar's cell" removed from the violet fragrance (source gives no place); "A pharmacist had testified" became "A witness" (en.wikipedia: the pharmacist's cousin; Gaeta and Tornielli call it unreliable, now in the Note).
14. Ch. 9: the march of the town and the forty Fascists are the Irish Catholic writer's own narrative, not the Observer quotation. Now "by one writer's account".
15. Ch. 10: McCaffery's "Holy Women" did not queue at 2-3 a.m.; they skipped the queue and charged the doors, and Mrs. Ghisleri was trampled. The draft said they "went further". "Misquoted" became "often quoted in a looser form" (Ruppi says only that the popular form differs). Added the Ruppi locator wording.
16. Ch. 11, Wojtyla: "Some who knew them affirmed it" became Stickler, Dziwisz, and John Paul II's own dismissal (en.wikipedia, capuchins.org). Prayer groups 1942: sources conflict (it.wikipedia 1942, en.wikipedia 1950s); now "by one account" in the bio, teachings, and Note; also in ch. 15 ("he encouraged"). 1959: "summer" removed; recovery now attributed to the Vatican chronology. Cut Valdoni, blood bank, Gambino to hold the word count.
17. Ch. 12: John XXIII's notes are now credited to Luzzatto (2007). In 1962 Wojtyla was a bishop, not a young priest. The wounds faded "some time before the end" (McCaffery), not "in his last months".
18. Ch. 13: Humanae Vitae letter has no date in the sources; "September" and "one of the last letters" removed. "Fifty-eight": Padre Pio was 81, so it cannot be his age; the draft's reading is now a plain count-back to 1910 (it.wikipedia counts the stigmata 1910-1968). The source is a Voce writer, not "a biographer". Wounds: McCaffery says "some time before"; it.wikipedia "a few days"; a critic says months; the bio now says "gradually before the end", "is said to have found". BBC: "A Very Private Man" title appears in no source and was removed; "not long before" became "some time before" (McCaffery).
19. Ch. 14: "within weeks of his death" removed (EWTN says 4 Nov 1968, Voce 4 Nov 1969; Congregation says only "in a short time").
20. Ch. 15: "in the last days" and "no wound at all" softened to match the earlier paragraph.
21. Note on the Sources rewritten and split: letter provenance, choir versus high altar (the letter does not say "no one present"; the accounts that go back to him say the friary was nearly empty), added conflicts (novitiate date, 1911 vs 1915 for the three questions, prayer groups, fading of wounds, namesake pope), listed what rests on encyclopedia summaries, and credited the Irish Catholic for the 1920 and 1923 quotations.
22. How This Life Was Researched: 39 became 38 sources (caccioppoli.com no longer cited and removed from the Sources list); "three" papal homilies and addresses became four (1987, 1994, 1999, 2002); the 1920 and 1923 press passages are said to be seen only through the 2018 Irish Catholic article.
23. Word count: the revisions pushed the draft to 10,210 words; trimmed to 9,969 (lint count), inside 8,000-10,000. Summary trimmed to 118 words (lint limit 120): removed "a town marched to keep him" and added "hear his Mass and".
24. entry.json: biography and summary re-synced. Miracles m04 (Romanelli year, pharmacist, wound fading, "never a decree" softened, choir/high-altar wording), m08 (Festa 48 C), m10 (no "in his cell", caccioppoli source removed), m17 (Stickler, JPII dismissal, capuchins.org source), m20, m21, m22 ("incorrupt headlines" claim removed, no source). Relic description same. Teachings: prayer-groups date, "pray for priests" attribution, "last days", recipient of the prayer-of-quiet letter (Lotti does not name one). Quotes: "I act only to obey you" and "It seems that Jesus has nothing else in hand" are from the 1999 beatification homily (Dicastery page), not the 2002 homily URL given; the 1103 and 1094 letters re-dated; "to Fr Benedetto" removed from the prayer-of-quiet quote. Patron field: civil defence volunteers is recorded as approved (2004), the rest popular. Share kit: 1923 march "by one account", last-night timing, Wojtyla and Galgani sentences, section id. Sources: caccioppoli removed (38), ORS "oxcart" removed. Gaps and field_review updated, including a new gap listing what rests on Wikipedia alone.

## Left as is, with attribution or limits
- Epistolario never read; all Padre Pio quotations come from Church documents, Voce articles, and Wikiquote. Wikiquote numbers match the page numbers Voce uses (1065), so they are given as locators.
- The 22 Oct letter has no page for its opening in any source.
- Wikipedia-only items (exhumation details, prayer-group figures, 2 million pilgrims, Orione, Gemma di Giorgi, Venafro fast, Festa 1925, Maccari, "straw idol", Pius XII dispensation) are attributed in the text or sit in the miracles list with a status.

- Every miracle status label matches its source: two approved (decrees of 21 Dec 1998 and 20 Dec 2001), the rest reported, popular, or contested; the stigmata have no approving decree.
