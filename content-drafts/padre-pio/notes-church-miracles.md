# Padre Pio — notes: church-miracles
Researcher: Sonnet 5.5, 2026-09-30. Focus: Church process, miracles, relics, cult. RIGHTS: Padre Pio d. 1968; books are in copyright. Facts only. Own wording. Vatican homilies are quoted short with source.

## 1. Identity (only what I read)
- Francesco Forgione, b. 25 May 1887 Pietrelcina (Benevento); parents Grazio Forgione and Maria Giuseppa De Nunzio; baptized next day as Francesco [S2]. Capuchin novice name Fra Pio, habit 22 Jan 1903 at Morcone [S2]. Priest 10 Aug 1910 Benevento [S2]. To San Giovanni Rotondo Sept 1916, stayed until death [S2]. Died 23 Sept 1968, aged 81 [S2].
- Tradition: Catholic only (playbook latin-italy). Tier A.
- Feast: 23 September (memoria) [S2][S5]. Names: Pio da Pietrelcina / Pio of Pietrelcina / St Padre Pio.
- Beatified 2 May 1999; canonized 16 June 2002; both by John Paul II, St Peter's Square [S2][S1].
- Patronage (Wikipedia, not official): civil defence volunteers, adolescents, Pietrelcina, stress relief, "January blues" [S5]. Not found in an official Church source. Treat as popular. TO CHECK.

## 3. Timeline (church side)
- 1905 (Feb): Padre Pio's note of seeing himself in Udine at a dying father's bed while a girl was born (Giovanna Rizzani Boschi, b. 18 Jan 1905) — reported bilocation; she later testified at beatification process [S4c]. NEW? (popular pages vary).
- 1918 5-7 Aug: transverberation, described by Padre Pio, read by his director Padre Benedetto as such [S5].
- 1918 20 Sept: stigmata reported during confession; wore them 50 years [S5]. Wearing red mittens, ashamed [S5].
- 1919 Mar: Padre Benedetto's report: "real wounds", side wound "a veritable gash" (paraphrase of S5 quote; check source) [S5].
- 1919-20: Drs Bignami and Festa examined; Festa 1925 report to Holy Office positive [S5]. Gemelli 1920 visit: refused exam without Holy Office paper; wrote harsh judgment, said the wounds fitted self-inflicted caustic wounds, never examined them [S5]. Contested.
- 1921 11 Jun: Holy Office names Bp Raffaele Rossi of Volterra; visitation from 14 June; report to Holy Office 4 Oct 1921: stigmata a "real fact", not fraud or devil's work; many alleged healings unconfirmed [S5].
- 1921-22: barred from public Mass and confession [S5]. May 1923 Holy Office statement: nothing supernatural proven [S5]. 1923-31 statements denying divine cause [S5].
- 1933: Pius XI ends Mass ban; reported quote "I have not been badly disposed toward Padre Pio, but I have been badly informed" [S5]. 1934 confession allowed [S5].
- 1956 5 May: Casa Sollievo della Sofferenza inaugurated [S2]. 1957: Pius XII dispensation from poverty to run it [S5]. Critics alleged misuse of funds [S5].
- 1960s: John XXIII era: bugs in cell and confessional, Maccari visitation; Pope wrote "straw idol" [S5]. Mid-1960s Paul VI dismissed accusations [S5]. Contested; verify in Luzzatto/Ceci.
- 1968 22 Sept: last Mass, 50th anniversary of stigmata [S5]. 23 Sept: dies ~2:30 am, repeating "Jesus, Mary"; stigmata gone, no scar per attending doctor [S5]. Funeral 26 Sept, ~100,000 [S5].
- 1968 4 Nov: preliminary process opens [S4]. 1969 4 Nov: postulator Bernardino da Siena asks Mons. Cunial for nihil obstat [S4b]. 11 Nov 1980 CDF "nihil obstat cum mente" [S4b]. 29 Nov 1981 Causes Congregation nihil obstat [S4b] (S4 says 29 Nov 1982; conflict).
- 1983 20 Mar: diocesan process opens in Santa Maria delle Grazie; ends 21 Jan 1990 [S4b]. 12 Feb 1990 Rome phase opens [S4b]. 7 Dec 1990 decree on validity [S4b].
- 1997 13 Jun: theologians unanimous on heroic virtue [S4b]. 18 Dec 1997: JPII approves heroic virtue; Venerable [S4][S4b].
- 1998: miracle review (see Miracles). 21 Dec 1998 decree on miracle; 2 May 1999 date set [S4][S4b].
- 1999 2 May: beatification, 9:59 am, "largest crowd ever" in the square (EWTN wording) [S4].
- 2000 20 Dec: decree on second miracle [S4]. 2002 28 Feb: canonization decree [S4]. 2002 16 June: canonization [S3].
- 1974 and 1987 JPII returned to San Giovanni Rotondo (1987 for the centenary) [S5].
- 2008 3 Mar: exhumation after midnight; see Relics. 2008 24 Apr: Cardinal Saraiva Martins Mass for ~15,000; body on display; display extended to Sept 2009 [S5].
- 2009 21 Jun: Benedict XVI visits shrine [S5]. 2010 Apr: remains moved to golden crypt [S5].
- 2016 5-11 Feb: relics of Padre Pio and Leopold Mandic in St Peter's, Jubilee of Mercy, requested by Francis; procession Via della Conciliazione; Ash Wednesday 10 Feb [S6]. (S5 says 8-14 Feb; conflict: S6 says until 11 Feb.)
- 2018 17 Mar: Francis visits Pietrelcina and San Giovanni Rotondo [S5].

## 4. Character (from sources)
- Official profile: "I want only to be a poor friar who prays" ("Voglio essere soltanto un povero frate che prega") [S2].
- JPII: confessor seemed severe, penitents "almost always came back" [S3].
- Tricks: bought carbolic acid and veratrine; said for sterilizing syringes and a joke on friars with snuff; Rossi saw "simplicity" and "playful spirit" [S5]. Contested by critics.
- Refused Gemelli exam without written Holy Office order: obedience [S5].
- Stayed silent under accusations (official bio) [S2].
- Hospital: wanted warm, "human" medicine (JPII) [S1].

## 7. Miracles (status key: APPROVED = decree; REPORTED = witness or shrine; CONTESTED)
A. Beatification miracle. Consiglia (Lina) De Martino, Salerno area (hospital; place not read). 1 Nov 1995: lump under left collarbone, CT scans twice (1 pm, 6:30 pm) showed ruptured thoracic duct, ~half gallon of lymph; surgery set for 3 Nov. Perfume of Padre Pio smelled 1 and 2 Nov. 3 Nov lump gone. 4 Nov 7:30 voice. Left hospital 6 Nov with no surgery. Friar Modestino prayed at tomb. Dr Tommaso Contaldi collected records [S4b]. Process: Medical Committee 30 Apr 1998 unanimous, "scientifically inexplicable"; theologians 22 Jun 1998; cardinals/bishops 20 Oct 1998; decree 21 Dec 1998 [S4b][S4]. Status: CHURCH-APPROVED. (Check: Dicastery decree text not read; S4 gives 21 Dec 1998 for pope approval.)
B. Canonization miracle. Matteo Pio Colella, b. 4 Dec 1992, son of Dr Antonio Colella (urologist at Casa Sollievo) and Maria Lucia Ippolito. Illness 20 Jan 2000 (fever, vomiting, purple spots); septic shock, heart stopped once, respirator, coma; 8 days later woke; said an old man with white beard and brown habit said "you will get well quickly" and picked Padre Pio from a photo; CT 27 Jan clean; home 6 Feb 2000 [S4b]. Mother reported vision at tomb and flower smell [S4b]. Process: diocesan 11 Jun 2000-17 Oct 2001 [S4b] (S4: papers sent 23 Oct 2000, conflicts); medical 22 Nov 2001; theologians 11 Dec 2001; cardinals 18 Dec 2001 [S4b]; S4 gives 18 Dec 2000 and decree 20 Dec 2000 (dating CONFLICT between S4 and S4b; S4 sequence looks early since the process ended Oct 2001, so S4b likely right; verify with Dicastery decree). Status: CHURCH-APPROVED.
C. Stigmata 1918-68 [S5]. Status: Church studied; Rossi 1921 "real fact"; Holy Office 1923 said nothing supernatural proven; later cause concluded in beatification. No decree "approves" stigmata as miracle; JPII spoke of "stigmata" in beatification homily [S1]. Status: CHURCH-RECOGNIZED in homily; CONTESTED by Gemelli, Holy Office 1923, Luzzatto (alleged use of acid).
D. Fragrance. Rossi noted "very intense and pleasant fragrance, similar to the scent of the violet", source unknown [S5]. Blood said to smell of violet/rose/lily [S5]. Also in cases A and B. Status: REPORTED; one visitation witness.
E. Bilocation. Padre Pio's own statement to Rossi (see Q9) [S5]. Cases: Udine 1905 / Rizzani Boschi (she testified at process) [S4c]; St Luigi Orione saw him in St Peter's on 29 Apr 1923 (Therese beatification) [S4c]; Cadorna story, Nov 1917 — dates differ (9 Nov in search summary; 12 Nov in S4c); Cadorna was dismissed early Nov 1917 (not read). Likely LEGEND: no contemporary source read. Padre Nazareno and the dying tertiary [S4c]. Status: REPORTED; none confirmed by Church; Rossi: several healings unconfirmed [S5].
F. Reading of hearts. Padre Pio told Rossi he sometimes felt others' sins or virtues clearly [S5]. Status: REPORTED.
G. Gemma di Giorgi, Sicily, brought 1947 by grandmother, born without pupils; reported to see steamboat and sea; he told grandmother the child sees [S5 citing 1999 book by Malachy Carroll]. Status: REPORTED, popular; no medical process read.
H. Prophecy to Wojtyla (1947, "highest post"): contested; Stickler says yes; Dziwisz denies; Weigel omits; Aurilia: he called him "your holiness" [S5]. CONTESTED.
I. Stigmata disappearance at death and exhumation: stigmata gone in 1968 [S5]; at exhumation 3 Mar 2008 body "fair condition", skull partly skeletal, chin and body well preserved, hands "like manicure", no visible stigmata; silicone mask made from 1968 photo (Gems Studio) [S5][S7]. Status: Church statement by Abp D'Ambrosio; not claimed as miracle; term "incorrupt" used by CNA headline (2016), not by this record. CONTESTED wording.
J. Fasting: friar said he lived 20+ days at Venafro on Eucharist alone [S5]. REPORTED, single account.
K. Levitation, tongues, mentioned as gifts by followers [S5]. REPORTED.
