# Padre Pio — notes, focus: own-words

Researcher limit note: about 22 tool calls. The saint died in 1968; his writings are under copyright (Edizioni Padre Pio da Pietrelcina). All quotations below are short Italian originals with our own English. I did not read any full Epistolario text. No public-domain English translation exists.

## 1. Identity
- Born Francesco Forgione, Pietrelcina (Benevento), 25 May 1887; baptized next day. Parents Grazio (Orazio) Forgione and Maria Giuseppa De Nunzio (Di Nunzio). [S1] [S3]
- Religious name Fra Pio from 22 Jan 1903 (Capuchin habit, novitiate Morcone). Solemn vows 27 Jan 1907. Ordained priest 10 Aug 1910, Benevento. [S1]
- At San Giovanni Rotondo from Sept 1916 (S1) (S3 says first arrival 28 July 1916) until death 23 Sept 1968, age 81. [S1] [S3]
- Blessed 2 May 1999; canonized 16 June 2002, both by John Paul II, St Peter's Square. [S1]
- Feast: 23 Sept (Catholic only; no Orthodox feast). [S1]
- Tradition type: Catholic only. Tier A. Names: Padre Pio; Pio da Pietrelcina; San Pio da Pietrelcina; "Santo Frate del Gargano" (John Paul II's phrase) [S2].
- Order: Friars Minor Capuchin. Founded Casa Sollievo della Sofferenza hospital (opened 5 May 1956) [S1] and prayer groups (Gruppi di Preghiera) [S1][S2][S3].

## 3. Timeline (own-words angle)
- 1892 (age 5): later said he saw the Sacred Heart and Our Lady daily in the church at Pietrelcina; thought all children did. Source is hagiographic. [S3]
- 1 Jan 1903: said a vision after Communion foretold a lifelong struggle with Satan. 5 Jan: last night at home, vision of God and Mary encouraging him. [S3] NEW for English pages? Unclear.
- 1903-1909: chronic illnesses: vomiting, fainting, headaches, bronchitis, tuberculosis (military discharge 1917/1918 on lung grounds). [S3]
- 7 Sept 1910 (Piana Romana hut, Pietrelcina): later said first marks appeared there. [S3]
- 8 Sept 1911: first written report of marks to his spiritual director in San Marco in Lamis. He said he stayed silent from "that cursed shame". [S3]
- 9 Sept 1911: he told the pastor Don Salvatore Pannullo: "Zi' Tore, fatemi la carità, chiediamo a Gesù che mi tolga questa confusione." Pannullo answered that, if it was God's will, he must bend to it. Dialogue is in S3 with no cited source: check. [S3]
- 10 Oct 1911: letter to Fr Agostino saying he would receive visible stigmata, "specie in una mano", and that prayer would remove the sign but not the pain. He also said he suffered crowning of thorns and flagellation almost weekly. [S3]
- 1911-1913: eleven letters to Frs Agostino and Benedetto contain large parts copied word for word from Gemma Galgani's writings (Gemma, first stigmatic saint of the 20th century). Critical point from scholarship cited by S3; an important conflict for the writer. [S3]
- 7 Dec 1911 to 17 Feb 1916: lives in Pietrelcina at brother Michele's house (health leave). [S3]
- 1914 (feast of the Assumption, 15 Aug): letter quoted in the canonization homily on the Cross and abnegation, Epistolario II p.155. [S2]
- 6 Nov 1915: military service, Naples; discharged after medical review (S3 says 16 March 1918 formal discharge, double bronchoalveolitis; the Illness section says March 1917 with tuberculosis; the two parts of S3 disagree: check).
- 28 July 1916: first arrival at San Giovanni Rotondo; stays after about a week in Foggia; becomes spiritual director of the seraphic seminary. [S3]
- 5 Aug 1918: reports a heavenly figure with a fiery blade who pierced his soul (transverberation). [S3] His own words in S4 (see Q10).
- 20 Sept 1918: after Mass, in the choir, the stigmata become permanent. His own account is in his letter of 22 Oct 1918 to Fr Benedetto (Epistolario I, about pp. 1218-1219 per a search snippet: check). [S3] [S5]
- 1919: examined by Dr Romanelli (15-16 May), Prof. Bignami (26 July), Dr Giorgio Festa (28 Oct). Reports disagree. [S3]
- April 1920: Fr Agostino Gemelli visits and says the wounds are hysterical; Padre Pio refuses the exam without written authorization. [S3] S4 repeats this via Mario Guarino (polemical writer; weak).
- 31 May 1923: Holy Office decree "non constat de supernaturalitate"; published in L'Osservatore Romano 5 July. Further decrees to 23 May 1931; ban on public Mass and confession. [S3]
- July 1933: Pius XI lifts the restrictions. [S3] S3 says the Holy Office did not retract its decrees, per some.
- 1940: construction of the hospital begins (9 Jan). [S3] 1956: opens (5 May). [S1]
- 1942: after Pius XII's call for prayer for peace (17 Feb), Padre Pio urges spiritual children to form prayer groups. [S3]
- 1960: John XXIII sends Mgr Maccari to investigate; his private notes (25 June 1960) show deep worry (published by Luzzatto 2007). [S3]
- 30 July 1964: Paul VI restores full freedom of ministry. [S3]
- 22 Sept 1968: last Mass, the 50th anniversary of the stigmata; 23 Sept, about 2:30 a.m. he dies in his cell, holding the rosary, saying "Gesù, Maria". Funeral 26 Sept, over 100,000 people. [S3] Stigmata disappeared before death (S3 gives conflicting accounts).

## 4. Character
- Calls himself "un povero frate che prega" (in S1 and S2). [S1][S2]
- Told a superior he acted only out of obedience (Epist. I p.807), per John Paul II. [S2]
- Struggles with vainglory, which he calls a martyrdom (Q8, Q9). [S4]
- Acute shame about the marks and a wish for hiddenness: "tutto nel nascondimento". [S3]
- Humor, in sayings given by Wikiquote with third-hand sources (use only after checking): "È meglio un topo tra due gatti che un malato tra due medici" (heard from Prof. Bruno Pavone, via Fr D'Ascanio); to Mrs Coppi: "Signora Coppi, stai attenta che non ti accoppi" (Coppi cyclist's wife, 1953). [S4: weak]
- Critics: Gemelli, Bignami, Maccari, historian Luzzatto, psychiatrist Cancrini (histrionic disorder opinion). Defenders: Festa, Paul VI, John Paul II. [S3] [S4]
- Confessor up to 18 hours a day; about 600,000 confessions in 50 years (estimate, S3 citing popular literature). Pilgrims met "apparent harshness" but returned for absolution (John Paul II). [S3] [S2]

## 5. Quotations
Original Italian, our English. All are short. Rights: copyrighted Epistolario; quoted as brief excerpts for commentary. Locators as given by the secondary source. Verify against the Epistolario if possible.
1. "Sono un povero frate che prega." — "I am a poor friar who prays." (S2; also S1: "Voglio essere soltanto un povero frate che prega" - "I want only to be a poor friar who prays.") Locator: no primary citation. Source: S1/S2.
2. "La preghiera è la migliore arma che abbiamo, una chiave che apre il Cuore di Dio." — "Prayer is the best weapon we have, a key that opens God's heart." S2 (no citation). S1 gives a variant: "Nei libri cerchiamo Dio, nella preghiera Lo troviamo..." — "In books we look for God; in prayer we find him."
3. "Sotto la Croce s'impara ad amare." — "Under the Cross one learns to love." Words Christ is said to have spoken to him. Epist. I p.339, via S2.
4. "Il Calvario è il monte dei Santi." — "Calvary is the mountain of the saints." S2 (no citation).
5. "Opero solamente per ubbidirvi..." — "I work only to obey you..." to a superior; Epist. I p.807, via S2.
6. "Sembra che Gesù non abbia altra cura per le mani se non quella di santificare l'anima vostra." — "It seems Jesus has no other care in his hands than to make your soul holy." Epist. II p.155, via S2.
7. "Abbandonatevi pienamente sul cuore divino di Gesù, come un bimbo tra le braccia della madre." — "Give yourselves wholly to the divine heart of Jesus, like a child in its mother's arms." From John Paul II's 1999 beatification homily (S1), as a saying he repeated.
8. "Dio mio! che martirio è la tentazione della vanagloria." — "My God! What a martyrdom the temptation of vainglory is." Epistolario, 314 (1995), via S4.
9. "Io cerco Dio, ma dove rinvenirlo?" — "I seek God, but where can I find him?" Epistolario, 1028 (1995), via S4. A line from his dry period.
10. On 5 Aug 1918 (S4, no. 1065): "A stento emisi un lamento; mi sentivo morire." — "I barely let out a cry; I felt I was dying." Longer passage describes the figure with a long iron blade with fire at the point.
11. "Voglio soffrire. È questa la mia brama." — "I want to suffer. This is my longing." Epistolario 1051 (1995), via S4.
12. "Gesù mi dice che nell'amore è lui che diletta me; nei dolori, invece, sono io che diletto lui." — "Jesus tells me that in love he delights me; in sorrows I delight him." Ep. 335 (1995), via S4.
13. "Il mio cuore era scomparso, come una goccia d'acqua che si smarrisce in un mare." — "My heart had vanished, like a drop of water lost in a sea." Ep. 273 (1995), via S4. Part of an account of a morning of union with Christ.
14. "Tieni nel tuo cuore Gesù crocifisso e tutte le croci del mondo ti sembreranno rose." — "Keep Jesus crucified in your heart and all the crosses of the world will seem like roses." Ep. III p.827 (S4).
15. "In mezzo al palmo delle mani è apparso un po' di rosso, grande quanto la forma di un centesimo, accompagnato da un forte e acuto dolore." — "In the middle of the palm of my hands a little red appeared, the size of a cent coin, with sharp pain." Letter of Sept 1911 per S3 (S3's dating is loose: 8 Sept 1911).
16. "Zi' Tore, fatemi la carità, chiediamo a Gesù che mi tolga questa confusione. Voglio soffrire, morire di sofferenza, ma tutto nel nascondimento." — "Uncle Tore, do me the kindness: let us ask Jesus to take this confusion from me. I want to suffer, to die of suffering, but all in hiding." S3, Sept 1911, spoken to the pastor. Source of the dialogue not named in S3.
17. "Per arrivare a raggiungere l'ultimo nostro fine bisogna seguire il divin Capo..." — "To reach our final end we must follow the divine Head, who leads the chosen soul by no other path than the one he walked, that of self-denial and the Cross." Assumption 1914; Epist. II p.155 via S2 (a longer quotation; shorten to one clause).
18. "Una sola cosa mi resta amica: la morte." — "Only one thing remains my friend: death." Ep. 767 (1995), via S4.
19. On priests who displeased Christ (vision): Jesus cried "Macellai!" — "Butchers!" Ep. 350 (1995), via S4.
20. Prayer "Resta con me, Signore": widely attributed to him as a post-Communion prayer. A WebSearch summary says he said it daily from the time of the Holy Office ban. I did not read the Italian text, and authorship is uncertain. Do not quote until checked.
Not found: any public-domain text, any quotation from the 20 Sept 1918 letter in Italian that I could copy exactly. A writer who wants the letter must find the Italian original in Epistolario I.

7. 1920: Gemelli at the convent door and the refusal to be examined without written orders. [S3]
8. 22 Sept 1968: last Mass, nearly falls on the altar steps; 23 Sept dies at 2:30 with rosary, saying "Gesù, Maria". [S3]
9. Rome, 1947: Karol Wojtyla visit and "prophecy" — legend; JPII denied it. [S3] Mark as legend.

## 7. Miracles (own-words focus: brief; church-miracles agent covers the rest)
- Stigmata, 1910-1918 provisional, 20 Sept 1918 permanent until about 1968; examined 1919 by Romanelli, Bignami, Festa with split verdicts. Status: not formally ruled on; Holy Office 1923 "non constat". [S3]
- Beatification miracle: Consiglia De Martino, Salerno, 1-6 Nov 1995; ruptured thoracic duct; healed without treatment; decree 21 Dec 1998. Approved. [S1][S3]
- Canonization miracle: Matteo Pio Colella, San Giovanni Rotondo, 20 Jan 2000; fulminant bacterial meningitis; recovered; decree 20 Dec 2001. Approved. [S1][S3] (S4 quotes a sceptic, Guarino: ignore.)
- Bilocation reported by Don Luigi Orione in 1925 in St Peter's Square. Popular/recorded, not approved. [S3]
- Hyperthermia 48 C recorded by Dr Festa 1920; popular/disputed. [S3]
