# Reverence pass: Saint John Maximovitch (standard, tier B)

Date: 2026-10-05. Copies of the originals: `biography.html.before-reverence`, `miracles.html.before-reverence`, `summary.txt.before-reverence`. Teachings files and `entry.json` untouched. Nothing uploaded.

Rule followed for God as actor (coordinator's note, now reverence-pass.md point 1): God is named as the one who acts only where the source names Him. Where the source says "he was healed" or "worked by his prayers", the text says that.

Every restored detail below was checked in the cited source text during this pass: S1 (Mileant, orthochristian.com/54575), S5 (1993 commission report, Sanidopoulos reprint), S7 (1994 ROCOR booklet on azbyka, Russian), S21 (Perekrestov, orthochristian.com/80365), and the 2019 San Francisco booklet PDF (synod.com).

Lint after the pass: biography no MUST FIX (grade 7.1, unchanged; the three 9-word h2 CHECKs predate this pass); miracles no MUST FIX and no "meta-commentary" lines.
