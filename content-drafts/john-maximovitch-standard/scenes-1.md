## SC-01 — Parade day, 1909 (Poltava, before the cathedral)
- Status: full (two sources, but they differ on details)
- When and where: 1909, a ceremonial march of the Poltava Cadet Corps through the city of Poltava, for the 200th anniversary of the Battle of Poltava. The march passed the Poltava cathedral. [S7][S17]
- Who was there: Michael (Misha) Maximovitch, a cadet; his fellow cadets; the officers; Grand Duke Konstantin Konstantinovich [S7]. S17 names no Grand Duke: it says an "Inspector General of Military Academies" was visiting for the anniversary.
- What happened, in order:
  1. Michael entered the Corps at 11 (1907; S7, S4, S1 give 1907). [S7][S4][S1]
  2. In 1909, at 13, the cadets marched past the cathedral. Michael turned to it and crossed himself. [S7]
  3. S17 adds that he was the only cadet who took off his cap. [S17]
  4. The boys mocked him for a long time and the authorities punished him. [S7]
  5. S7: the Grand Duke stepped in, and the punishment became a written commendation for the boy's healthy religious feeling. The mockery turned to respect. [S7]
  6. S17: the Inspector General told the whole corps: "Though he was wrong, well done nonetheless!" [S17, paraphrase of a quoted line]
- Details: S7 says he was quiet and religious and "little like a soldier" (мало походил на солдата). The charge was "violation of order" (нарушение порядка). [S7]
- Words spoken: only S17's line above, in English, with no Russian. Do not print it as the Grand Duke's words.
- Testimony: S7 (facts-only; 40-word quote at most): «Когда они проходили перед Полтавским собором, Михаил повернулся к нему и – перекрестился.»
  plain: As they passed the Poltava cathedral, Michael turned toward it and crossed himself.
- What changed: the boy's faith became public; the later respect of his comrades follows (S7 only).
- Not in the sources: the weather; the cathedral's name; how many boys laughed; what the punishment was; what he felt. The Grand Duke's presence is S7 only and S17 conflicts (unnamed Inspector General). Write "one account says the Grand Duke, another an inspector general." No source says it was a Sunday or a feast.
- Age: S7 says he was 13 in 1909. "Turned 11" in S7 refers to entering the Corps. S1 and S4 give entry 1907, so 11 fits.
- Sources: S7, S17, S4, S1.

## SC-02 — Kharkov, 1914–1918
- Status: full
- When and where: Kharkov, 1914–1918. The 1934 address looks back at it. [S1][S4][S8]
- Who was there: Michael; his parents (who pressed him to study law, S7, S4); Archbishop Anthony (Khrapovitsky) of Kharkov, his guide [S8][S1][S4].
- What happened, in order:
  1. After the Cadet Corps he wanted to enter the Kiev Theological Academy. His parents insisted on law at Kharkov. "For obedience's sake" (послушания ради) he prepared for a legal career. [S7][S4]
  2. At Kharkov he read the lives of the saints more than he went to class, and still did well. [S1][S7]
  3. Archbishop Anthony noticed him, kept him close, and guided his spiritual life. [S1][S8]
  4. Other boys called religion "grandmothers' tales"; he saw the wisdom of the saints' lives against the university course (S7, facts-only).
  5. In 1918 he graduated in law. [S1][S4]
- Details: S7 says Archbishop Meletius (d. 1841) lay in Kharkov, an ascetic who did not sleep, and panikhidas were served at his tomb under the church; S7 draws the parallel with John. S7 only.
- Words spoken: his own 1934 address, in his words (Russian, S8-ru).
- Inner life: the address leaves out the law degree and his parents' insistence. It says only that the monastery and church "attracted me more" than the place where he studied. The law and obedience come from S7 and S4, not from him. Keep the two apart.
- Testimony (S8-ru, paragraph 4 of the Russian page; his own words, 1934; rights: see above, translate ourselves):
  «День окончания мною среднего учебного заведения совпал со днем вступления на кафедру города, где предстояло проходить высшее образование, нового иерарха (ныне митрополит Киевский Антоний, тогда архиепископ Харьковский), который сделался навсегда руководителем моей духовной жизни. Изучая светские науки, я все больше углублялся в изучение науки наук, в изучение духовной жизни. Монастырь, где жил архипастырь, и церковь привлекали меня больше, чем место где я учился высшим светским наукам.»
  plain: The day I finished secondary school was the day a new bishop took over the city where I was to study. He was Archbishop Anthony of Kharkov, later Metropolitan of Kiev, and he became my guide in spiritual life for good. While I studied worldly sciences, I went deeper into the science of sciences, the spiritual life. The monastery where the archbishop lived, and the church, drew me more than the place where I studied the highest worldly sciences.
- Also, from paragraph 1: «с первых дней, что начал сознавать себя, желал служить правде и истине.» plain: From the first days I became aware of myself, I wanted to serve justice and truth. (Note: S7 prints this as "праведности и истине", an edited form. The original on azbyka reads "правде и истине". Use the original.)
- Quotes for obedience (S8-ru, later paragraph): «из послушания церковной власти и моему духовному руководителю покоряюсь сему избранию» plain: out of obedience to the Church authorities and to my spiritual guide, I submit to this choice.
- What changed: he leaves law behind in spirit, and the Russian state collapses, which he says convinced him to give himself to God alone (S8-ru, paragraph 4 end). Revolution and flight from Crimea follow (S4: Nov 1920).
- Not in the sources: any talk with his parents; the lectures he skipped; any meeting with Anthony beyond S1's "took special pains to become acquainted." The bell and jail story is S7 only and is left out.
- Sources: S8-ru, S8, S7, S1, S4.

## SC-03 — Bitol seminary dormitory at night, late 1920s–1934
- Status: full for the facts; thin on a named student (none exists in the sources)
- When and where: the seminary of St. John the Theologian, Bitol (Okhrida diocese), Serbia. Start year: 1927 (S4), 1928 (S7). Write "late 1920s." [S1][S4][S7]
- Who was there: Hieromonk John, teacher of pastoral theology and church history [S4] / of the Law of God [S7]; 400–500 students [S7]. Bishop Nikolai Velimirovich was the diocese's ruling bishop [S1]. Archbishop Averky (then a young hieromonk) heard the students speak of him [S1].
- What happened, in order:
  1. The students saw he stayed up after everyone else was in bed. [S1]
  2. He went through the dormitories at night, picked up blankets that had fallen, covered the sleepers, and made the Sign of the Cross over them. [S1]
  3. Later they learned he hardly slept, never in a bed: an hour or two seated, or bent over on the floor before icons. [S1]
  4. Years afterward he said himself he had not slept lying in a bed since his vows. [S1]
  5. Bishop Nikolai said: "If you wish to see a living saint, go to Bitol to Father John." [S1] (S4 and S7 give other wordings.)
- Details: S7: he knew every student and his needs. Entering or leaving a room he blessed them with the Sign of the Cross. [S7]
- Words spoken: Velimirovich, English from S1 (above). Original Serbian not found.
- Testimony: an unnamed student, S7 (facts-only, 40 words at most): «Каждую ночь он, как ангелъ-хранитель, оберегал нас: одному поправлял подушку, другому одеяло.»
  plain: Every night, like a guardian angel, he looked after us. He fixed one boy's pillow and another boy's blanket.
  Also, same student: «Отец Иоанн любил нас всех, и мы – его.» plain: Father John loved us all, and we loved him.
  Rights: S7 (1994 booklet) is facts-only; the two lines are 30 words together.
- What changed: the students find out the hidden prayer life; his fame as a "living saint" spreads (S1).
- Not in the sources: the student's name; the night in question; the room, the season, the lamp or candle; whether anyone woke. S7 says "pillow" and "blanket"; S1 says only blankets and the Sign of the Cross. Do not invent a boy who woke.
- Sources: S1 ("Belgrade"), S7 ("Эмиграция. В Югославии"), S4.

## SC-04 — Holy Trinity Church, Belgrade, June 10, 1934
- Status: full
- When and where: May 27/June 10, 1934, the Russian Church of the Holy Trinity, Belgrade. The Synod of the Church Abroad named him on May 20/June 3 (S4 gives 3 June). [S8][S4] S1, S2 give "May 28"; use S8 and S4.
- Who was there: Metropolitan Anthony (Khrapovitsky), who led the consecration; John. The address names Archbishop Varlaam (earlier teacher) and Bishop Victor "in absentia." [S8][S4]
- What happened, in order:
  1. The Synod chose him for Shanghai, vicar of the Chinese and Peking diocese. [S8][S4]
  2. Shortly before, he told Maria Dmitrievna Shatilova it was a mistake; the next day he said it was bigger than he thought. [S4]; S1 adds the speech defect and Moses.
  3. At the consecration he gave an address (S8). He said he did not dare think himself worthy but feared to refuse, hearing "feed my sheep."
  4. He said he submitted "out of obedience" to the Church authorities and his spiritual guide, "not for honor and power."
  5. Anthony wrote the same day to Archbishop Dimitry (Voznesensky) of Hailar. [S8][S4]
- Details: S1: Anthony was very old; John was the last bishop he consecrated. [S1]
- Words spoken, John (S8-ru; English S8):
  «Не смею помыслить себя достойным сего сана, сознавая греховность свою, но боюсь и отрекаться от него»
  plain: I do not dare think myself worthy of this rank, knowing my sinfulness, but I am afraid to refuse it.
  «Нет у него личной жизни»  plain: He has no personal life. (about the archpastor)
  «Нельзя проповедывать Евангелие, не проявляя любовь в делах.»  plain: You cannot preach the gospel without showing love in deeds.
  «Сознаю свои слабые силы; из послушания церковной власти и моему духовному руководителю покоряюсь сему избранию не ради чести и власти»  plain: I know my strength is weak. Out of obedience to Church authority and my spiritual guide, I accept this choice, not for honor or power.
  «Священнослужитель не смеет превращаться в обыкновенного политического или общественного деятеля»  plain: A clergyman must not turn into an ordinary political or public figure.
- Testimony: Anthony's letter, Russian (S4, ru.wikipedia, footnote 16 not visible; Anthony died 1936, so the text is public domain; the quoted wording comes via Wikipedia and I did not see the original print):
  «Как мою душу, как моё сердце посылаю к Вам Владыку Епископа Иоанна. Этот маленький, слабый физически человек, по виду почти как дитя — есть чудо аскетической стойкости и строгости в наше время всеобщего духовного расслабления»
  plain: In place of myself, as my soul and my heart, I send you Bishop John. This small man, weak in body, almost like a child to look at, is a miracle of ascetic firmness and strictness in our age of general spiritual slackness.
  English as printed: S1 ("a miracle of ascetic firmness and strictness in our time of total spiritual enfeeblement"); S8 ("a miracle of ascetic perseverance and rigor").
  Missing: the Russian of the first lines ("I am already so old and weak..."). S8 and S1 have an English version only; I did not find the Russian.
- Inner life: his own words above; S1 Shatilova's story (his own words about "mistake") only through S4 in Russian: «Ошибка оказалась большей, чем я думал. Это именно меня назначили…» plain: The mistake was bigger than I thought. They did mean me.
- What changed: he is a bishop, sent east. He reaches Shanghai on December 4, 1934. [S8][S4]
- Not in the sources: the weather; the number in the church; who sang; what Anthony said aloud; whether the address came before or after the laying on of hands (the Russian title says "при наречении," the naming, which in Orthodox practice is the ritual before the consecration; no source states the order). The phrase "my soul, my heart" is from the written letter, not a spoken farewell.
- Sources: S8-ru, S8, S4, S1.
