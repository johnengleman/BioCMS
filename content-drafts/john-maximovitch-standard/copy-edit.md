## Listed for the checker (not changed)

Facts and wording I could not fix without a fact:
1. Summary: "gave his shoes to beggars." The biography never says this; it says the girls' knitted socks ended up on beggars. Check the source, or change the summary to match the biography.
2. Opening and summary: "He went with them to Shanghai … France … California" and "followed a people … to China". The Shanghai Russians were there before him, and the Tubabao people went mostly to the United States and Australia, not France. This is a framing choice. Is it fair to the sources?
3. "In 1987 Father Seraphim Rose and Father Herman Podmoshensky … printed the memories." Father Seraphim Rose reposed in 1982. Check the year, or say that the 1987 book used his earlier work.
4. "From 1951 the people of Tubabao began to leave the island for the United States and Australia, and almost the whole camp came to the New World." Australia is not the New World. Check the source wording.
5. Menshikova "had refused the injections or taken them carelessly." This "or" puts a doubt inside the story. If the sources disagree, choose one and put the other in the Note.
6. "On those days he gave them Christmas trees and plays." "Those days" covers Easter too. Check whether the trees were only at Christmas.
7. "A monk named Father Peter had kept the key" (crypt) and "Father Peter Perekrestov, who was in the crypt that night" (Note). Are these two different men? A reader may think they are the same.
8. Lawsuit: "Sixteen members of the parish council and their helpers had filed it. It named sixteen members of the former council." Please confirm both numbers. Also, is the newspaper "petition from about a dozen parishioners" the same as the "letter" to the bishops? The text leaves this unclear.
