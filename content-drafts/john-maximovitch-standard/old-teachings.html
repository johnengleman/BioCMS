Faith expressed in a life

In What do we mean by the word ORTHODOX?, John connects Christian identity with both belief and conduct. A name alone does not establish faithfulness: profession of Christ must be expressed in a life that honors God. He writes from an explicitly Orthodox understanding of the Church, not from the idea that doctrinal differences are immaterial. The practical challenge in the text is integrity between confession and action. Read the essay, especially its final paragraph.

The Cross as the sign of salvation

In his sermon on the Exaltation of the Cross, John describes Christ transforming an instrument of execution into the sign of salvation. He explains the Church's prayer for God's people as a request for grace and inward strengthening as well as deliverance from calamity. The endurance of the Church through persecution and conflict is central to his account: Christian hope rests in Christ, not in the apparent stability of states or worldly arrangements. Source: sermon excerpt, St John Maximovitch on The Exaltation of the Precious Cross.
