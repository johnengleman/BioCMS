## Fixes made

When he was worn out, he would lay his head on the floor and sleep a few hours near dawn" (S1: "usually in prayer... a few hours of sleep near dawn").
- Ch. 6, Tubabao: "Their bishop blessed the camp" could read as the Filipinos' own bishop. Now "They said the archbishop blessed the camp from four directions every night." (First draft of the fix said "holy man"; the lint flagged it as a glow word, so it uses "the archbishop".)
- Ch. 9, crypt: the almost-daily panikhidas and the Psalter are S1, not Perekrestov, but sat inside his paragraph. They now come first as their own sentences, then "Father Peter Perekrestov ... tells what the years after were like". "going down to the tomb" became "going to the tomb" (S21 says only "going to his tomb").
- Ch. 10, boy: "taller than the priest himself" was wrong (S21: "much taller than I", so than Perekrestov; "the priest" read as the boy's father). Now "the boy is completely healthy now, much taller than he is, and plays college rugby."
