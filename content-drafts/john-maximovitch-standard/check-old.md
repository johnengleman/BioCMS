# Check: St. John Maximovitch (standard, tier B)

Copies of the originals: biography.html.before-check, summary.txt.before-check. Lint: no MUST FIX; one CHECK line (reading grade 7.1, was 7.1; 6,201 words, average sentence 15.5).

## Fixes (location, reason)

Wrong or unsupported facts
- Ch. 10, tomb opening: "no smell of any kind" was wrong. The report says no sweet or foul smell, only a slight smell of damp earth or rust. Fixed.
- Ch. 10: the vestments "were green" became "the white vestments had turned green" (report). The coffin "had rusted through in many places"; Peter "was unable to open" the lock (report), not "the key would not turn". The key-keeper is now "Hieromonk Peter Lukianov" so he is not confused with Father Peter Perekrestov.
- Ch. 10, the boy: Belikow was not one of the 15; the report says father and son waited nearby and were sent for. The boy was touched to John's hands (the old text said "the archbishop's hands", which could mean Archbishop Anthony). Order fixed: the body was already in the new coffin. Perekrestov's words are now "was healed ... is completely healthy now ... plays college rugby" (he did not say "grew up healthy"). Healing stays attributed to his later interview, here and in "A Note on the Sources".
- Ch. 9, crypt: "from the beginning" contradicted Perekrestov ("at first they prayed for him"). Rewritten; he "names none" of the miracles. Cut the unsourced "The Church would need to test that memory". Ch. 10 opening: "had to open the tomb" became "the Synod sent bishops to open the tomb" (report).
- Ch. 7, Christmas 1951: the old text said the message "opened with" "Born today is He Who is our Peace!". It does not (it opens "Christ is born, glorify Him!"). Reworded; "still on Tubabao" became "had come from China to the Philippines" (title of the message). The 1961 Paris church sentence moved to ch. 11 to keep time order.
- Ch. 7, San Francisco arrival: "21 November 1962 ... same day of the year" clashed with "4 December 1934" for Shanghai. The Orthodox Encyclopedia gives 4 December 1962; text now names the feast.
- Ch. 8, Kennedy hospital scene: the three-year-old was her own daughter Musenka, after an operation. "The nurses stared" was invented; the source says the nurses were afraid to enter the ward and a nurse asked next morning who the man in black was. Rewritten.
- Ch. 6, Washington: removed "because the refugees could not stay on an island forever" and "American law ... said nothing about" (no source). Namee: he went to ask the US to admit his people. "Arrived with Alexandrov" is not in Namee; now both were "invited to testify". "Wrote home" became "wrote". "Grateful above all" became "especially" (Namee: "particularly grateful"). "The senators questioned" became "the archbishop was questioned".
- Hook and ch. 6: "led five thousand people out of China" was stronger than the notes (taken to Tubabao; Namee: "had to get his people out"). Now "worked to bring".
- Hook: "known for never going to bed" and the unclear "seminary there" (read as Belgrade). Now "seminary in Bitol ... his students had noticed". "Russians call him" became "He is known as" (only English Wikipedia says it). "Country estate" at his birth: sources say the village of Adamovka; the estate was the summer home. Fixed. "From the first days he could remember" became "was aware of himself" (Russian: "начал сознавать себя").
- Ch. 4: "A true archpastor" (no "true" in the Russian), now "a bishop". "Must not" for «не смеет» became "does not dare". "He began with his childhood" was wrong (he began with the Gospel call); now "spoke of". Address tied to the day of consecration (dated 27 May O.S. = 10 June), not "the service in which he was named".
- Ch. 3, 1927 letter: it does not "end" with the rule, and the rule continues "and will be accepted into the Heavenly Kingdom"; quotation now whole. "Kept writing" became "wrote". Student quotation: the two lines were in the wrong order without an ellipsis; now in source order with an ellipsis. "Years afterward" cut (no date). "That is what his students would call him" cut (invented).
- Ch. 7, orphan letters: "every winter" (three letters only) cut; "He signed as he always did" (Kennedy gives a different closing) became "each of these letters". 1953 letter now notes the month-long visit. Orphanage move to San Francisco is supported (S1, S9).
- Ch. 5: "Shakhmatova ... grown men" became "adults"; "once he bent down" became "sometimes" (S1: "Occasionally"); the girl who said "He squirts you" was one girl. Halloween: "the Russians of San Francisco" became "a group of Russians". "Soaked ... in the street" (socks) trimmed. "Each of them" became "them" (patron saints). "No one ever touched him" is now attributed to his Life. "One after the other" cut; "all of them had to flee" softened. "Every morning" Liturgy became "every day" (sources say daily).
- Ch. 8: "Not all of his critics were liars" took a side; replaced with a neutral line that a later Russian history lists the complaints. "He had made mistakes with staff and paperwork" is an accusation (S4): now "had blamed him". "Crowded into" became "met at". The dozen parishioners' petition: source says a petition printed in a paper, not "written to the Synod". "Newspapers that survive" became "reports we found". "Two bishops who had come to stand by him" trimmed to "who stood by him".
- Ch. 1: invented contrast "Other boys played at war" cut. "Asked to be baptized" became "was baptized" (S7). "The mockery turned to respect" now "The Life says".
- Ch. 2: "most important man in Michael's life" (superlative) cut. "Passed his examinations" became "did well" (S1). Meletius: "never slept" and "still sung" became "did not sleep" and "again and again" (S7: constantly); added that the Life says the same came true for John.
- Ch. 12 and ch. 11: "Most of all he left letters" softened; "hundred reports" is "said to" (not read); Vsevolod sentence tidied; closing now says "a woman he knew", not "a friend". "Printed and read" became "printed".
