At thirteen, a farm girl who could not read heard a Voice in her father's garden at noon. When Saint Michael and the angels went away, she wept and wished they had taken her along. The Voices asked a great deal: leave home and her family, ride through a country at war, and raise the siege of Orléans for a king who had not yet been crowned. Saint Joan of Arc said she would have gone if she had had a hundred fathers and a hundred mothers. Captains doubted her, a king held her back, and a church court tried her for the very Voices she trusted. What would she give to stay faithful to them, and to God's grace?
