- Item 1, father's dream (Q1 p.131-132, 12 March 1431). The record has the condition, but it is about the father's belief, not about the dream coming true. - Item 2, Compiègne (Q4 p.35, Cagny). Cagny says that while she was at Beaulieu, "celui qui estoit son maistre d'ostel avant sa prinse et qui la servit en sa prinson" told her Compiègne would fall, and she answered "Non sera". "One of his servants" was wrong: it was her own steward, at Beaulieu, not a servant of Jean de Luxembourg. Biography ch. 9 now says "The chronicler of her side says that at Beaulieu her steward told her Compiègne would fall."
