## Errors of fact fixed

Opening and childhood
- P4: "knew only the three prayers" and "the judges made her tell them" were too strong. She learned her prayers from her mother and told the judges herself (Murray p. 10). Note 4 now also cites Murray p. 265 ("neither A nor B") and Guérin p. 391.
- Chapter 2 block quote and chapter 1: "about thirteen" is not in the Latin of 22 February (Q1 p. 52). The quote says "thirteen".
- "Her closest friend was Hauviette" became "One of her friends" (Hauviette says "socia sua"; the sources do not rank her).
- Notes 5, 6, 7 gave the wrong or too few pages (Isabellette, Q2 p. 427, was missing).

Leaving home (note 15, 17, 18)
- Laxart "came to her father's house" and she "walked past her father's house": two witnesses, one house. Now: he came to Domrémy; she passed her father's house with him (Guillemette, Q2 p. 416). "A cousin" became "a relative". "She begged him" became "asked him" (Q2 p. 444).
- "He asked her who her lord was": it was Baudricourt (Poulengy, Q2 p. 456). Now "The captain asked".

Chinon and the banner
- The banner image was Dunois's memory, not her answer. She told her judges: lilies, the world, an angel on each side (Q1 p. 78). Text follows her; the Note on the Sources gives both.

Orléans
- Dunois's first mention of "the Maid" now has her name where he hears of her (Q3 p. 3).
- "Bloody boy" is Coutes's "sanglant garson" (Q3 p. 68): now "wretched boy" (see `plan.md`).
- "Over the next two days": the sources do not date the forts (Coutes and d'Aulon compress the days; the Journal puts 4 May on the eve of Ascension). Now "In the days that followed".
- The standard-bearer, not d'Aulon, handed the banner to the Basque (d'Aulon, Q3 p. 216). D'Aulon asked the Basque to follow him into the ditch. Rewritten.
- "She said to d'Aulon, 'we will enter the city tonight'": d'Aulon only heard her say it that day (Q3 p. 217). Fixed.
- The twilight bells were her habit with the army (Dunois, Q3 p. 14), not a scene of the march to Reims. Reworded.

Reims road, Paris, Bourges
- Letter to Burgundy: "against them" is "contre nous", her own side. Now "the king's side" (Q5 p. 127).
- Paris: she went "at the request of the gentlemen", not "the soldiers" (Murray p. 73).
- Bourges: "that autumn" is not in Touroulde (Q3 pp. 85-88). Removed.

Beaurevoir
- Saint Catherine's line: Murray's "do not falter" renders "sine defectu / sans faulte" (without fail) wrongly. Now "You must accept it gladly, without fail" (Q1 p. 151). Note 66 says so.
- Note 59 cited Murray p. 55 for the sortie (it is p. 58). Note 69 cited Q1 p. 162 for the mortal-sin answer (it is p. 161).

Rouen
- "He could have sent her to a church prison, and he would not" is Ladvenu's testimony (Q2 p. 7; Murray p. 162). Now attributed in four words.
- 24 February quote: two skipped sentences, now marked with an ellipsis (Q1 p. 62).
- Basel: Isambart says "une fois" (Q2 p. 4). "Again and again" and "Once" corrected.
- "Seventeen times in February and March": Guérin says seventeen, Murray lists 6 + 9. Removed the count (also in the research paragraph).
- Notary story: Murray p. 305, not p. 304; the speaker (Pierre Daron) heard it from others. Note 76 fixed. "So she told him" became "She was glad, and she told him".
- Saint-Ouen: "Halfway through the bishop's sentence" became "Before the bishop had finished" (Murray p. 130: "in great part").
- 28 May block quote ended with "from fear of the fire" and the next paragraph repeats it in her own words. Cut the first (Q1 p. 457).
- Cauchon's answer dropped "and because you went back to your first offense" without an ellipsis (Q2 p. 4). Restored; note 88 adds Q2 p. 4.
- "The secular power" became "the civil authorities" (Q3 p. 159: "manus saecularis").
- The little cross was made "from the end of a stick" (Massieu, Q2 p. 20; the bull). "Broke a stick" was invented. The church cross now comes in Massieu's order, before the soldiers' "What, priest!"

Note on the Sources, research paragraph, Sources

- Sources list dates: Quicherat vol. 2 is 1844 and vol. 3 is 1845 on the title pages (1842 and 1844 before).

summary.txt
