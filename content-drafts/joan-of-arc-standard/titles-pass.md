# Titles pass (2026-10-05)

Files changed: biography.html, summary.txt. Originals: *.before-titles. miracles.html and entry.json were not touched.

## Added "Saint" or "Blessed"
- Saint Joan (every use of "Joan" and "Joan of Arc" in text, headings, and cite lines, from childhood on). "Jeannette" is kept once as the name she was called at home.
- Saint Thérèse (of Lisieux), with her birth name "Thérèse Martin" given once.
- Pope Saint Pius X (canonized 1954).
- Pope Blessed Pius IX (beatified 2000; Blessed, not a saint).
- Saint Michael, Saint Catherine, Saint Margaret, Saint Martin, Saint John (Gospel; feast day), Saint Peter's: these already had "Saint" and were left as written.

## Left plain, and why
- Dunois, Charles VII (the Dauphin, the king), the duke of Alençon, La Hire, Robert de Baudricourt, Jean d'Aulon, Louis de Coutes, Bertrand de Poulengy, Jean de Metz, Jean de Luxembourg: not saints.
- Bishop Pierre Cauchon, Guillaume Érard, Nicolas Midi, Pierre Maurice, Jean Ricquier, the Cardinal of England: the judges and churchmen of the trial, not saints.
- Brother Isambart de la Pierre, Brother Martin Ladvenu: friars at the trial, not canonized.
- Perrin Drapier, Hauviette, Durand Laxart, Catherine Royer, Marguerite La Touroulde, her parents Jacques d'Arc and Isabelle Romée: not canonized.
- Popes Calixtus III, Pius II, Leo XIII, Pius XI, Pius XII, Benedict XV, and Bishop Dupanloup: not saints.

## Not changed on purpose
- Everything inside quotation marks and block quotes, including "Joan, go in peace", "So died Joan", "Saints Catherine and Margaret", and "Saint Louis and Saint Charlemagne" (Dunois's testimony).
- The titles of Thérèse's plays, the Sources list, and heading ids.
- Place and church names with "Saint-" (Saint-Loup, Saint-Ouen, Saint-Denis, and so on).
- Five headings that the title made too long were shortened. One sentence was split because the title made it 31 words.
