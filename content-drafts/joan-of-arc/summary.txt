At thirteen, at noon in her father's garden, a farm girl heard a voice and was very much frightened. When it told her to raise the siege of Orléans, she answered that she was a poor girl who knew nothing of riding or fighting. Four years later she rode with the army that broke the siege, carrying a banner so that, as she said, she would not kill anyone. Two years after that, a prisoner in irons before a Church court, she was asked whether she was in the grace of God. She had asked her voices for one reward only, the salvation of her soul. Would she keep faith with them when they led her to the fire?
