# Blind review (2026-10-07), first draft (written with the "resistance in each period" line)

Scores: heart 7, story 7, prose 7, whole 7. Verdict: heartbeat for most of its length (Easter opening, sieve, poisoned glass, Saint Maurus, Saint Scholastica's storm — "He had refused her a single night. Now he gave her his own grave."); stops at his death: three chapters after it read like an encyclopedia appendix.

Main weaknesses: tidy explanations after each scene ("It was a hard lesson, and it was a father's lesson."); the widened heart stated four or five times; cost stated, not felt (the nurse, the twelve monasteries); "Saint Gregory says/explains/writes" stands between reader and saint; the Rule chapter a list; the ending trails into Aachen, relics, medal, titles; some old wording hard for plain readers; small repetitions.

One change: end the story when the story ends; after the death, one or two short scenes or a short coda. Second: trust the scene, cut the sentence that explains it.

No revision (policy).
