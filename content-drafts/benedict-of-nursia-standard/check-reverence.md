## Fixes

1. Dead child (ch. 32), biography and miracles. The father's "He is dead ... restore him to life" comes in Gregory before Saint Benedict's "such miracles are not for us". The reviser had put it after. Order corrected. The father's oath not to leave, and Saint Benedict going to the gate with the monks, are in the source.
2. Cave at Subiaco (ch. 37), biography (two places) and miracles. Gregory says Saint Benedict "worketh miracles" in the cave. The pass had "God still works miracles through Saint Benedict". Changed to "Saint Benedict still works miracles". The faith of those who pray stays.
3. Falling wall (ch. 11). Gregory says "he made him sound", with no mention of God in that sentence. "God made the young monk sound" changed to "Saint Benedict made the young monk sound again". The prayer and the place stay.
4. Leper boy (ch. 26). Gregory says "he was by him quickly restored". "Through him God quickly restored" changed to "Saint Benedict quickly restored him".
5. Miracle list opening. "Saint Gregory says plainly that God worked these things through Saint Benedict" was stronger than ch. 30-31, which say he worked wonders by prayers and by power God gave him. Reworded to that.
6. Opening sentence on canonization ("honored as a saint long before the Church had formal rules for canonization"). The notes do not support "formal rules": they say only that the 1220 canonization is unconfirmed, the cult at the cave is old, and 21 March is in early martyrologies. Softened to "The Church has honored Saint Benedict as a saint since early times. Saint Gregory already wrote that Saint Benedict worked miracles in his cave, and early lists of saints keep his death day on 21 March." The definition of canonization was dropped (nothing else uses it).
7. Clergyman of Aquinum (ch. 16). Restored "God's holy martyrs did not heal him", as Gregory writes it.
8. "Church approved the Saint Benedict Medal" (Metten) changed to "Pope Benedict XIV approved", as the biography already says.
