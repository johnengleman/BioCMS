## Listed, not fixed (need a fact or a quotation change)

1. biography.html, Rule chapter: "He must ask of every newcomer whether he really seeks God." In the Rule (ch. 58) a senior monk, not the abbot, tests whether the newcomer truly seeks God. The checker should confirm and correct the fact.
2. miracles.html, restless monk (ch. 4): "he saw a little black boy pulling the monk out". Saint Gregory means a devil in that form, but a reader today may take it as a real child. A fix needs the source wording ("the devil in the form of..."), so it is left for the checker.
3. miracles.html, the shillings (ch. 27): thirteen shillings are found, and "Saint Benedict had twelve given to the man."
