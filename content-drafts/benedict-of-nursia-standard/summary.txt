For three years a young man lived in a cave so narrow and so hidden that one friend fed him by lowering bread on a long rope, with a little bell tied to it so he would hear it coming. He had left his studies in Rome, his father's house, and the nurse who loved him, because he wanted one thing: to please God alone. But the world kept finding him. Monks begged him to be their father and then poisoned his wine. A jealous priest drove him from everything he had built. How does a man who ran from people end up with a heart wide enough to hold them all?
