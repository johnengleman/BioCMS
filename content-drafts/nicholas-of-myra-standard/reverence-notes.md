# Reverence pass (2026-10-05): biography.html, miracles.html, teachings.html

Backups: `*.before-reverence` (summary.txt was backed up and left unchanged). This pass follows the nine points of `reverence-pass.md` and the Saint Francis model. Only tone, framing, and the treatment of God and holy things changed. Every restored prayer, thanksgiving, or holy name below was read in the source text itself this session (Michael's Life, the Praxis in Jones's English, Nicephorus in Jones's English, the Greek account at Fordham, the OCA pages, the St. Nicholas Center slap page). Keys: S2 = Michael the Archimandrite, Life (section numbers); S3 = Praxis de stratelatis; S8 = Greek account of the translation; S9 = Nicephorus of Bari; S12 = Encomium under the name of Saint Proclus; S16 = St. Nicholas Center, "Bishop Nicholas Loses His Cool"; T-S1 = OCA troparia for 6 December; T-S2 = OCA life for 9 May.

Words changed throughout: "attributed to" and "credited to" (sermons) became "under the name of" or "carries the name of" in the bodies and `<cite>` lines; "Status: popular tradition" became the teller and date; "his bones" became "his holy relics" where the text speaks of the relics as the Church does; "the Mother of God" in the prison story became "the Most Holy Mother of God"; "liquid" in the tomb became "the holy oil" (S8 "sacred oil"; S9 "holy liquid"). Sources lists and quotations were not touched. All heading `id` values are unchanged.
