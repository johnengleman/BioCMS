## Fixes (errors of fact)

1. Opening: "He left no writings" and "written down within a century or two of his death". Now "Nothing written in his own lifetime has come down to us" and "written down by the end of the sixth century". Reason: an absolute claim; Anrich dates the Praxis to 460-580 (up to about 240 years), so "a century or two" was too narrow. Eustratios quotes it after 583.
2. Ch. 1: "in the dead of night" kept in meaning, now "at night" (Michael §12). "Everyone would know who had given it" is now "it would put Nicholas's own name on the gift" (Michael §12: not to trumpet his own charity; Metaphrastes: a good deed not to be identified as his).
3. Ch. 1 block quote (Golden Legend, the dying words): "I give my spirit" is now "I commend my spirit" (Caxton: "commend").
4. Ch. 4: Theognes. "The bishop of a breakaway sect" and "some anger had come between them along the way" were not in Andrew. Andrew: "bishop of the church of the Marcianists"; "perhaps a hint of some kind of irritation". Now: "a bishop of the Marcianists, a group apart from the Church"; "brought the man back to what was good, though a little anger had come between them". The Note on the Sources already says Cioffari cannot say who he was.
5. Ch. 5 (officers): "That was not enough for the men who had paid" (copy editor's list, item 1). The Praxis says "those who were plotting against the officers". Now "those who plotted against them". "Why keep them alive?" is now "Why do you put them in prison but leave them alive?" (Praxis). "The same charge" is now "said the officers were still plotting from prison" (Praxis).
6. Ch. 5: Proclus block quote. "Tell him not to answer my anger with anger, but to ask God to forgive what I have done" had "my" and a changed object. Now "not to repay anger with anger, but to ask forgiveness for what I have done against God" (Proclus §5).
7. Ch. 5: "Saint Nicholas was in Myra, far across the sea" is now "far away" (Praxis "far from us"; no source names the sea).
8. Ch. 5: "he had never left his home" is now "he had not left Myra" (Proclus §3: "without leaving your home, redeem the captives"). It was an absolute claim.
9. Ch. 4: "Saint Nicholas said that the condemned men must be coming out by now" is now "should already be on their way out" (copy editor's list, item 3). The Praxis does not say out of where, so the new wording does not say either.
10. Ch. 6: "The people of Myra remembered how he looked" is now "The Church has always remembered how he looked". Michael §40 gives an account "from the beginning, in tradition", told by a monk; it does not name Myra's people.
11. Ch. 9: "On 9 May both Churches remember the day he came to Bari" is now "the Catholic Church and the Russian Orthodox Church", and "within a few years of 1087" is now "soon after 1087". The OCA page says the Greek Church did not adopt the feast and that the Russian Church kept it "soon after the year 1087"; Patriarch Kirill says the Roman Catholics keep it on 9 May.
12. Summary: the sentence that gave away Byrra (sword taken from the executioner, the turn on the governor) is rewritten. It now shows the three men kneeling with linen over their faces, the raised sword, and the bishop running to reach them (Praxis). The outcome is not told. 103 words.
13. Note on the Sources, Praxis paragraph (copy editor's list, item 2): "It then calls the governor Eustathius, and this account follows the governor" is now: the Praxis names Judge Datianus as the man who sentenced the three; later Saint Nicholas faces the governor, Eustathius, and Michael names Eustathius too; this account tells only the meeting with Eustathius and leaves Datianus unnamed.
14. Note on the Sources: "Cioffari thinks the election of a layman by acclamation" is now "the popular election of a layman" (Cioffari's words). "Nicaea first appears in a eulogy by Nicetas" is now "Pearse's list gives a eulogy by Nicetas ... as the first mention", because Cioffari holds that Theodore the Lector (about 515) names Nicholas at Nicaea. In the Sion paragraph, "a storm calmed at sea" is now "a storm calmed on that voyage" so it is not mixed up with the sailors' story.
