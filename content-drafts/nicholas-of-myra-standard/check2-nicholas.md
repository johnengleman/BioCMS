- Metaphrastes at Nicaea: "The admirable Nicholas helped to bring this about as a member of the sacred synod." Exact in the St Nicholas Center translation (Metaphrastes, "Council of Nicaea"). OK.

## 3. miracles.html

Checked each account against notes.md, the cards, and Michael's Life (secs. 5, 17, 21-24, 28-29, 31, 34-41, 44-48).

- Vial before the sword: in both Nicephorus and the Greek account, the glass vial falls before Matthew's sword. The list had the sword entry ("threatens to leave Myra") before the vial entry. The two entries are now in story order: vial (m09), sword and vision, coffin (m10).

- "April 1087" (m09): Nicephorus says 11 April, the Greek account 20 April. OK.
- Cures "that night and the next day" (m11): Nicephorus has "that night and on Monday", the Greek account "that night and the following morning". The entry already says both. OK.
- "Threatens to leave Myra": rewritten so Matthew is introduced and the order matches the sources: sword drawn, the monk's account of the vision, and the three people of Myra. The stades are kept ("twelve stades away, a little over a mile").
- Becalmed ship: "a north wind pushed the ships back as far as Patara" changed to "blew against the ships all the way to Patara" (Nicephorus).
- Ammonios: "died on the voyage" is in no source on the cards (Pearse only says the sailor was raised from death). Removed.
- Dowry status line: see section 1 (worship-texts claim).
- Election quotation: matched to sec. 23 (see section 2).
- Golden Legend, Andrew of Crete, Metaphrastes, Vita Compilata, Praxis, Martyrology lines: all agree with the cards.
- Intro: "our sources record no Church inquiry" and "makes no claim of scientific proof, and it does not dismiss" opened the list. They are against reverence-pass.md and the tone rule. Opening now presents the accounts as the Church's memory from the oldest tellers. The inquiry and proof sentence moved to the start of "A Note on the Sources".
