## Errors of fact fixed

1. **Opening quotation "more clearly"** (ch. 1, n. 5). Latin: "quod ipsum mundum clarius illustrabit". "More clearly" had nothing to compare with. Now "will shine very brightly on the world"; the Process (W3.28, W6.12) says "mucho/grandemente". Note 5 explains.
2. **"Neither Pope Gregory nor the bishop of Ostia"** (ch. 6, n. 42). Gregory was the bishop of Ostia before he was Pope; the witnesses (W2.22, W3.14) speak in 1253, when Rainaldo held that see (Gilliat-Smith p. 48; Robinson App. I p. 96). Now "Cardinal Rainaldo, the bishop of Ostia when they spoke".
3. **Juan Ventura** (ch. 12, n. 100). W20.9: "lo vieron todos los de la plaza, y corrieron junto con él": the crowd saw the man and ran with him; the healing was at the tomb. Reordered so "it" is gone.
4. **"with your seal"** (ch. 12, n. 96). Now the Pope's order is quoted as his own words to the bishop (Process, bull §5).
5. **"his companion Brother Philip"** (ch. 3). The Process says only "fray Felipe"; no saved source says he is Saint Francis's companion. Now "Brother Philip".
6. **Francisca "later remembered"** a Friday in September (ch. 7, n. 54). W9.2 says "según creía". Now "She thought".
7. **"Cardinals and bishops came ... every day"** (ch. 11, n. 82). The Legend says "frequent visits". Now "often".
8. **Opening: "a Pope offered her property, and she asked instead for the right to own none."** It is true of Gregory IX ("as you have asked", letter of 17 September 1228) but read as if Innocent III. Now "The Pope offered her property, and she asked him instead..."; note 3 adds the letter.
9. **Summary.** "vow of poverty" is now "her vow" (the sources say "vow"). The emperor's soldiers did not threaten her poverty; now they are a danger to her sisters.
10. **Notes.** Note 29: Legend p. 54, not 53 (Hortulana entering the Order). Note 17: added Legend p. 16 (the relatives learn where she went).
