Saint Clare of Assisi was a knight's daughter, raised to marry among the great and powerful. At about eighteen she gave it all up to follow Saint Francis and to live as poor as Christ, whose mother laid him in a manger. Later she asked a Pope for a favor that, he said, no one had ever asked of the Church before: the right to own nothing at all. What did she see in poverty that her family could not? And in forty-two years behind the walls of San Damiano, who would try to take it from her?
