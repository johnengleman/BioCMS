## Listed for the fact checker (not fixed)

1. **Quotation (chapter 1):** "You will give birth safely to a light that will shine on the world more clearly." "More clearly" has no comparison and reads oddly. Please translate it again from the Latin, *clarius illuminabit* (perhaps "shine brightly on the world"). I did not edit it.
2. **"Neither Pope Gregory nor the bishop of Ostia" (chapter 6):** two paragraphs earlier, the reader learns that Pope Gregory himself was the bishop of Ostia. So the sentence reads as one man counted twice. The witness (Process W1.13, W3.14, "ni por el papa ni por el obispo de Ostia") must mean the later bishop of Ostia, Cardinal Rainaldo (facts.md). Please confirm, and if it is so, write "nor Cardinal Rainaldo, the later bishop of Ostia".
3. **Juan Ventura (chapter 12):** "everyone in the square saw it and ran with the man to the tomb." The order is unclear: if "it" is the healing, the run to the tomb comes after it. The source (W20.9) says "lo vieron todos los de la plaza" and ran with him to the tomb. Please decide whether "it" is the man being carried, and reword.
4. **"with your seal" (chapter 12):** "Everything he learned was to be sent "faithfully copied in a public document, and with your seal."" "Your" inside a narrated sentence is a little hard to follow. Perhaps quote the Pope directly to the bishop, or paraphrase without quotation marks.
5. **Church words without an explanation:** "Rule" (first used in the opening and in chapter 6), "the Trinity" (chapter 11), "absolution" (chapter 10), and "reliquary" (chapter 13). The rules card asks for a short explanation the first time. Adding one is the writer's or checker's call.
6. **Titles to confirm:** Pacifica (Blessed Pacifica di Guelfuccio?) and Brother Bernard (Blessed Bernard of Quintavalle?) are both plain in the text. I am not sure that they have the title, so I left them plain.
7. **Opening paragraph:** "A Pope offered her property, and she asked instead for the right to own none." "Instead" makes the request an answer to the Pope's offer. But she asked Pope Innocent III for the privilege, before Pope Gregory IX made his offer. Please check that this order does not mislead.
8. **Summary:** "vow of poverty" and "against … the emperor's soldiers". The biography says only "her vow", and the soldiers did not threaten her poverty. Please check that the summary does not say more than the biography.


## Resolved by the checker (2026-10-06)

1. "clarius illuminabit": the Latin is "clarius illustrabit". Now "a light that will shine very brightly on the world" (the Process says "mucho"). Note 5 explains it.
2. "Neither Pope Gregory nor the bishop of Ostia": now "nor Cardinal Rainaldo, the bishop of Ostia when they spoke". Note 42 adds Gilliat-Smith p. 48.
3. Juan Ventura: "everyone in the square saw the man and ran with him to the tomb" now comes before the healing.
4. "with your seal": now a direct quotation of the Pope's order to the bishop, "Whatever you learn ... see that you send it to us faithfully copied in a public document, and with your seal."
5. Church words: short explanations added for "Rule" (opening), "the Trinity", "absolution", and "reliquary".
6. Pacifica and Brother Bernard: left plain; no saved source gives a title.
7. Opening: "The Pope offered her property, and she asked him instead for the right to own none." The Pope's letter of 1228 says "as you have asked".
8. Summary: "vow of poverty" is now "her vow"; the soldiers are no longer said to threaten her poverty ("keep her sisters safe from the emperor's soldiers").
