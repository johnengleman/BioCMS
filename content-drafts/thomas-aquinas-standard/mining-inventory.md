# Mining inventory, Saint Thomas Aquinas miracle list (2026-10-05)

Sources read: Tocco, Ystoria, catalogue of miracles after death (Prümmer fasc. 1, pp. 145-160); Bernard Gui, Life, chapters and list (same volume, pp. 162-256); the Naples process, 1319 (NP), and the Fossanova process, 1321 (FP), in Laurent fasc. 4, pp. 267-510; the bull of 1323 (FSSPX English). Latin read in the archive.org OCR. Tocco's chapters on his life were not mined. The same miracle appears once, with all its sources. "NP 53" means item 53 of the Naples process; page numbers are in the accounts.

Reading limits: Gui nos. 45-47 are missing in the OCR (a page is repeated). The numbering of some OCR items is uncertain, and the page numbers in the new accounts follow the printed page heads.

## Totals

- Accounts already in the list: 61 lines, of which 17 received merged details (16 accounts changed: tomb-opened-again-fourteenth-year, m02, m04, m05, m06, m07, m08, m09, m10, m13, m16, m30, m34, m37, m45, doubting-blacksmith).
- New accounts found: 99; added: 60 (m49-m108, the limit is 60); not added (limit): 39.
- Skipped: 11 lines.
