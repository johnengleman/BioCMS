## Titled

Saint Thomas Aquinas, Saint Albert the Great (canonized 1931), Saint Louis IX, Saint Bonaventure, Pope Saint Pius V, Pope Saint John Paul II, Saint John Chrysostom, and Saint John the apostle. Saint Dominic, Saint Peter Martyr, and Saint Nicholas already had the title.

## Names left plain, and why

- Reginald of Piperno, William of Tocco, Peter Calo, Bernard Gui, Bartholomew of Capua, Ptolemy of Lucca, Peter of Monte San Giovanni: not canonized.
- Popes John XXII, Innocent IV, Urban IV, Clement IV, Clement VIII, Leo XIII: not canonized.
- Dionysius (author of the Divine Names): left plain. The author is unknown, and the tradition that makes him a saint is another man.
- Brother John of San Giuliano, John Peckham, John Blasi, John of Caiazzo, Dominic of Caserta, Peter of Ireland, Elias of Toulouse, Landulf, Theodora, Frederick II, Charles of Anjou, Louis the Duke of Anjou: not saints.
- Place and church names keep their form: Santa Sabina, Santa Maria at Capua, San Severino, Saint Peter's, the chapels of Saint Nicholas and Saint Stephen.
- Aristotle, Plato, Simplicius, and the Virgin Mary keep their names or titles.

## Choices to know about

- Popes who are Blessed, not saints, now carry "Blessed": Blessed Pope Urban V (1368), Blessed Pope Gregory X, Blessed Pope Benedict XI. The owner's rule says a Blessed person is called "Blessed". Remove the word if you prefer them plain.
- "His name was Thomas" became "The friar was Saint Thomas Aquinas".
- Canonization lines use "him": "Pope John XXII declared him a saint". The heading "A Pope Declares Thomas a Saint" became "A Pope Names Saint Thomas Among the Saints".
- Heading "Thomas Dines With King Louis of France" became "Saint Thomas Dines With Saint Louis, King of France". "King Louis's dinner" in the sources note became "Saint Louis's dinner".
- "Brother Albert" at Cologne became "Saint Albert". "Brother Thomas of Aquino" and "Blessed Thomas" (Urban V's letter, in the narrator's words) became "Saint Thomas".
- "John Chrysostom" and "the apostle John" got the title in the narrator's text. "Chrysostom on Matthew" stays inside the quotation.
- Heading ids are unchanged, because entry.json may refer to them.
- Sentences the title pushed over 30 words were split: two sentences, in the Paris vision and the abbot's dream.
