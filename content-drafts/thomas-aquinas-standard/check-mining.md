# Check of the mined miracle accounts (miracles.html, 123 accounts)

Original kept as `miracles.html.before-check-mining`. Method: every new account (m49–m108) and every merged status line (16 accounts) was read against the raw Latin in the archive.org OCR (Prümmer fasc. 1: Tocco and Gui; Laurent fasc. 4: Naples process 1319 and Fossanova process 1321). Page numbers were recomputed from the printed page heads. The check script gives no MUST FIX and no CHECK line.

## Identifications

- Tocco 23 = Gui 98 (girl-sees-friar): confirmed as probable. Both tell a father who prays that his paralyzed daughter be "taken from this life or healed". Tocco names the father Peter Garellus (OCR; the inventory reads Carellus), an oblate. Gui gives no name. The account text does not claim more, so no text change.
- Tocco 27 = Gui 75 = Fossanova item 124 (m106): confirmed as probable. Same name Marcus, same town, same bloody cough, cured at the tomb. Tocco's version adds the surname Brachialis. The text says only "a like account", so it stays as it is.
- Bull miracle 3 = Peter Francisci (m34): confirmed. Gregory of San Stefano swore (Naples item 24) that Peter was drawn up in hands and feet, twisted in mouth and face, looked dead, and did not feel fire put near his limbs. These are the bull's own signs. m34 said "It is not among the ten in the bull", which contradicted this. I removed that line and wrote that the bull's third miracle is this same healing.
- Bull miracle 6 = Nicholas Grassone (m06): confirmed. Fossanova item 84 matches the bull point by point: seven weeks of daily fever with liver and stomach pain, worse on Friday, vow on Saturday evening at his wife's urging, free on Sunday morning.

## The two place names

- "Carmicensis" (m62, Naples item 95): not a known Latin see name, and the printed footnote gives another manuscript reading ("Teriucensis"). No source identifies it. Kept the plain wording "a bishop of the Dominican order".
- "Mathia" (m04): the Latin does not settle it. Naples item 46 (the abbot's own oath) reads "Thomas de Mathia", both scans. Naples item 68 (Maffeus) reads "Thomasius de Marchia". Gui reads "Matheus de macia" (list no. 13) and "Thomasius de massia" (ch. 49). I kept "Mathia", but wrote "Abbot Thomas of Mathia, a canon of Salerno" so that it does not claim he was abbot of a place called Mathia. The "Maenza" guess in the notes is not in the text and has no support.
- Bonus: "Aequensis" (m64, Landulf) is Vico Equense. Prümmer's own note on Gui p. 228 says so (citing Eubel). Changed "bishop of Aequum" to "bishop of Vico Equense".

## Page and item numbers (about 60 source lines)

The OCR page heads are damaged in many places, and the miner's page numbers drifted by one to three pages, mostly in the Fossanova process (items 6–134) and in Naples items 52–97. All pages were recomputed and corrected. Item numbers were all confirmed (the OCR prints "XL" twice; the second is XLI, Henry Caracchulus). Notable corrections: m34 Naples 22 is pp. 293–295 (it starts at the foot of p. 293); m72 Naples 71 is p. 365; m74 Naples 66 is pp. 355–357; m76 and m77 Naples 84 is pp. 385–386; m78 Naples 89 is pp. 393–394; m60 Naples 91 is pp. 396–397; m16 Naples 92 is pp. 397–399; m45 and m62 Naples 95 is pp. 402–404; m63 Naples 96–97 is pp. 404–406; m09 Fossanova 9 is pp. 420–421 and 34 is p. 442. Gui pages fixed: no. 58 (241–242), 8 (225), 10 (226), 23 (230–231), 30 (233–234), 34 (234–235), 37 (235–236), 41 (236–237), 48 (238–239), 61 (242–243).

## Facts corrected (details the source does not give, or gives differently)

- m55 Peter Grassus: "counselor of the king" is not in the Latin ("miles regis, commissarius et familiaris"). Now "a knight of Naples in the king's service". "Riding" to Rome is not in the source; now "traveling" (also m56).
- m59: the vow was "a wax offering", not "for your tomb". "First of all Richard" was "especially".
- m58: "your devoted servant" was "devotus tuus"; now "devoted to you".
- m61: "a short prayer" is not in the source ("memoriam faceret"). Now: he vowed to remember Saint Thomas in his prayers at Vespers and Matins.
- m08: the illness lasted about twenty days; only on the last night could she take nothing. The text said she took nothing for twenty days.
- m06: the list of pains now matches Fossanova item 84 (spleen and head added, stomach was already in the bull's text).
- m02: Octavian (not Raynaldus alone) is the one who heard the vow words.
- m10: the father's own oath differs from the mother's and the grandmothers'. He says he lifted the boy from the tomb. Both tellings are now given.
- m07: the wife Theodora also swore (item 114). Added.
- m94: the boy's mother Margaret did not swear in the items cited, and only one neighbor (Lady Maria Barrola) did. Fixed.
- m91: Gui tells both events, not only the second.
- m101: the inquisitors "saw that he heard well"; the Latin does not say they tested him. Title changed to "A deaf man hears even low voices". The words "I do not hear you" are James Cafassus's report only.
- m103: "comater Mabilia" is now "Dear Mabilia" (not "my friend").
- m108: "consanguineum" is "kinsman", not "cousin" (text and title).
- m85: "the size of a hen" is not clear in item 21; the brother says "an egg or more". Now "the size of an egg".
- m92: Leonard Palumbus is not called a neighbor. Now "a notary of Sermoneta".
- m97: the boy was nearly eight, not about seven.
- m68: "keep it burning for fifteen days" was a guess; the Latin says he had promised to do this (go with the candle) for fifteen days.
- m67: Stephana "did not dare to enter", as item 63 says.
- m69: "noble lady" is "domina", now "a lady".
- m70: "intercession" is not in Tocco ("meritis"); now "merits".
- m73: the date 16 July is the date of the healing, not of the telling.
- m54: "the friars found" is "it was found"; Gui's wording of Saint Albert's words corrected.
- m34: "His mother had him carried to her house" is "he was carried".
- tomb-opened: Peter "had the body moved" as abbot (Naples 52), not "moved it himself".
- m60: grammar of the status line and Tocco's "third day".
- m50, m51: removed "it is told that" (Gui's "fertur") in favor of plain telling with the teller named once.

## Tone, titles, straight sentences

- 25 paragraphs said "blessed Thomas of Aquino" in the narrator's voice (copied from the witnesses' "beatus"). Changed to "Saint Thomas of Aquino" outside quotation marks. Quotations are untouched.
- m52 "Brother Thomas" (narrator) is now "Saint Thomas". In m62 the friar Thomas of Adversa is now always named in full, so he is not confused with Saint Thomas.
- No new account makes God the actor where the source does not name God.
- Sentences that the check script flagged (three starts in a row, long, bent) were rewritten in m10, m51, m34, m61, m62, m68, and the tomb-opened account.
- Closing note: "Most of the visions and wonders come from the early Lives" is now true only for the visions and the life, so it reads "Most of the healings on this page come from those witnesses. Most of the visions and wonders of Saint Thomas's own life come from the early Lives".

## Not changed, for the owner to know

- Old accounts (m01, m12, m17, m22, m23, m24, m27, wounded-arm and others) still carry the miner's earlier page numbers for Naples items 8, 60, 62, 70, 75, 78 and 57. Some are one page short. They were outside this check.
- m47 and m48 (a note and a saying) now sit before the 54 sworn accounts in "After his death". The order is odd but not wrong.
- Gui nos. 45–47 are missing in the OCR, so nothing from them is claimed.

## Verdict

Ready. The 60 new accounts and the 16 merged status lines now agree with the Latin. Remaining limits: the Carmicensis see and the Mathia town are not identified because the sources do not identify them.
