Copies kept: biography.html.before-check, summary.txt.before-check. Lint: no MUST FIX and no CHECK lines (7,897 words, avg sentence 13.4).
Raw text read for this check: Fontes fasc. 1 (Calo, Tocco, Gui) and fasc. 4 (Naples process) OCR from archive.org; corpusthomisticum.org (ST II-II q.168 a.2, Adoro te, Sacris solemniis, Lauda Sion); Aeterni Patris (vatican.va); Butler vol. 3 (local).

## Writer's questions
- **Candle scene (ch. 8).** Confirmed in Tocco p.121 (and Calo p.44), but the draft had it wrong. It was his scribe, while he dictated on the Trinity, not "a friar who sat with him". The candle burned for an hour to his fingers. Tocco calls it a gift from God. Fixed.
- **Golden Chain.** Notes place it only loosely (Wikipedia: Orvieto; Baring-Gould: Rome). Changed "put together" to "worked on" so 1259-65 is not claimed as exact. No city given.
- **What the Summa covered (ch. 9).** Matches the structure of the Summa (God and creation, soul, law, grace, virtues, Christ's life, sacraments). Stop at III q.90 a.4 confirmed by Laurent's note p.376 n.a. Left as written. "Middle of a question" (hook) was wrong: changed to "middle of the treatise on Penance".
- **1323.** "Fifty years" changed to "Almost fifty years" (intro).
- **Translations 1-7 (plan.md).** All seven keep the Latin's meaning. Psalm 11:2, the oath (Bartholomew p.377), "pro certo scio", Adoro te (checked against pat.html), Os iusti, SCG I.2, and the Paris friars (checked against p.376) are fine. Item 7 now also carries the friars' and Thomas's missing lines from the Latin.
