# Check — Sergius of Radonezh (standard mode)

Originals kept as biography.html.before-check and summary.txt.before-check. summary.txt needed no change. Lint: no MUST FIX and no CHECK lines.

## Quotations (raw text)
Compared with the raw Russian (Dimitry on Wikisource; BLDR vol. 6 Old Russian; azbyka S1 for the short modern quotations). All nine block quotes and the in-text quotations match in meaning, with nothing added.
- Dimitry passages (Daniil bargain, grumbling monk, "If He feeds the birds", carriers, farmer lines, Philotheus letter, Alexis exchange): match. "Abba" as "Father" is fine.
- Old Russian passages (blessing "Богу помагающу ти", letter, Dmitri's vow, Mother of God, last words): match. The conditional "God helping you" is kept.
- Gloss 1, "the hymn before the great entrance": correct. S1 ch.2 puts the second cry "at the very start of the Cherubic Hymn". Changed "during" to "at the start of" to match.
- Gloss 2, "the priest's call just before Communion": correct. S1 has the priest proclaiming "Let us attend. Holy things for the holy!" for the third cry.
- Block quote after the grumbling monks began "If He feeds the birds" with no antecedent. Added one lead-in sentence with Christ's words about the birds, which Dimitry has just before it.
- S1 (modern translation) quotations are all short (under 30 words each). Rights are fine.

## Invented or stretched detail removed
- Boyhood: "went to school with his older brother Stefan" (source: only that all three learned or failed). Now "was sent to school".
- "the other boys laughed at him" (source: "reproached"). Now "blamed him".
- "foals that had strayed" (source: just "to find foals"). Now "to look for some foals".
- Wolves "howled around the hut" (card SC-03 says general terms, not at the hut). Now "howling and roaring, and sometimes a bear came".
- "sang the psalms" (source: "said"); "held up the Cross" (source: the Cross was his weapon). Fixed.
- "a stranger knocked at the hut" (source: monks came and begged to stay). Now "a monk came to the hut and asked to stay".
- Daniil "had the timber ready" (source: "everything necessary"). Fixed.
- "dust rise ... with each bite" (source: dust came from his mouth as he ate). Fixed.
- Night rounds: "stopped and blessed it" (source: he rejoiced and prayed for them). Fixed.
- "nobody ever learned who sent the bread" (absolute). Now "the Life does not say who had sent the bread".
- "the farmer walked ... through the forest" (not in the sources). Cut.
- Mother of God scene: "One night" (the Old Russian says "Once"; night follows only from the later line). Now "Once". "out of his mind" (Old Russian "in ecstasy, fear and trembling") now "seized with fear and trembling and beside himself". "The four of them sang" (source: "all together"). Now "They all sang".
- Death: "asked for Communion" (source: took Communion). "A great sweetness" (source: fragrance). "a grave the brothers prayed beside" (source: one man near the monastery prayed there). All fixed.
- "Sergius knew it before anyone else did" (unsupported). Cut.
- Radonezh "small town": S1 ch.5 says "village" (деревня). Now "village" or just "near Radonezh".
- "the old Metropolitan" and "Alexis was old": age not in the cards. Cut "old".
- "crossed the sea to Constantinople": not in S1. Now "reached Constantinople".
- "into the camp" (runner): source says only that he arrived. Fixed.
- Robe: "wearing something like it on the day the farmer came" and the closing "robe that seven monks had refused" tied the refused cloth to the garden day. Now the farmer "saw him in clothes of that kind"; the closing line says "patched robe".
- "the Horde's armies had ruined Sergius's own family" (S1 ch.5 gives tribute, raids, and Moscow's violence). Softened.
- "Stefan was the elder, so the choice was his": Bartholomew asked as the younger brother; the text does not say the choice was Stefan's. Cut.

## Wrong attributions and order
- "Dimitry of Rostov adds that [the farmer] became a monk": S1 ch.11 has it too. Attribution removed.
- Epiphanius "draws one lesson ... Sergius did everything he could to make the outside worth nothing": second half not in S1. Now "the farmer looked at the outside, and the Lord looks at the heart" (S1 ch.11).
- Isaac the Silent (S1 ch.21, "Исаакий") was merged with the Isaac of ch.26 ("Исаак"), and the two Simons were merged. The notes do not show they are the same people. Separated.
- "Epiphanius calls Nikon young..." and "Epiphanius wrote that his face shone like snow": these are in the BLDR 16th-century compilation, not shown to be Epiphanius's own words. Now "the Life".
- Twelve brothers placed before Sergius became abbot, and his long refusal of the abbacy was missing. S1 ch.7-8: he refused until the brothers said they would leave. Added one plain sentence (it fits the theme) and moved the count of twelve.
- "Early years of the fourteenth century": birth is 1314 or 1322. Now "first decades".

## Absolute claims and counts
- "The longest piece of his teaching that survives is the deathbed one": the Life gives it only as a summary. Rewritten.
- "His fame rests on the blessing": now "Part of his fame rests".
- "No Church document names him patron of students ... grew up on its own": now "No Church decree is known".
- "about fifty-five miracles at the shrine": S1 ch.34-88 holds 55 chapters, not all miracles and not all at the shrine. Now "more than fifty chapters of miracles".
- "Its link to Kulikovo comes from the Tale and Dimitry": now "appears in later works, such as" (the modern editors and Lavra also make the link).
- Relics note: "added by Simon Azarin in 1646" conflicts with the same edition's introduction (Pachomius first told the relics story). Both now stated.
- Jona's 1449-50 letter: "called Sergius a saint" now "named Sergius among the wonderworkers" (ru-wiki: calls him "prepodobny" beside wonderworkers and hierarchs).
- "How This Life Was Researched": counts verified (11 sources: 2 Old Russian, 8 modern Russian, 1 English; 9 notes marked NEW; about 21 chapters read in full).

## Confirmed as written
- Bear: bread on a stump or log, never from his hand.
- Kulikovo: named once, "later accounts"; blessing keeps "God helping you"; doubt (Kuchkin, Vozha 1378) is in A Note on the Sources.
- No "cell" for the blessing or the death. "Cell" appears only where the source has it (hut, cell building, Daniil, Mother of God porch scene).
- Age 78 is the Life's claim, with the editors' doubt in the note.
- Relics: "most accounts give 1422", 1423 in the note.
- Andrei Rublev: "later sources". Siege: credited to Palitsyn. Students: a custom of prayer.
- Plan departures all match: no secrecy after Micah's vision; runner unnamed; no "Makovets".

- Micah as a saint rests on ru-wiki's Synaxis of Radonezh saints list (Михей).
