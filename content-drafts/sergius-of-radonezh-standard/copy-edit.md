## Resolved by the checker (2026-10-07)

1. "each in a monastery": S1 ch.5 says each went to a monastery of his or her own (в свой монастырь). Now "each in a monastery of their own". (Dimitry says both at Khotkovo; S1 followed.)
2. "plead for them": the prayer asks her to be a protector "for us, the unworthy" and to pray to her Son for "this holy place". Now "protect him and his brothers, unworthy as they were, and to pray to her Son for that holy place".
3. "They also painted its chief icon": the Lavra page says a workshop under Saint Andrei Rublev and Daniil painted the icons and frescoes (1425–1427) and that the Trinity icon is Rublev's. Rewritten with named painters.
4. Document words: "Later accounts say" became plain (Dimitry gives the field and the date; doubt stays in the Note on the Sources). "The Life counts his years" dropped from the body (doubt in note 82 and the Note). "Most accounts give the year as 1422" became "On 5 July 1422" (Dimitry p.552; the editors' 1423 in the Note). "The Life tells it as an appearance" told plainly (chapter title).
5. Who reminded whom: Saint Dmitri reminded Saint Sergius (S1 ch.22). The sentence now says so ("Then he reminded the abbot of his vow to build a monastery for the Most Pure Mother of God").
6. "Stefan's friend": S1 ch.5 says Alexis and Stefan lived a common spiritual life and sang side by side. Now "He had sung beside Stefan in the choir."
7. "He would not quarrel…": a motive not in any source. Cut.
8. "like a treasure": in S1 ch.3 ("как некое сокровище"). Kept.
9. "ninth hour": now "the time of the Church's late-day prayers" (S1 note 29). "Dormition": now "named for her falling asleep".
10. "Sergius's spring": kept without "Saint" (the people's name; S1 ch.12 and Dimitry).
11. "no food and nothing to drink": follows S1 ch.5. Kept.
