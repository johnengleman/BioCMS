As a boy, Saint Sergius could not learn to read. He prayed in tears over his letters until a stranger under an oak gave him a piece of holy bread that tasted like honey. As a young man he wanted only to be alone with God, in a forest far from any road. For a while he had it, with wolves coming by his hut and a bear that waited for its bread like a creditor. Then monks came through the trees and begged to stay. What does a man do when God answers his prayer for solitude with a crowd? Saint Sergius of Radonezh spent his life finding out.
