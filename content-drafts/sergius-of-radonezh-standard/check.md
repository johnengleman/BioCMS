## Errors of fact fixed (location, reason)
1. Ch.6 (note 21): Mitrofan was called "to his little church". S1: to his hermitage (пустынька); the tonsure was in the church. Now "to his hermitage in the forest".
2. Ch.7 (note 32), monks' warning: list dropped "discomfort" (S1 неудобства). Restored.
3. Ch.7, block quote "I receive you with joy": S1 sentence continues ("only let each build his own cell"). Ellipsis added.
4. "In those first years the monastery had no stores of anything" (note 38): too strong. S1: it lacked many things; the bread and salt ran out. Now "often lacked the things it needed most".
5. Ch.11 (note 44): "the monastery was given a poor piece of cloth". S1 and Dimitry: it had a poor cloth; no giver. Fixed.
6. Note 42: "feed the carriers before anyone else ate". S1: he ordered the carriers called to the table. Now "invite the carriers to the table".
7. Note 51: envoys "came to the gate". S1: they came to the monastery. Fixed.
8. Ch.17 (note 56), Stefan's words: "founded this place". S1 обитель: "this monastery". Fixed inside the quotation.
9. Ch.17 (note 57): "He would not quarrel with his brother over the abbot's place". A motive the sources do not give (S1: he heard it and said nothing; Dimitry: said nothing). Cut. (Copy-edit item 7.)
10. Ch.22 (note 69): "Later accounts say this march led to the Kulikovo field". Told plainly now: "In September 1380 the Russians reached the Kulikovo field by the river Don" (Dimitry pp. 543–544). The doubt stays in the Note on the Sources.
11. Ch.22 (note 72): "He reminded the abbot of the vow he had made". Dmitri reminded Saint Sergius (S1: напомнил старцу о своем обете). Now says the prince reminded the abbot of his vow to build a monastery for the Mother of God and wished to begin at once.
12. Ch.26 (note 75): "plead for them" had no antecedent. The prayer says "for us, the unworthy" and asks her to look on "this holy place" (BLDR, S1, Dimitry). Now "protect him and his brothers, unworthy as they were, and to pray to her Son for that holy place".
13. Ch.32 (note 82): "the Life counts his years as seventy-eight". The age is doubtful (S1 editors' note 89). Said less: dropped from the body; the note and the Note on the Sources carry it.
14. Ch.33 (note 85): "Most accounts give the year as 1422". Dimitry (p.552) and ru-wiki give 5 July 1422. Body now says "On 5 July 1422"; the 1423 of the editors stays in the Note.
15. Ch.33 (note 86): "his workshop ... They also painted its chief icon" had no clear subject and was a partial misreading. The Lavra page: icons and frescoes by a workshop under Andrei Rublev and Daniil, 1425–1427; the Trinity icon is Rublev's work "in praise of Saint Sergius". Rewritten; "Later sources identify" became "The Church remembers this Andrei as Saint Andrei Rublev" (pravenc: veneration as a saint; the Note on the Sources keeps the doubt).
16. Ch.63 (note 89): Andrei and his men "fled to the monastery". S1: they wished to repent and fled from the Lithuanian regiments; it does not say they reached the monastery. Fixed. "The Life tells it as an appearance" told plainly (chapter title: the appearance of Sergius to Andrei Boldyr, and victory by his prayers).
17. 1919 and 1941 (notes 90–91): "made the monastery a museum" and "far east". Ru-wiki: the opened shrine was given to a museum set up in the closed monastery; it was evacuated "deep into the rear" to Solikamsk. Fixed ("far away").
18. Last paragraph: "It stands where Saint Sergius once lived alone" pointed at the cathedral. Now "in the monastery that grew where…".
19. Boyars "the old nobility": "old" is not in the sources (S1: noble and well-born). Now "the nobles of Rostov".
20. Parents' vows (note 14): "each in a monastery" now "each in a monastery of their own" (S1: в свой монастырь). Dimitry says both entered Khotkovo; S1 is followed.
21. Note on the Sources: "The Life gives no dates except the death" is false (S1 and BLDR give 6885/1378 for Alexis and 6903/1395 for Theodore). Now "only his death has a year; his birth has none".
22. Note on the Sources: "Only the Tale has the holy water and the two warrior monks". Dimitry (p.543) has both. Fixed ("Both have ... whom the old Life does not mention").
23. Note on the Sources: Soviet record "lists the bones of a whole body". Ru-wiki: bones, with the skull and a lock of hair. Now "bones and a skull".
24. Note on the Sources: Maxim the Greek's reason was "the monasteries held land and wealth". Ru-wiki: Saint Sergius and the Moscow hierarchs held towns, villages, wealth. Fixed.
25. How This Life Was Researched: "more than twenty-five" chapters read in full. The notes use about 25. Now "about twenty-five ... read closely".
26. Summary: "wolves around his hut" now "coming by his hut" (Dimitry: wolves ran past his cell).
