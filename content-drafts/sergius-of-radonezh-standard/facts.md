# Facts — Saint Sergius of Radonezh (checked 2026-10-07)

The miracle list and the teachings essay use these exactly. Note numbers refer to `biography.html`.

## Names and spellings
- Saint Sergius of Radonezh, born Bartholomew (note 3, ch.2; note 22). Parents Saint Kirill and Saint Maria (S1 note 4). Brothers Stefan (eldest) and Petr (youngest). Stefan's sons: Kliment and Ivan; Ivan took the name Theodore (S1 ch.5, 9). Saint Theodore, Archbishop of Rostov, founded the Simonov monastery (ch.20).
- Abbot Mitrofan tonsured him (ch.6); Daniil the old monk of the porch (D p.525); Saint Nikon (successor), Saint Roman (Kirzhach), Saint Savva (Dubenka), Saint Athanasius (Vysoky), Saint Micah, Saint Isaac the Silent, Makarii, Simon (angel scene: Isaac and Makarii; Mother of God scene: "Isaac and Simon" are not shown to be the same men).
- Metropolitans: Saint Theognostus, Saint Alexis. Patriarch Saint Philotheus of Constantinople. Metropolitan Saint Jona. Grand Prince Saint Dmitri Ivanovich (Donskoy); his son Prince Yury Dmitrievich; Prince Vladimir of Serpukhov.
- Kinela road, Makhrishche, river Kirzhach, river Dubenka, Golutvin (near Kolomna), Vysoky (Serpukhov).
- Spelling in text: Kirill, Maria, Stefan, Petr, Theodore, Mitrofan, Daniil, Andrei, Nikon, Savva, Epiphanius the Wise, Dimitry of Rostov (the author), Dmitri (the prince), Kulikovo, Mamai, Peresvet, Oslyabya.
- "Sergius's spring" stays without "Saint".

## Dates and numbers
- Tonsure 7 October (feast of Saints Sergius and Bacchus), age 23 (S1 ch.6). Birth 1314 or 1322 (scholars; not in the body).
- Alexis reposed 12 February 1378 (Dimitry p.542). Blessing before the war with Mamai; the field and date as Dimitry gives them: Kulikovo, September 1380 (Dimitry pp.543–544). The Life names no battle.
- Ryazan mission 1385 (S1 note 175; Dimitry p.546). Saint Dmitri reposed 1389 (S1 note 80).
- Saint Sergius reposed 25 September 1392 (S1 ch.32). The age seventy-eight is the Life's claim and doubtful: say less.
- Relics found 5 July 1422 (Dimitry p.552; ru-wiki); the editors give 1423 (S1 note 93). Thirty years after the death.
- Saint Epiphanius wrote the Life 1417–1418 (S1 introduction). Pachomius 1440–1459. Jona's letter 1449 or 1450.
- Trinity stone church painted 1425–1427 (Lavra page). Siege 1608–1610 (S1 note 134: 23 September 1608 – 12 January 1610).
- Shrine opened 11 April 1919 (ru-wiki; body says "April 1919"); Solikamsk 25 July 1941 – 19 November 1944 (body: 1941, 1944); relics returned to the Church 20 April 1946, Holy Saturday (ru-wiki and its note).
- Twelve brothers for two or three years, Saint Sergius the thirteenth (S1 ch.8). Bear: more than a year (ch.7). About two years alone before monks came (ch.7, "or more or less"). Three days without bread (ch.10: "three or four days").

## Approved wordings of quotations (modern English; "our translation")
1. The stranger under the oak (note 9, S1 ch.3): "Your son will be a dwelling of the Holy Trinity, and he will lead many after him to understand the commandments of God."
2. To Mitrofan (note 21, ch.6): "Now I am free of everything, and I thirst to be made a monk. As a deer longs for a spring of water, so my soul thirsts for a monk's life in the wilderness."
3. Wish for solitude (note 23, ch.6): "For a long time I have longed with all my thoughts and desires to live alone in the wilderness, without a single person."
4. To the first monks (note 32–33, ch.7): "I wanted to live alone in this wilderness, brothers, and to die here. But if God has willed it, and if it pleases Him that there be a monastery in this place and many brothers gathered here, let the Lord's will be done! I receive you with joy…"
5. On being abbot (note 34, ch.7): "I have never even thought of being abbot. My soul wants one thing: to die a monk in this place."
6. "If He feeds the birds, can He not give food to us? Now is the time for patience, and we complain. If we bear this short trial with thanks, the trial itself will do us great good. Even gold is not made pure without fire." (note 41, Dimitry p.526)
7. On the spring (note 48, ch.12): "I wanted to live here in silence, alone. But it pleased God to raise up such a monastery in this place, to the glory of His most holy name. Be bold in prayer, and do not lose heart." And (note 50): "Let me never hear you call this spring by my name. I did not give you the water. The Lord gave it to us, unworthy as we are."
8. To Alexis (note 62, Dimitry p.541): "Forgive me, holy master. From my youth I have not worn gold, and in old age I want even more to stay in poverty." And (note 64): "Holy master, if you do not want to drive me out of this land, so that you hear nothing more of me, do not speak of this again. Let no one else speak to me so. No one will find me willing."
9. The blessing (note 67, BLDR / S1 ch.22): "It is right, my lord, that you care for the Christian flock God has given you. Go against the godless. With God helping you, you will win, and you will come back to your own land safe, with great honor." (The oldest text: "God helping you"; BLDR modern translation: "if God helps you".)
10. The letter (note 69): "Without any doubt, my lord, go boldly against their fierceness. Do not be afraid at all. God will surely help you."
11. The Mother of God (note 77, ch.26): "Do not be afraid, my chosen one. I have come to visit you. Your prayer for your disciples and for your monastery has been heard. Do not grieve any more, because from now on it will have plenty of everything. Not only in your life, but after you go to the Lord, I will not leave your monastery. I will give what it needs without lack, and I will keep it and cover it."
12. Last words (note 81, BLDR): "God is calling me, and I am going away from you. I give you to the Almighty Lord and to his Most Pure Mother. Let her be your refuge and a wall against the snares of the enemy and his barking."
13. Stefan (note 56, ch.17): "Who is abbot here? Was it not I who first founded this monastery?"
14. To the Metropolitan (note 60, ch.17): "Tell my lord the Metropolitan: everything that comes from your mouth I will receive with joy, as if it came from the mouth of Christ. I will not disobey you in anything."
15. "Firm as a diamond" (note 66, S1 ch.25).

## Disputes (stay out of the body)
Birth 1314 or 1322; age 78 doubted; relics 1422 or 1423; blessing at Kulikovo (1380) or Vozha (1378, Kuchkin); Peresvet and Oslyabya only from the Tale and Dimitry; Andrei = Rublev (Pachomius's Life of Nikon, via pravenc); the hidden head (tradition) against the 1919 record; Maxim the Greek's doubt.
