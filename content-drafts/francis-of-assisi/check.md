# Check: St. Francis of Assisi

Checker: not the writer. Originals kept as `biography.html.before-check`, `entry.json.before-check`, `summary.txt.before-check`.
Method: quotations and dates checked against raw archive.org text (Howell's Celano, Three Companions, Evans and 1902 Mirror, Robinson 1906, Quaracchi 1904, Boehmer 1904, Wadding 1732, Jordan, Bonaventure 1868 and Everyman, Robinson's Clare, Jacques 1597), the Spanish Process of Clare, the Mira circa nos text, the Vatican decree, Fratelli tutti, the 2013 address, Key to Umbria, the Catholic Encyclopedia and the OFM Conv. news pages. I viewed the page scan of Jacques de Vitry fol. 353: the Latin matches.

## Fixes in biography.html

1. Ch. 3 (Rome, St. Peter's). Reversed claim. Celano (2C §8) says he would have eaten with the poor at home "had he not been ashamed" before those who knew him. Text said he did it anyway. Fixed.
2. Ch. 1. "whom all the rest hated" and "made a friend of him" go beyond 2C §4 and 3C §4 (the others shunned him; Francis stayed by him). Reworded.
3. Ch. 2. "king of their revels" is not in the sources (2C: "chief"; 3C: "master of the revels"). Now "master".
4. Ch. 5. After Gubbio, Celano has him serve the lepers before San Damiano (1C §17). Added.
5. Ch. 7. "the only reason they survive" (the will for Clare) was an absolute claim. Now "that is how they have come down to us". Removed the invented "small square" detail. Removed "he could hardly believe it" (Jacques); now "wrote down what he had seen".
6. Ch. 8. "Twice he tried" was wrong. Celano (1C §55-56) has a Syria attempt (driven off by winds), then Morocco/Spain, then 1219. Fixed.
7. Ch. 9. Jacques is called "the eyewitness" of the Sultan meeting. He was in the camp but did not see the meeting. Now "the bishop in the camp". Added his 1220 letter's "little result". Celano's "unparalleled favour" is not said to be the stigmata; now stated as Celano's words with the stigmata reading attributed to readers.
8. Ch. 11. "true minister general" corrected (2C §193: "Minister-General"). Letter to the Order: the words "not my brothers" apply to those who refuse the Office as the Rule requires, or wander outside it (Quaracchi Ep. II p. 106); narrowed. Letter to a Minister: "whatever came from the brothers" narrowed to what the Latin says. Bull original "still kept" now "long kept" (Robinson 1906).
9. Ch. 12. Pacifico "trick": Celano says "pious fraud". Reworded. Chartula "carried in procession once a year" and "still kept" came from Robinson 1906; now only the 1348 archive mention. Foligno priest: Celano says "clothed in white, of very great age", not "white-haired". Fixed, and dated only "around that time".
10. Ch. 14. Testament date is Robinson's inference: "Probably that summer". The city "hoped to keep his body, Celano says" was Howell's note; replaced by Celano's own words (2C §77, "give their glory to another"). Death verse of the Canticle is marked as traditionally added; Canticle date marked "about 1225".
11. Ch. 15 (death). "His last words were the psalm's own: thou art my hope…" came from Baring-Gould only. Bonaventure has him go on to "The righteous wait for me until you reward me." Fixed, with Bonaventure's order (Gospel, then psalm).
12. Ch. 17 (canonization). The cardinals' verdict was given at Perugia (1C §123-124), not Assisi. Fixed. 1230: "the bull says the rulers took the body when it reached the church" mixed Wadding's words with the bull's. Split. 1818: "his bones" is not in Key to Umbria (it says essentially intact); now "remains". 1934: Jacoba's casket was set at the stairs to the crypt, not inside it; the four brothers went into niches. Fixed.
13. Ch. 18. 2026 exposition: "nearly 400,000 booked" was an early figure; the Franciscans report more than 350,000 pilgrims. Fixed.
14. Source note. Catholic Encyclopedia does not give 16 June for Speravimus; Key to Umbria does. Fixed. The CE article on Elias puts the translation a few days before 25 May (25 May as consecration); added. Bull header: the English edition's heading gives 16 July, not the bull; the dating clause "fourteenth Kalends of August" at Perugia quoted. "Only its editor of 1871" and "only eyewitness" softened. Celano "second-hand" softened to "probably". Surviving copy of Elias's letter is addressed to France; added. Robinson named as source for the "two autographs" and the Testament date.
15. Research paragraph. "fifteen details" replaced by "more than a dozen" (notes show 17 NEW marks). Source count 32 to 33 (OFM page replaced by two verified pages). Replaced the bare ofmconv.net link with the two news pages.
16. Mechanics. Removed a run of three sentences starting "The" (ch. 6) and the word "journey" (AI-tell). Trimmed about 110 words to keep the lint count under 10,000 (now 9,972). Lint: no MUST FIX; 31 CHECK lines, all long sentences.

Verified, no change needed: the Perugia boast; Spoleto dialogue; the leper, the cave, Testament quotes; the bishop scene; Clare Palm Sunday (Guido named by Robinson note 98; Bona in Rome for Lent in the Process; Clare's age about 18; fasting and half a roll); Jacques's Latin "Ego sum Christianus, ducite me ad Dominum vestrum" and "Ora pro me…"; no fire or baptism in Jacques; Jordan (vicars, "Redite", Pope's door, 3,000, tunic tug, 90 volunteers, San Giorgio and Perugia); the Mirror's 5,000; Thomas of Spalato; Damietta (6,000 is Celano's text); Elias's letter (Boehmer and Wadding: no seraph, mountain or date; "Quarto nonas Octobris… prima hora noctis praecedentis"; body soft); the Greccio, La Verna, cautery, lute, "Mind your own business"; Mira circa nos (no stigmata or wound word; "many brilliant miracles"; patron "whose friendship We enjoyed"; 4 October); Wadding's 25 May and Speravimus date (xvi kal.
